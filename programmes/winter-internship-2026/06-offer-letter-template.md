### Other terms
1. **Confidentiality.** You may see internal (non-client) information about how the team works. Keep it confidential during and after the internship. You will not be given access to client information; if you see any by accident, tell your supervisor and don't record or share it.
2. **Your work.** You own the capstone you create. You give us a licence to use it internally for training. We will only publish it, in whole or part, if you agree in writing at the time, it passes compliance review, and you are credited as you choose.
3. **Social media.** Please don't post about the financial content of the internship or name clients, colleagues or the principal insurer without written approval. You may say you completed the internship.
4. **No obligations after the internship.** You are under no obligation to apply for any role, sit any exam, attend any event or introduce anyone to us. Whether you take part in anything after the internship has no effect on your allowance, certificate or reference.
5. **Personal data.** We handle your data as set out in the PDPA notice you received ([Annex B]).
6. **Welfare.** Your welfare contact, who is not your supervisor, is [name, phone]. You can raise any concern with them or with your school's career office.
7. **Right to work.** This offer depends on your being entitled to work in Singapore for the whole period. [For Student's Pass holders: you confirm the dates fall in your institution's official vacation.]
8. **Governing law.** Singapore law.

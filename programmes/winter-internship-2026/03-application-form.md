## Questions we deliberately do NOT ask (and why)

| Not asked | Why |
|---|---|
| Age, date of birth, NRIC/FIN | TAFEP: remove age fields; NRIC reveals age. PDPC NRIC guidelines. Age is asked **after** acceptance only, as one yes/no for guardian consent. |
| Gender, race, religion, nationality, marital status, children, pregnancy | TAFEP: not related to the job. Work eligibility is asked as a yes/no instead of nationality. |
| Photo | TAFEP: remove photograph fields. |
| Disability, health or mental-health declarations | TAFEP. Applicants may ask for adjustments instead. |
| NS liability | TAFEP. |
| Grades / GPA | Not needed for a learning internship; disadvantages people without strong grades. |
| Family members' jobs or connections in finance | Not relevant; creates a networking bias. |
| "Do you know people who'd like financial advice?" / contacts of friends and family | Not allowed — the internship is not a lead-generation activity. |
| Income goals, "how much do you want to earn?" | Not relevant; the programme makes no income claims. |

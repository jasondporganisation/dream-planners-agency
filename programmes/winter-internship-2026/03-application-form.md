# Application Form — Winter Internship 2026

> Copy these questions into your form tool. **Use a form tool whose data is stored securely and access-limited to the selection panel** [JASON: name the tool and who has access].
> **Use only after the principal insurer confirms the agency may run its own application form and ads (README section 8).**
> Built to TAFEP's guidance: ask only what relates to "the qualifications, skills, knowledge, and experience required for the job", and "remove fields on age, gender, race, religion, marital status and family responsibilities, or disability", photographs, National Service liability and mental-health declarations; don't ask for NRIC, as "NRIC numbers can reveal the age of a person". Where information that "may be viewed as discriminatory" is genuinely needed, TAFEP says to state the reason clearly. Section B does that for the principal's eligibility conditions (21+, SC/PR, NS completed if liable), which are conditions of the programme, not selection preferences ([TAFEP, Preparing job application forms](https://www.tal.sg/tafep/employment-practices/recruitment/preparing-job-application-forms); [Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices](https://www.tal.sg/tafep/getting-started/fair/tripartite-guidelines)).
> PDPC guidelines generally bar collecting NRIC numbers unless a law requires it or identity must be verified to a high degree of accuracy ([PDPC, Advisory Guidelines on NRIC and other national identification numbers](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/files/pdpc/pdf-files/advisory-guidelines/advisory-guidelines-for-nric-numbers---310818.pdf)).

## Questions we deliberately do NOT ask (and why)

| Not asked | Why |
|---|---|
| Date of birth, exact age, NRIC/FIN | TAFEP: remove age fields; NRIC reveals age. PDPC NRIC guidelines. Only a yes/no "21 or older on the cut-off date" is asked, with the reason stated, because it is a programme condition; it is checked by sighting ID at shortlist stage, without recording the number. If the principal's own registration form asks for NRIC, that is the principal's form and notice. |
| Gender, race, religion, nationality, marital status, children, pregnancy | TAFEP: not related to the job. SC/PR status is asked as a yes/no (programme condition) instead of nationality. |
| Photo | TAFEP: remove photograph fields. |
| Disability, health or mental-health declarations | TAFEP. Applicants may ask for adjustments instead. |
| NS liability or enlistment dates | TAFEP. Only "completed NS, or not liable" is asked, as a programme condition, worded so that it does not ask gender. |
| Grades / GPA | Not needed for a learning internship; disadvantages people without strong grades. |
| Family members' jobs or connections in finance | Not relevant; creates a networking bias. |
| "Do you know people who'd like financial advice?" / contacts of friends and family | Not allowed — the internship is not a lead-generation activity. |
| Income goals, "how much do you want to earn?" | Not relevant; the programme makes no income claims. |

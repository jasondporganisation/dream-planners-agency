# Week-by-Week Plan

> **Default: the principal's 5-day programme, Mon 7 – Fri 11 Dec 2026** (next section). The 3-week plan further down is the **optional extended version**: run it only with the principal's written OK and a separate allowance decision by Jason. 2-week and 4-week variants are at the end.
> Every day follows the rules in the README, section 4: internal meetings only, no clients, no advice, no products.

## Default 5-day plan (principal's programme)

The principal sets the content of its own days. The agency's job is to supervise, buddy and keep its own days non-advising. Under **Option 1** the principal runs all 5 days and the agency provides buddies and the Day 3 (Agency Day) content. Under **Option 2** the agency also runs Days 2–4 itself and submits attendance.

| Day | Principal's schedule (per 1 Oct briefing) | Agency's part |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon 7 Dec** | Day 1 (compulsory): launch, orientation, team bonding, money-skills session | Buddies attend. Walk through the "may / may not" table; each intern signs that they've read it. PDPA and confidentiality briefing. |
| **Tue 8 Dec** | Day 2: profiling tool | Option 2: **Workshop 1** (budgeting) and **Workshop 5** (scams). Learning log. |
| **Wed 9 Dec** | Day 3: **Agency Day** (marketing plan preparation) | Internal shadowing only (team huddle, training) and an **anonymised client-needs case study** built on a fictional persona: interns describe needs in general terms; nobody quotes, compares or recommends a product. Talk: "What an adviser actually does" (licensing, 21+ rule, supervision). Any "marketing plan" work is general education content, checked with the Workshop 6 checklist; no products, prices or income claims. |
| **Thu 10 Dec** | Day 4: plan submission by 12pm, then sports activity | Option 2: **Workshop 6** (talking about money fairly) as the self-check before submission. Buddy review. |
| **Fri 11 Dec** | Day 5 (compulsory): Top 5 presentations, graduation, conferment night | Buddies attend. Feedback survey (anonymous). Written handout about what happens next (see `09-career-talk-path.md`); nobody is asked to sign up in the room. |

Notes:
- Workshops 2–4 (debt and credit, CPF, insurance concepts) can be given as optional reading or used in the extended version.
- Anything interns produce stays internal unless it passes the principal's ad-approval process and the intern agrees in writing.
- Exam talk: interns may hear in general terms what the licensing exams are. Nobody is asked to register for exams during the programme; any exam-reimbursement scheme is explained in writing after Day 5, to those who ask.

# Optional extended version (3 weeks)

> **3 weeks, Mon 7 Dec – Thu 24 Dec 2026** (14 working days). Needs the principal's written OK, full written KETs (18 calendar days) and Jason's allowance decision.

## Daily rhythm

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 9.30–9.45 | Check-in with buddy: plan for the day, any questions |
| 9.45–12.30 | Morning block: workshop, shadowing or capstone work |
| 12.30–1.30 | Lunch |
| 1.30–5.00 | Afternoon block |
| 5.00–5.30 | Learning log (3 lines: what I learned, what surprised me, one question) |

Shadowing means **internal** sessions only: team huddles, training, case discussions where client names and details have been removed, compliance briefings, and planning meetings. Before each one, the meeting lead checks the agenda and removes client items or asks interns to step out for them.

## Learning objectives
By the end, each intern can:
1. Explain in plain English six core personal-finance topics (Workshops 1–6).
2. Describe what a financial adviser does, how advisers are licensed and supervised, and why interns may not advise.
3. Check a piece of content against basic fair-communication rules (accurate, balanced, sourced, no promises).
4. Plan, write and present a short financial-literacy education campaign for young adults.

## Capstone: "Money Basics for Your First Paycheque" education campaign

Teams of 2 (or individually if the cohort is small) make a short **education** campaign aimed at students and first-jobbers. Example formats: 3–5 social carousels, a one-page guide, or a 5-minute talk script.

**Rules**
- Topic chosen from the 6 workshop topics. General education only.
- Every fact must come from an official source (e.g. MoneySense, CPF Board, MAS, IRAS) with a link and the date it was checked. Figures already checked in [`compliance/sg-fact-pack-2026-11.md`](../../compliance/sg-fact-pack-2026-11.md) may be used as written there.
- **No** products, insurer or firm names, premiums, returns, "guaranteed", "best", income or earnings claims, calls to "speak to an adviser", or contact details.
- **Compliance review**: Jason checks against the fact pack in week 3; anything that might be published is then sent through the principal insurer's ad-approval process. **Nothing is published during the internship.** Interns keep their work as a portfolio piece; if the team later wants to publish it, the intern is credited only with their written consent.

**Marking (feedback only, not graded for school):** accurate and sourced (30%), clear for the audience (25%), balanced and compliant (25%), presentation (20%).

## 2-week version (e.g. Mon 7 – Fri 18 Dec)
- Week 1: as above, but run Workshops 1, 2 and 3 (move Workshop 3 to Thu 10 Dec; combine the "What an adviser does" talk into Workshop 6).
- Week 2: Workshops 4, 5 and 6 on Mon, Tue and Wed. Capstone shrinks to **one** carousel or one-page guide. Compliance review Thu; showcase and survey Fri.
- Note: a 2-week contract (12 calendar days) may fall under MOM's 14-day KET threshold, but **give written terms anyway**; IHLs expect them.

## 4-week version (e.g. Mon 7 Dec – Thu 31 Dec, or into January after checking school term dates)
- Weeks 1–3 as above, with the showcase moved to week 4.
- Week 4 adds: a second capstone format (e.g. turning the carousel into a short talk script); a session on budgeting tools and spreadsheets; a "day in the life" of non-adviser roles (operations, compliance, training, marketing); and one extra 1:1.
- Check that Student's Pass holders' vacation still covers week 4; NUS/NTU term usually restarts in the second week of January.

## Supervisor notes
- Keep interns busy with learning, not with admin. If there's no internal meeting worth shadowing, give capstone time instead.
- Never ask interns to "observe" a client meeting, even quietly or online.
- Keep the learning logs. They form the basis of the written reference.

# Sources

Official sources used in this pack, and what each was used for. The principal's internal programme papers are listed separately below and are not public. Checked 5 Nov 2026 unless stated. Re-check before each run: rules and figures change, often on 1 January.

## Ministry of Manpower (MOM)
| Source | Used for |
|---|---|
| [Work pass exemption for foreign students](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-pass-exemption-for-foreign-students) | Student's Pass holders may work during vacation without a work pass if they are full-time students at a listed institution, hold an ICA-issued Student's Pass and are aged 14+; term-time work is capped at 16 hours a week unless it's a school internship |
| [Young persons and children](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/young-persons-and-children) | Age bands "children" 13 to under 15 and "young persons" 15 to under 16 (not expected in this programme) |
| [MOM parliamentary reply on KETs (2020)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/parliament-questions-and-replies/2020/0302-written-answer-by-mrs-josephine-teo-to-pq-on-number-of-wphs-not-issued-with-kets) | Written key employment terms within 14 days of the start of employment; KET items in the offer letter |
| [MOM WorkRight guide to employment laws (PDF)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/employment-practices/workright/workright-guide-employment-laws.pdf) | Written KETs apply to employees "employed for a continuous period of 14 days or more" (so not strictly required for the 5-day programme; written terms are given anyway) |

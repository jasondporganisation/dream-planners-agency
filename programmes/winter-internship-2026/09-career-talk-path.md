# Optional Career Talk for Interns Aged 21+

> **No pressure. No income claims.** The internship is complete in itself. This page describes the **only** route from the internship to any conversation about advisory careers.
> Consistent with [`research/recruitment-2027.md`](../../research/recruitment-2027.md) recommendations 3 (no income figures), 4 (honest cost table), 6 (RES5 study track for 21+) and 8 (someone without recruitment targets in the loop). LIA's Good Recruitment Practices say hiring assessments should involve someone who "should not have sales and recruitment targets" ([LIA, 6 May 2024](https://www.lia.org.sg/media/4208/good-recruitment-practices-in-the-hiring-of-financial-advisory-representatives-6-may-2024.pdf)). MAS expects "structured recruitment policies and processes, approved by senior management" ([MAS thematic review, Dec 2025](https://www.mas.gov.sg/publications/monographs-or-information-paper/2025/thematic-review-of-financial-institutions-recruitment-and-onboarding-training-of-representatives)).

## Who it's for
- Interns who **are 21 or older** (in the default programme all are, because 21+ is a programme condition checked at selection) **and** who ticked the optional box in the intern PDPA notice. MAS Notice FAA-N26 requires representatives to "be at least 21 years old" ([MAS FAA-N26](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/notices/cmg/notice-faa-n26/faa-n26-notice-on-competency-requirements-for-representatives-of-financial-advisers-1.pdf)).
- Extended stand-alone version only: interns under 21 get no invitation and no follow-up. The written handout (below) tells them they are welcome to contact us themselves after their 21st birthday, if they want to. Use the age-check record made at selection; never infer age.

## The rules
1. **Nothing during the internship.** No talk, no sign-up sheet, no "who's interested?" in the room. The only thing during the internship is the written handout given to **every** intern on the last day (Fri 11 Dec; Wed 23 Dec in the extended version).
2. **One message only**, sent after the internship ends (not before **Mon 4 Jan 2027**), only to interns who ticked the box. If they don't reply, there is no follow-up.
3. **No link to the internship.** Allowance, certificate and reference are already done. Going or not going changes nothing.
4. **No income figures, ranges, "up to" amounts, or lifestyle images.** Not in the talk, slides, handout or follow-up messages. Explain how pay is structured (allowance schemes if any, commission, the risk of variable income), not how much.
5. **Honest costs and requirements first.** See the cost table below.
6. **A second person without recruitment targets** sits in the talk (e.g. Heron or a non-recruiting team member) and is the contact for any concerns.
7. **No contacts asked for.** Never ask attendees for names of friends or family.
8. **Cooling-off.** No one is offered or signs anything at the talk. If someone wants to go further, the next step is a separate 1:1 at least **one week** later, booked by them.
9. **The talk is free** and held in office hours or online.
10. **Approved first.** The handout, the January message and the talk slides go through the principal insurer's ad-approval process before use (research recommendation 3).

## Written handout (given to all interns on the last day)

> **After the internship — what happens next?**
> Nothing, unless you want it to. Thank you for taking part.
>
> - **References:** ask your supervisor any time in the next 12 months.
> - **Your capstone:** it's yours. Add it to your portfolio.
> - **Thinking about a career as a financial adviser representative?** It's one option among many — in finance, many more roles don't involve advising (operations, compliance, training, marketing, analytics).
>   - In Singapore you must be **at least 21**, hold the required education (for example GCE 'A' Levels with the required passes, a polytechnic diploma or a degree), pass the required exams, and be assessed as fit and proper before you can give advice (MAS Notice FAA-N26).
>   - If you are 21+ and ticked the optional box, we'll send you **one** message in January about a free, no-obligation career talk. If you didn't tick it, you won't hear from us about this.
>   - If you're under 21 and curious later, you're welcome to contact us after your 21st birthday. We won't contact you.
> - **Want independent information?** MoneySense explains how financial advice works for consumers: [moneysense.gov.sg](https://www.moneysense.gov.sg/financial-advisory-process/).

## Career talk outline (60 min)

| Min | Section | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Welcome | Who's in the room (including the non-recruiting colleague); "nothing is signed today"; questions any time |
| 5–15 | What the job is | A day and week in the role: needs analysis, admin, compliance, learning; who it suits and who it doesn't |
| 15–25 | How you get there | Requirements: 21+, education, exams (RES5, M9, M9A, HI for a typical life/health representative), fit-and-proper checks, appointment, at least 30 hours of structured CPD a year ([MAS FAQ on FAA-N26](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/faqs/cmi/faqs-on-competency-requirements-for-representatives-of-financial-advisers/faa-exam-notice-faqs.pdf)) |
| 25–35 | **What it costs you** | The cost table below; realistic time to study; resit risk |
| 35–45 | How pay works — without figures | Commission-based income varies and can be low or irregular at first; any allowance usually comes with conditions; repayment clauses can apply if you leave early. Ask for the written terms and read them before deciding. |
| 45–55 | Questions to ask any firm | Supervision and mentoring; training; the written terms; what happens if it doesn't work out; who to talk to who isn't recruiting you |
| 55–60 | Close | "Take at least a week. If you want a 1:1, book it yourself. If not, thank you — no follow-up." |

### Never say (talk, slides, chat, socials)
- Any income figure, range, average, "top earners make…", "six figures", "up to $X", "unlimited income".
- "Be your own boss", "financial freedom", "passive income", "work when you want".
- "Everyone can do it", "it's easy", "you'll pass the exams easily".
- "Spots are limited", "decide today", "this offer ends".
- Anything about bringing friends or family as clients.

## Measuring it (for Heron)
Count only: how many ticked the box, how many came, and the anonymous feedback. **Do not set targets** for career-talk attendance or sign-ups from the internship. The internship is judged on learning and the feedback survey.

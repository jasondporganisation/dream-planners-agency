## Session 5.1 — Rehearsal (60 min)

### Slide 1 — Today's plan (3 min)
- Rehearse your plan presentation with your buddy
- The principal runs the presentations and picks its Top 5
- Agency feedback uses our own rubric: it is **feedback only**, not linked to your allowance, certificate or reference

**Speaker notes:** Say clearly: "Whether or not you're in the Top 5 changes nothing about your allowance or reference." The completion bonus depends on attendance and presenting, not on results (pack README section 6).

### Slide 2 — What a strong presentation does (5 min)
- One audience, one thing they should learn
- Every fact sourced and dated on the slide
- Says "it depends" where true
- Ends with "learn more at [official site]", not "contact us"

### Slide 3 — Activity: "Rehearse and rate" (45 min)
- Each team presents for **5 minutes** to its buddy and one other team
- Listeners score with the agency rubric (`presentation-brief-and-rubric.md`) and give 1 "keep" and 1 "change"
- 3 minutes of questions in the style of the judges (below)
- Teams make final fixes

**Speaker notes:** Practice judge questions, with what a good answer contains:

| Question | A good answer… |
|---|---|
| "Where does this figure come from?" | names the official site and the date checked |
| "Who is your audience, and why would they care?" | names one audience (e.g. first-jobbers) and a real moment (first payslip) |
| "What could go wrong if someone followed your advice?" | corrects the framing: "It's general education, not advice; we point people to official sources" |
| "Which product would you recommend?" | "None. This is education content; recommending products needs a licence." |
| "How would you know it helped?" | proposes a simple check (e.g. a 3-question quiz), not leads or sign-ups |

### Slide 4 — Final check (7 min)
- Run the Workshop 6 checklist once more
- Remove anything added since the 12pm submission that breaks it
- Breathe. You've done the work

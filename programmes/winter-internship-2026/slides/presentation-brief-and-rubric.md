# Day 5 Presentation Brief and Judging Rubric

> For the plan presentations on **Fri 11 Dec 2026**. The **principal insurer** runs the Day 5 presentations and chooses its own Top 5 with its own criteria; this rubric does **not** replace them. The agency uses it for **rehearsal feedback and the written reference only**. It is never linked to the allowance or completion bonus, which depend on attendance and presenting (pack README section 6), or to any later career decision.
> Weights follow the capstone marking in `01-week-by-week-plan.md`: accurate and sourced 30%, clear for the audience 25%, balanced and compliant 25%, presentation 20%.

## Brief for interns

**Task:** present the education plan you submitted on Thu 10 Dec at 12pm.

**What it is:** a general financial-literacy education campaign for students or first-jobbers, on one topic from the week (budgeting, scams, CPF basics, emergency funds, or talking about money fairly).

**Length:** **[JASON: the principal's time limit]**. Rehearsals with buddies use 5 minutes plus 3 minutes of questions.

**Must include:**
1. Your audience, and the one thing they should learn
2. Up to 3 key messages, each with an official source link and a "checked on" date on the slide
3. Your format (e.g. 3–5 carousels, a one-page guide, a talk script) and where it *could* appear if it were ever approved
4. How you'd know it helped (e.g. a short quiz for readers)
5. Your compliance self-check: one thing you changed after the Workshop 6 checklist

**Must not include:**
- Any product, insurer or firm name, premium, return or price
- "Guaranteed", "best", "risk-free", "you will", or any income or earnings claim
- "Contact an adviser", phone numbers, sign-up links, or any plan to collect leads or contacts
- Real people's personal data, photos or stories without consent
- Figures that are not on an official site or in the fact pack, or figures with no date

**Remember:** nothing is published. Your plan is your portfolio piece. If the team ever wants to publish it, that happens only through the principal's ad-approval process and with your written consent.

## Judging rubric (agency feedback)

Score each criterion 1–4, then apply the weight. Score is out of 100.

| Criterion (weight) | 4 — Strong | 3 — Good | 2 — Developing | 1 — Not yet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Accurate and sourced (30%)** | Every fact correct, linked to an official site and dated; figures match the fact pack | All facts correct; one missing date or link | One factual error or several missing sources | Several errors, or unsourced figures |
| **Clear for the audience (25%)** | One audience and one clear lesson; plain English; an 18-year-old would get it on a phone | Clear, with some jargon left undefined | Audience vague or too many messages | Hard to follow; no clear audience |
| **Balanced and compliant (25%)** | Passes every Workshop 6 checklist item; says "it depends" where true | One minor wording issue (e.g. a strong word like "must") | A borderline claim or missing caveat | Breaks a house rule (product, "guaranteed", income claim, call to action) |
| **Presentation (20%)** | Confident, on time, answers questions honestly ("I don't know, but here's where I'd check") | On time, mostly confident | Over time or reads from slides | Not ready |

**Score = (Accurate × 7.5) + (Clear × 6.25) + (Balanced × 6.25) + (Presentation × 5)**, giving 25 to 100.

**Automatic stop:** any product or insurer name, "guaranteed" claim, income claim or call to contact an adviser means the plan is not presented in that form. The buddy helps the team fix it first. Record it as feedback, not as a penalty.

## Worked example (fictional)

Team "First Payslip": a carousel for first-jobbers explaining gross pay, employee CPF and take-home pay.
- Accurate **4**: uses "20% employee, 17% employer, 37% total for ages 55 and below on wages above $750, 2026 (CPF Board, checked 9 Dec 2026)"
- Clear **3**: "Ordinary Wages" isn't defined
- Balanced **4**: no products, ends "Learn more at cpf.gov.sg"
- Presentation **3**: half a minute over time
- Score: 30 + 18.75 + 25 + 15 = **88.75**

## Session 1.1 — What interns may and may not do (35 min)

### Slide 1 — Welcome from the Dream Planners team (3 min)
- Who we are: a team of appointed representatives working with the principal insurer
- Your buddy for the week (names on screen) and your welfare contact **[JASON: name and phone]**
- This week is about **learning**, not selling: no one will ask you to join, buy or sign anything
- Questions any time; "I'm not sure" is always a good answer

**Speaker notes:** Introduce each buddy by name. Say plainly: "Your allowance and certificate don't depend on anything you decide about careers." Don't mention pay structures, income or recruitment. Point out the welfare contact is **not** a supervisor.

### Slide 2 — Why there are rules (4 min)
- Only licensed, appointed representatives may give financial advice in Singapore
- MAS has banned people who advised without being appointed
- You are not appointed representatives, so **you don't advise** — even friends and family
- The rules protect the public, the team and you

**Speaker notes:** Source: pack README section 4 (MAS enforcement, 2023). Keep it calm, not scary: "This isn't about trust; it's the law for everyone." No figures needed.

### Slide 3 — What you MAY do (4 min)
- Attend internal meetings, trainings and huddles (client details removed)
- Do the workshops and activities; research public sites (MoneySense, CPF Board, MAS)
- Draft general education content for your plan; ask advisers about their work and rules
- Present your work internally

**Speaker notes:** Read from the printed table. Emphasise "internal" and "general".

### Slide 4 — What you MAY NOT do (5 min)
- No client meetings, calls or events — in person or online, even "just observing"
- No advice or recommendations; no naming, comparing or explaining any insurer's products or premiums
- No handling client or prospect data; no leads, contacts, surveys of the public or business cards
- Nothing published (including your own socials) about the week's financial content without written approval
- Not asked to sit exams, sign contracts or "commit" during the programme

**Speaker notes:** Go through every row of the table's right-hand column. Stress the socials rule: interns often post "first day at…" stories. A photo of the venue is fine only if the principal allows it; nothing about money topics.

### Slide 5 — The one rule to remember (2 min)
- **If unsure: stop and ask your buddy**
- If it might look like advice or selling, an intern doesn't do it
- Buddies follow the same rule

**Speaker notes:** Ask everyone to say the rule back once. Then move to the quiz.

### Slide 6 — Activity: "Allowed or not?" quiz (12 min)
- 8 scenario cards, teams of 2–3
- Decide: **Allowed**, **Not allowed**, or **Ask first**
- One sentence why

**Speaker notes:** Read each card aloud after teams vote. Answers:

| # | Scenario | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your aunt asks which hospital plan she should switch to. | **Not allowed** (to advise) | Say: "I'm not licensed to advise; a licensed adviser can look at your situation." You may point her to MoneySense. |
| 2 | Your buddy invites you to sit quietly in a client video call "just to see". | **Not allowed** | No client meetings in any form. Say no and tell the programme lead. |
| 3 | You attend the team's weekly huddle with client names removed. | **Allowed** | Internal meeting, client items removed. |
| 4 | You post a carousel on your own Instagram about "3 budgeting tips I learned". | **Ask first** → not without written approval | Financial content needs written approval before publishing. |
| 5 | You look up CPF contribution rates on cpf.gov.sg for your plan. | **Allowed** | Public official source. Note the date checked. |
| 6 | A friend asks for the team's contact number "for my dad". | **Not allowed** (to hand out) | No leads or contacts. Your friend can find licensed representatives on the MAS Register themselves. |
| 7 | Someone asks you to sign up for an exam slot during the week. | **Not allowed** | Nobody is asked to register for exams during the programme. Tell the welfare contact. |
| 8 | You help tidy a shared folder that has client files in it. | **Not allowed** | No access to client data at all; interns have no system logins. |

### Slide 7 — Sign-off (5 min)
- Read the printed table one more time
- Sign: "I have read and understood the may / may not table"
- Keep your copy for the week

**Speaker notes:** Collect sheets. Record only name, date and signature; no ID numbers. Store with the attendance record.

## Session 1.2 — Privacy and confidentiality (25 min)

### Slide 1.2.1 — What personal data is (5 min)
- Any information that identifies a person: name, phone, NRIC, photo, address, health, money details
- In this team, that's mostly **client** data — and you won't see any
- Your own data: collected only for the programme, used as the PDPA notice says

**Speaker notes:** Refer to the PDPA notice interns already received (`07-pdpa-consent.md`). Don't re-collect anything.

### Slide 1.2.2 — If you see something you shouldn't (5 min)
- Don't read it, copy it, photograph it or share it
- Close it or step away
- Tell your buddy or the programme lead the same day; no blame

**Speaker notes:** Example: a meeting slide that still shows a client's name. The fix is the team's job, not the intern's.

### Slide 1.2.3 — Confidentiality in practice (4 min)
- What's said in internal meetings stays in the room
- No photos or screen captures of internal slides, whiteboards or screens
- You may talk about **what you learned** in general terms

**Speaker notes:** Give a good example: "I learned how CPF contributions are split" (fine) vs "Our team had a meeting about Mr X" (never).

### Slide 1.2.4 — Activity: "Red flag, green flag" role-play (8 min)
- Pairs: one is the intern, one is a friend asking questions over lunch
- Friend card prompts: "So what did your team's clients buy?", "Can you send me that slide?", "What did you learn today?"
- Intern replies within the rules; swap after 3 prompts

**Speaker notes:** Model answers:
- "What did clients buy?" → "I didn't see any client information, and I couldn't share it if I did."
- "Can you send me that slide?" → "Internal slides stay internal; I can send you the MoneySense page it was based on."
- "What did you learn today?" → "How a payslip is split into take-home pay and CPF" (general learning: fine).

Watch for interns over-sharing names of the principal's staff or internal plans; gently correct.

### Slide 1.2.5 — Who to call (3 min)
- Buddy: day-to-day questions
- Welfare contact (not your supervisor): **[JASON: name and phone]**
- Your school's career office is also available
- You may leave early for medical or family reasons without penalty

**Speaker notes:** Hand out the welfare-contact card. Repeat: absence terms are in the allowance letter. Don't discuss the allowance amount on a slide.

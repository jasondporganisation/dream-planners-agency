# End-of-Internship Feedback Survey

> Run on the last day (Thu 24 Dec), 15 minutes, **anonymous by default**. Supervisors leave the room. Results go to Jason and Heron as totals only; with a cohort of 2–4, don't report any group of answers that could identify one person.
> Scale: 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree, plus "N/A".

---

**Intro text:**
> Thanks for spending three weeks with us. This survey is anonymous unless you choose to add your name at the end. Your answers won't affect your certificate, allowance or reference. Please be honest — we'll use this to improve the next programme.

## A. Learning
1. The internship met the learning goals I set in week 1.
2. Workshops — rate how useful each was (1–5):
   - W1 Budgeting and your first paycheque
   - W2 Emergency funds, debt and credit
   - W3 CPF basics
   - W4 Insurance concepts in plain English
   - W5 Scams and checking who you're dealing with
   - W6 Talking about money fairly
3. I can now explain at least three personal-finance topics to a friend in plain English.
4. I understand what a financial adviser does and the rules they work under.
5. The capstone was a worthwhile project.
6. The compliance review of my capstone helped me improve it.
7. What's the most useful thing you learned? (free text)

## B. How the programme was run
8. I knew what was expected of me each day.
9. My supervisor and buddy were available when I needed help.
10. The workload was reasonable.
11. The hours and arrangements matched what my offer letter said.
12. My allowance and terms were clear before I started.

## C. The non-advising rules (important — please answer honestly)
13. The rules on what interns may and may not do were explained clearly.
14. At any point, was I asked or encouraged to do any of these? (tick all that apply)
   - [ ] Give financial advice or recommendations to anyone
   - [ ] Talk about or promote a specific product, insurer or firm
   - [ ] Attend or listen in to a meeting or call with a client
   - [ ] See client names or personal details
   - [ ] Share contacts of friends or family, or invite people to meet an adviser
   - [ ] Post about financial products on my social media
   - [ ] None of these
15. Did I ever feel pressured to sign up for a career talk, exams or an adviser role? (No / A little / Yes)
16. If you ticked anything in 14 or answered "A little" / "Yes" to 15, please tell us what happened (optional; you can also tell the welfare contact or your school privately).

## D. Wellbeing and fairness
17. I felt safe, respected and included.
18. I was treated fairly compared with other interns.
19. I knew who to go to if I had a concern that I didn't want to raise with my supervisor.

## E. Overall
20. Overall, I'd rate this internship: 1–10.
21. How likely are you to recommend it to a friend? 0–10.
22. One thing we should **keep**: (free text)
23. One thing we should **change**: (free text)
24. Anything else? (free text)
25. Optional: your name, if you'd like us to follow up on anything.

---

## For Jason / Heron: how to use the results
- **Any tick in Q14 other than "None", or "Yes" in Q15, is a red flag.** Investigate the same week, fix the cause, and record what you did. Repeated issues = pause the programme.
- Track over cohorts: Q3, Q4, Q13, Q17 (target average ≥ 4), Q20 (target ≥ 8).
- Workshop scores below 3.5 → rewrite that workshop before the next run.
- Report to Heron by Fri 15 Jan 2027 with: totals, top 3 "keep", top 3 "change", red flags and actions.
- This survey measures the programme, **not** recruitment. Don't report career-talk sign-ups as a success measure of the internship.

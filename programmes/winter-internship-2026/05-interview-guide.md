# Interview Guide — 25 minutes, structured

> Every candidate gets the **same questions in the same order**, scored with the rubric in [`04-scoring-rubric.md`](04-scoring-rubric.md). This follows TAFEP's guidance on fair job interviews: ask about ability to do the job, not personal characteristics ([TAFEP, Job interviews](https://www.tal.sg/tafep/employment-practices/recruitment/conducting-job-interviews)).
> Two interviewers. Online or in person, the candidate's choice. Offer adjustments if asked (more time, camera off, written questions in advance).

## Before the interview
- Read only the candidate's Section C answers. Don't look them up on social media.
- Have the score sheet and this guide open. Take notes on **what the candidate said**, not impressions ("confident", "polished").
- Send the "explain task" topic (Q2) **24 hours in advance** so nobody is disadvantaged by nerves.

## Opening (2 min) — read aloud
> "Thanks for coming. This is a 25-minute interview with six questions; every candidate gets the same ones. The internship is a learning programme in personal-finance education. It's non-advising and non-sales: interns don't give financial advice, sell anything or meet clients. There are no right or wrong career plans. At the end you can ask us anything."

## Questions (20 min)

**Q1. Learning motivation (3 min)**
"What do you want to get out of this programme? What would make it worthwhile for you?"
- Follow-up: "What do you already do to learn about money, if anything?"

**Q2. Explain task (4 min)**
"Yesterday we sent you a topic: *what an emergency fund is and why it matters*. Please explain it to us as if we're 17 and have never heard of it. Two minutes."
- Follow-up: "How would you check that your explanation is accurate?"
- Score clarity and accuracy, not presentation polish or accent.

**Q3. Teamwork (3 min)**
"Tell us about a time a group you were in disagreed or got stuck. What did you do?"
- Follow-up: "What would you do differently?"

**Q4. Understanding the rules (3 min)**
"Interns here are not allowed to give financial advice or talk about products. Why do you think rules like that exist?"
- Looking for: protecting people making important decisions; only qualified, licensed people advise.

**Q5. Scenario (3 min)**
"During the internship, your uncle messages you: 'You're working with financial advisers now — which insurance plan should I buy?' What do you reply?"
- A 4: politely declines to advise, explains they're an intern and not licensed, suggests he speak to a licensed adviser of his own choosing or check MoneySense; tells the supervisor if unsure. Does **not** offer to pass his details to the team.

**Q6. Capstone idea (4 min)**
"Your project will be a short financial-literacy campaign for young people. What topic would you pick, who is it for, and what format?"

## Candidate questions (3 min)
Answer honestly. Common ones:
- *"Will I be expected to join as an adviser after?"* → "No. Some interns choose to come to an optional career talk afterwards. Nobody is asked to. Most interns go on to other things, and that's a good outcome too. You're not bonded. Please do read the principal insurer's participation agreement before you sign: it has its own terms, for example on joining a different team within a year if you later choose this career."
- *"How much do advisers earn?"* → "We don't discuss earnings in this programme. If you're 21+ and come to the optional career talk later, we'll explain how pay is structured and the costs and risks, without income figures or promises."
- *"Is the allowance linked to anything?"* → "No. It's S$300 plus a S$100 completion bonus for attending all 5 days and presenting on Day 5, the same for every intern. It isn't linked to sales, leads, exams or joining."

## Do not ask (TAFEP and Workplace Fairness Act)

| Don't ask | Ask instead (if relevant) |
|---|---|
| "How old are you?" / "What year were you born?" | Nothing. The 21+ condition is a yes/no on the form and is checked separately by sighting ID, outside the interview. |
| "Where are you from?" / "What's your race/religion?" | Nothing. |
| "Are you married? Do you have a boyfriend/girlfriend? Plan to have kids?" | "Can you attend all programme days?" (already on the form) |
| "Have you done NS? When do you enlist?" | Nothing. The NS condition is a yes/no on the form. |
| "Do you have any health conditions?" | "Do you need any adjustments to take part?" |
| "What do your parents do?" / "Do your parents have insurance?" | Nothing. |
| "Do you know many people who'd want financial planning?" / "How big is your network?" | Never ask. Not a lead-generation programme. |
| "How much do you want to earn?" | Nothing. |

## Closing (1 min)
> "Thank you. We'll let everyone know by [JASON: date]. Whatever the result, you can ask us for short feedback."

## After the interview
- Each interviewer scores alone first, then agree scores (see rubric).
- Store notes in the selection folder only. Delete according to the retention schedule in [`07-pdpa-consent.md`](07-pdpa-consent.md).

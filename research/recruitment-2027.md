## 1. Who joins and why

### The pool is shrinking, and fastest among the young
MAS figures for **life and composite insurance agents** ([MAS parliamentary reply, 2 Apr 2024](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/parliamentary-replies/2024/pq-on-insurance-agents-in-labour-force)):

| Year | 21–30 | 31–40 | 41–50 | 51–60 | 60+ | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 4,735 | 4,499 | 2,472 | 2,127 | 1,372 | 15,205 |
| 2022 | 4,101 | 4,443 | 2,471 | 2,102 | 1,429 | 14,546 |
| 2023 | 3,773 | 4,207 | 2,428 | 2,048 | 1,478 | 13,934 |

- The **21–30 band fell 20%** in two years (4,735 to 3,773). It peaked at 6,031 in 2018 ([MAS reply, 5 Apr 2021](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/parliamentary-replies/2021/reply-to-parliamentary-question-on-statistics-on-insurance-agents)). Only the 60+ band grew.
- "There is an even distribution of male and female agents." "About 90% of the agents are Singapore citizens." MAS doesn't separate full-time from part-time agents and doesn't collect education data (same 2024 reply).
- LIA counts of reps holding exclusive tied-agency contracts: **13,771** at end-2023 ([LIA](https://www.lia.org.sg/media/4141/20240215_lia-4q2023-results_media-release.pdf)), **12,767** at end-2024 ([LIA](https://lia.org.sg/media/4458/20250213_lia-4q2024-results_media-release.pdf)), **12,197** at mid-2025 ([LIA](https://www.lia.org.sg/media/4643/20250813_lia-1h2025-results_media-release.pdf)), and **12,389** at end-2025 ([LIA](https://www.lia.org.sg/media/4789/20260211_lia-4q2025-results_media-release.pdf)). That is about a 10% fall over two years, with a small recovery in late 2025. The 2026 LIA releases don't give this count.

### Business is moving to FA firms
By weighted new-business premium in FY2025, FA representatives brought in **35.7%**, bank reps **32.9%** and tied reps **28.2%** ([LIA 4Q2025](https://www.lia.org.sg/media/4789/20260211_lia-4q2025-results_media-release.pdf)). In 1H 2026 the shares were 37.3% FA and 25.8% tied ([LIA 1H2026](https://www.lia.org.sg/media/4950/20260812_lia-1h2026-results_media-release.pdf)). Recruits now have more choice of platform than before, and FA firms compete hard for them.

### Career switchers vs fresh graduates
- **No industry-wide split has been published.** The big insurers target both groups. Prudential's Management Associate Programme targets "fresh university or polytechnic graduates and young professionals" and features a 30-year-old career switcher from finance operations ([Prudential, Mar 2024](https://www.prudential.com.sg/about/newsroom/press-release/2024/prudential-ramps-up-hiring)). Income recruited "fresh graduates and mid-career job seekers" with "little to no financial advisory experience" ([Income, Jan 2021](https://www.income.com.sg/about-us/corporate-information/press-releases/ntuc-income-opens-up-1,200-job-opportunities-with)).
- **Mid-career supply in 2027:** in finance and insurance, retrenchments rose "from 510 in Q4 2025 to 560 in Q1 2026" while the resignation rate "fell to 0.6%" ([Insurance Business, 2026](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/asia/news/breaking-news/singapore-insurance-sector-posts-recordlow-resignation-rate-amid-restructuring-wave-579479.aspx)). Few people are leaving jobs voluntarily, so likely switchers are mostly the retrenched and people who feel stuck. **(Inference, not a measured figure.)**

### Why people join
No Singapore survey of motives was found. Recruiters' own copy leans on flexibility, being your own boss and income without a cap, e.g. "more control over your time and income" ([job ad](https://jobleads.com/sg/job/independent-financial-advisor-representative-mid-career-switch-opportunity--singapore--e162e938caeaadd146c3c5a30dfaa3548)). Ray Alliance's JD takes the opposite line ("not suitable for individuals looking for quick commissions"), which suggests some firms see a market for recruits who are put off by hype. **The reasons people join are untested assumptions. careerdna.sg can measure them directly** (see recommendations).

## 2. What new representatives earn in years 1–2, and how agencies present it

### Hard data is thin
- **No published median first-year income** for new Singapore reps was found.
- **MOM Occupational Wages, June 2022:** "insurance sales agent/broker (including independent financial planner)" in the insurance industry had a median gross of **S$5,050 a month** (basic S$4,062) ([data.gov.sg](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_43a37d383fe7b33368f1a3465467571d/view)). **Caveat:** the survey covers *employees* of firms with 25 or more staff, and basic wage excludes commissions. Commission-only tied agents are self-employed and mostly not counted. **Don't use this as a new-rep benchmark.**
- **Self-reported or unsourced figures:** Payscale has 3 responses (2018) for a Prudential Financial Consultant ([Payscale](https://www.payscale.com/research/SG/Job=Financial_Consultant/Salary/5a6a1141/Prudential)), which is too few to use. A careers blog gives S$24k–54k a year in years 1–2 with no source ([SkillUp](https://www.skillup.sg/blog/financial-planner-salary-singapore)). **(Estimate, low confidence.)**

The pattern: an "up to" allowance figure, conditional on hitting production targets, over 18–36 months. ### Rules on earnings claims in recruitment ads
**Bottom line: no MAS or LIA rule aimed specifically at income claims in recruitment ads was found in public sources**, and no 2023–2026 MAS/LIA warning about "earn $X a month" ads either. That doesn't make such claims safe. Five frameworks apply nearby:

1. **MAS digital advertising guidelines (FSG-03).** Issued 25 Sep 2025 and "effective from 25 March 2026", they "apply to all financial institutions and their appointed third parties, including online content creators" ([MAS, 25 Sep 2025](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2025/initiatives-to-promote-responsible-online-financial-content); [Allen & Gledhill summary](https://www.allenandgledhill.com/sg/perspectives/articles/31293/sgkh-mas-publishes-guidelines-on-standards-of-conduct-for-digital-advertising-activities-to-promote-responsible-online-financial-content-sharing-and-advertising)). They are written for ads about financial products and services, and the MAS release doesn't mention recruitment. But a rep's TikTok or Instagram account mixing "join my team" posts with product content is digital content the FA firm is responsible for. **Interpretation, not legal advice:** treat recruitment posts on reps' social accounts as covered and run them through the firm's ad-approval process.
2. **MAS on recruitment incentives.** MAS warned that mass-recruitment practices "increase the risk of FA representatives engaging in aggressive sales tactics" ([MAS, 7 Mar 2018](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2018/promoting-responsible-recruitment-in-the-financial-advisory-industry)). Its final measures for experienced recruits: sign-on incentives "paid out over a minimum period of six years", first-year payment "capped at 50% of a representative's average annual remuneration in the preceding three years", and extra safeguards for "30 or more representatives from the same FA firm within a 60-day period" ([MAS response, 6 May 2019](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/MAS/News-and-Publications/Consultation-Papers/Response-to-consultation-paper-on-measures-to-address-the-risks-posed-by-the-use-of-signon-incentive.pdf)). LIA's incentive guidelines (2019) were replaced on **13 Feb 2026** by a revised version "shared with LIA members" only ([LIA](https://www.lia.org.sg/industry-guidelines/distribution-channels/2019/lia-guidelines-on-the-use-of-incentives-in-the-recruitment-of-financial-advisory-representatives/)). **Ask your principal insurer or FA firm for the 2026 text before designing any allowance.**
3. **LIA "Good Recruitment Practices" (6 May 2024).** Hiring assessments should involve someone who "should not have sales and recruitment targets", and recruits with adverse records need enhanced monitoring ([LIA](https://www.lia.org.sg/media/4208/good-recruitment-practices-in-the-hiring-of-financial-advisory-representatives-6-may-2024.pdf)). It says nothing about advertising.
4. **MAS recruitment and onboarding expectations (Dec 2025).** "Structured recruitment policies and processes, approved by senior management" ([MAS thematic review](https://www.mas.gov.sg/publications/monographs-or-information-paper/2025/thematic-review-of-financial-institutions-recruitment-and-onboarding-training-of-representatives); [Linklaters summary](https://financialregulation.linklaters.com/post/102m25m/singapore-mas-sets-out-expectations-for-recruitment-and-onboarding-of-financial)). A recruitment ad promising income the firm can't show typical recruits earning would be hard to defend under this.
5. **General advertising and fair-hiring rules.** The ASAS Singapore Code of Advertising Practice covers all advertising through industry self-regulation ([ASAS](https://asas.org.sg/About/Singapore-Code-of-Advertising-Practice-SCAP)). The Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices cover job ads, especially discriminatory criteria such as age ([TAFEP](https://www.tal.sg/tafep/Getting-Started/Fair/Tripartite-Guidelines)), and the Workplace Fairness Act will turn much of this into law ([TAFEP, 2026](https://www.tal.sg/tafep/resources/articles/2026/fair-recruitment-practices-for-employers-in-singapore---preparing-for-the-workplace-fairness-act)). **Don't advertise for age bands. Target by life stage (graduating, career switch) without making age a requirement.**

**What market practice shows:** some insurers' own pages make broad claims, e.g. Great Eastern's FAQ says income "is usually much higher than a regular salaried job" ([Great Eastern FAQ](https://www.greateasternlife.com/sg/en/careers/financial-representative/frequently-asked-questions.html)). Aggregator ads quote "$2K–$10K monthly" ranges (search snippets). Ray Alliance's anti-hype positioning is the alternative (section 3).

### What the market's offers have in common
- **Exam sponsorship plus a small upfront allowance** (about $700–$2,700 at Income) is the floor.
- **Large guaranteed or performance-linked monthly allowances** come from the big insurers, run over 2–3 years, and are tied to targets.
- **Named mentoring and a first-year roadmap** are standard claims. A small team can make these concrete in a way big agencies can't.

## 4. Licensing path, exam costs, timelines and who pays

### Entry requirements
- Under MAS Notice **FAA-N26** (in force since 1 Apr 2024), reps must "be at least 21 years old" and hold at least "a GCE 'A' Level certificate with passes in at least three subjects at 'Higher 2' level and two subjects at 'Higher 1' level", an IB Diploma, a Singapore polytechnic diploma, or equivalent ([FAA-N26](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/notices/cmg/notice-faa-n26/faa-n26-notice-on-competency-requirements-for-representatives-of-financial-advisers-1.pdf)). "A degree in any discipline will also be acceptable" ([MAS FAQ](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/faqs/cmi/faqs-on-competency-requirements-for-representatives-of-financial-advisers/faa-exam-notice-faqs.pdf)). **Many undergraduates are under 21 (most women in years 1–2, and anyone who did not do National Service), so check each intern's age: those under 21 can't be appointed until their 21st birthday, whatever exams they pass.** Male undergraduates who have finished NS are usually 21 or older.
- **Fit and proper:** MAS [Guidelines FSG-G01](https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/guidelines/guidelines-on-fit-and-proper-criteria). The firm must "certify that the individual is fit and proper via the Corporation and Representatives System" and notify MAS through the Representative Notification Framework (RNF). MAS charges **$200 per new lodgement** and a **$100 annual fee per representative** ([MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/capital-markets/appointing-a-representative-for-licensed-fas-or-exempt-fas)).

### Exam modules for a typical life/health rep
**Correction to common usage: M5 no longer exists.** "The CMFAS M5 examination module will be renamed as RES5 Rules, Ethics and Skills for Financial Advisory Services" from 1 Apr 2024 ([SCI notice](https://www.scicollege.org.sg/docs/NewCMFASExams.pdf)). MAS: "They are required to pass RES5, M9 and M9A of the CMFAS Examination" ([MAS FAQ Q20](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/faqs/cmi/faqs-on-competency-requirements-for-representatives-of-financial-advisers/faa-exam-notice-faqs.pdf)). **HI** is needed to sell health insurance ([SCI HI](https://www.scicollege.org.sg/Exam/ExamDetails/HI)). All four are run by the **Singapore College of Insurance (SCI)**. M8/M8A (collective investment schemes) are needed only for unit trusts. Prudential says reps must "attain Capital Markets and Financial Advisory Services (CMFAS) certification" for its 2027 unit-trust rollout ([Media OutReach via Viet Nam News, 23 Sep 2026](https://vietnamnews.vn/media-outreach/1800379/prudential-singapore-expands-advisory-services-to-help-customers-plan-and-grow-wealth-for-retirement.html)). Expect recruits at some platforms to need more modules.

| Module | Format | Pass mark | Fee, non-member, weekday 9–5 (incl. GST) | Member fee, weekday |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RES5 | 150 MCQ, 3 h | Part I 75%, Part II 80% | **S$218.00** | S$185.30 |
| M9 | 100 MCQ, 2 h | 70% | **S$130.80** | S$109.00 |
| M9A | 50 MCQ, 1 h | 70% | **S$130.80** | S$109.00 |
| HI | 50 MCQ, 1 h 15 min | 70% | **S$109.00** | (single rate) |
| **Total, first-time passes** | | | **S$588.60** | **S$512.30** |

Fees are from SCI's exam pages, checked 3 Nov 2026: [RES5](https://www.scicollege.org.sg/Exam/ExamDetails/RES5), [M9](https://www.scicollege.org.sg/Exam/ExamDetails/M9), [M9A](https://www.scicollege.org.sg/Exam/ExamDetails/M9A), [HI](https://www.scicollege.org.sg/Exam/ExamDetails/HI). Totals are my own sums. After-5pm and weekend sessions cost more (e.g. RES5 S$236.53 non-member). M9 and M9A can be taken together as CM-LIP (S$283.40 non-member; [SCI](https://www.scicollege.org.sg/Exam/ExamDetails/CM-LIP)), but that costs S$21.80 **more** than sitting M9 and M9A separately (S$261.60 non-member; members pay S$239.80 vs S$218.00). The gap isn't the registration fee: CM-LIP includes one S$21.80 fee, while two separate sittings include two. SCI simply prices the combined paper higher. CM-LIP saves a trip, not money. Fees include a non-refundable registration fee (S$21.80 CMFAS, S$10.90 HI). Since 1 Apr 2024 first attempts and retakes cost the same. "SkillsFuture Credit is not applicable for CMFAS Examinations. UTAP funding support is available for eligible NTUC members" (SCI fee pages). **Budget for resits: plan on about S$700–900 a recruit (estimate)**, since RES5 Part II's 80% pass mark is the usual stumbling block.

### Timelines
- Exams run "daily on weekday", and CMFAS/HI results are given "immediately upon completion" (SCI registration policies).
- **A RES5 pass lapses** if the person doesn't start giving advice "within three years after the date of passing that module" ([FAA-N26 para 4.15](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/notices/cmg/notice-faa-n26/faa-n26-notice-on-competency-requirements-for-representatives-of-financial-advisers-1.pdf)). Product modules don't need retaking.
- **No official end-to-end timeline was found.** Income's allowance window (exams passed "within three months prior to appointment date and three months after", [Income](https://www.income.com.sg/careers/be-a-financial-planner/financial-consultant)) suggests insurers expect exams to take a few weeks to about 3 months. **Planning estimate: 4–8 weeks from offer to RNF appointment** for a focused full-time recruit (study, four sittings, firm onboarding, RNF lodgement). **(Estimate.)**
- **CPD after licensing:** "at least 30 hours of structured CPD training annually", six of them on "Ethics, Rules and Regulations or relating to the Central Provident Fund" ([FAA-N26 / MAS FAQ](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/faqs/cmi/faqs-on-competency-requirements-for-representatives-of-financial-advisers/faa-exam-notice-faqs.pdf)).

### Who pays
- **Usually the candidate first, then the firm reimburses on conditions.** Income gives "Exam Sponsorship of $688", a "$1,000 CMFAS allowance" and a "$1,000 training allowance" ([Income careers](https://www.income.com.sg/careers/be-a-financial-planner/financial-consultant)). If the agreement ends "within 12 months from the date you are successfully registered under the RNF", the amount becomes "immediately due to Income" ([Income T&Cs](https://www.income.com.sg/ag-support-tncs)). Ray Alliance offers "examination guidance and subsidies" (section 3).
- **Government:** For selected Career Conversion Programmes with structured training, a "course fee subsidy of 70% will be provided" ([MOM CCP factsheet, Budget 2024](https://www.iac.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/budget2024/factsheet-on-ccp-and-cf.pdf)). These are employer-run conversions into salaried jobs. No CCP for commission-based FA reps was found, and SkillsFuture Credit can't be used for CMFAS exams (above). **Don't plan around government funding.**

**Channel quality matters more than volume.** LIMRA (US) found "only 15% of financial professionals in agency systems that recruit mainly inexperienced individuals" stayed, and "the majority of those left within their first two years" ([InsuranceNewsNet, 1 Mar 2023](https://insurancenewsnet.com/innarticle/5-key-factors-that-help-retain-new-financial-professionals)). ### Internships: the regulatory line
Unlicensed interns must not give financial advice. MAS's requirements "aim to ensure that such services are only provided by fit and proper and adequately qualified persons", and it has banned unappointed individuals for doing so: 3–5-year bans in the 2023 sub-agency case ([MAS, 2023](https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/enforcement/enforcement-actions/2023/mas-bans-four-former-representatives-and-one-other-individual-for-unlawful-sub-agency-activities)). Interns can shadow, research and run events. They should not quote, recommend or close. Universities also expect written key employment terms, and student-pass holders are limited to 16 hours a week during term ([NUS guidelines](https://fass.nus.edu.sg/cnm/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2020/06/internship-guidelines-for-employers.pdf)).

### Recruitment practices regulators have penalised
- **Supervision caps:** On 19 Sep 2025 MAS fined Singlife FA **$93,750** for "failing to implement effective policies relating to recruitment of representatives". Managers "recruit[ed] more Tier 1 representatives than they were allowed to supervise" and "parked" the excess under others ([MAS, 19 Sep 2025](https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/enforcement/enforcement-actions/2025/mas-issues-composition-against-singlife-fa-pte-ltd-and-takes-reg-action-against-8-individuals)). **Size your intake to what you can supervise.**
- **Upfront incentives:** MAS warned that mass-recruitment incentives "increase the risk of FA representatives engaging in aggressive sales tactics" and proposed spreading sign-on incentives "over a minimum period of 6 years" ([MAS, 2018](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2018/promoting-responsible-recruitment-in-the-financial-advisory-industry)).

## 6. Retention benchmarks

**Singapore: no published 90-day, 13-month or 1-year agent retention figure** was found from MAS, LIA or any insurer. Prudential's own view is that reps who "persist for at least two years are more likely to stay" ([Prudential, 2024](https://www.prudential.com.sg/about/newsroom/press-release/2024/prudential-ramps-up-hiring)).

**Regional and global benchmarks (not Singapore):**
| Market | Figure | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| South Korea, 1H 2021 | 13-month agent retention averaged **41.5%** at 20 life insurers and **57.6%** at non-life insurers | [Asia Economy, citing FSS](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2021120806572644664) | Verified |
| US (LIMRA, 2020 data) | "only 15%" of new reps in agencies recruiting mainly inexperienced people stayed; "the majority of those left within their first two years" | [InsuranceNewsNet, 2023](https://insurancenewsnet.com/innarticle/5-key-factors-that-help-retain-new-financial-professionals) | Verified (secondary) |

| Hong Kong | 62% of new licences still active; >50% chance of quitting before the first 3-year licence ends | [HK Insurance Authority](https://www.ia.org.hk/en/legislative_framework/Conduct_in_Focus_Issue_10_03.html) | Unverified (page blocked) |

No reliable 90-day benchmark exists; "30% leave in 90 days" figures online have no source. **Planning estimate for Dream Planners:** assume about 40–60% of contracted recruits are still active at 13 months, which is the Korean range. Set your own baseline from your first cohort. **(Estimate.)**

**Regulatory angle:** MAS's [Dec 2025 thematic review of recruitment and onboarding](https://www.mas.gov.sg/publications/monographs-or-information-paper/2025/thematic-review-of-financial-institutions-recruitment-and-onboarding-training-of-representatives) expects "structured recruitment policies and processes, approved by senior management" and competency assessment before reps advise ([Linklaters summary](https://financialregulation.linklaters.com/post/102m25m/singapore-mas-sets-out-expectations-for-recruitment-and-onboarding-of-financial)). Retention problems that come from weak onboarding are now a supervisory issue, not just a business one.

## 7. Ten recommendations for Dream Planners (small team, careerdna.sg, winter internship)

1. **Turn careerdna.sg into a recruiting funnel, carefully.** No Singapore agency was found running a public career quiz as a recruiting lead magnet (section 5). Add an optional, consented "Is advisory work a fit for you?" result path that books a free 20-minute career conversation. Keep the test useful even for people who never apply. Collect only what the PDPA consent covers.
2. **Use the test data to replace guesses about motives.** No Singapore survey explains why people join (section 1). Add 3–5 anonymised questions (motive, life stage, income needs, risk tolerance) and report aggregate patterns to Heron each quarter. This becomes your own benchmark.
3. **Write recruitment copy with no income figures.** There's no explicit MAS/LIA rule against income claims, but FSG-03 (since 25 Mar 2026), MAS's Dec 2025 recruitment expectations and ASAS all favour truthful, substantiated content. Show what you provide (exam sponsorship, a named mentor, a structured first 90 days) instead of "up to $X" (section 2). Route every post, including reps' personal social posts, through the firm's ad approval.
4. **Lead with an honest cost table.** Publish "what it costs you to start": about S$589 in exam fees (section 4), the 21+ age and education requirements, and the realities of commission. Treat honesty as the brand, as Ray Alliance does. It also screens out hype-seekers, who probably leave early **(inference)**.
5. **Sponsor exams with fair clawback terms.** Match the market floor: reimburse RES5, M9, M9A and HI on passing, and budget about S$700–900 a recruit for resits **(estimate)**. Any clawback should be modest and clearly disclosed (Income uses 12 months, section 4).
6. **Run the winter internship as a non-advising programme.** Interns can't advise until appointed, and most undergraduates are under 21 (section 4; MAS bans in section 5). Build it like SCI's INSPIRE: shadowing, research, events and a client-needs case study. Pay at least the university norm (NUS ≥ S$800, NTU recommends S$1,250, section 5). Give written key employment terms, and offer an RES5 study track for those aged 21+.
7. **Cap each intake at what you can supervise.** The Singlife FA fine (S$93,750, Sep 2025) came from recruiting beyond supervision limits (section 5). For a small team, plan **cohorts of 2–4 per supervisor (estimate)**, starting together so training and mentoring can be shared.
8. **Focus effort on the first 90 days.** No Singapore retention benchmark exists. Overseas data suggests about 40–60% of new agents are still active at 13 months, and most leave within two years (section 6). Set weekly mentoring, activity goals that aren't only sales, and a 30/60/90-day check-in led by someone without recruitment targets, as LIA recommends (section 4 / LIA 2024). Track 90-day and 13-month retention from the January 2027 cohort onward.
9. **Fish where the big insurers don't.** Prudential, AIA and Singlife FA are visible at NUS/NTU fairs and roadshows. Instead target faculty societies, SUSS and polytechnic graduates (most current polytechnic students are under 21, so treat them as internship and pipeline leads, not appointable recruits; ITE graduates usually lack the required diploma, so check eligibility first), InternSG listings, and retrenched mid-career finance professionals (sector retrenchments rose to 560 in Q1 2026, section 1). Ask every intern and recruit for one referral. Referrals are reportedly the strongest channel overseas **(unverified)**.
10. **Check the platform's 2026 terms before finalising.** For Dream Planners the platform is its principal insurer (a tied agency), so every item below is a question for the principal's agency office, not for an FA firm. The LIA's revised incentive guidelines (13 Feb 2026) are members-only, and the business continues to shift to FA firms (37.3% of weighted premium in 1H 2026). Before the January plan is locked, get from the principal insurer's agency office: the 2026 LIA text, its exam reimbursement policy, any allowance scheme you can offer, and its ad-approval process. Treat this as the go/no-go list for the plan.

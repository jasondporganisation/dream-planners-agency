## How to use

- Answers are general information for existing clients. They do not consider anyone's specific objectives, financial situation or needs.
- Copy, then personalise. Where an answer says "check your own policy documents or ask me", the details depend on the client's own policy.
- No product or insurer names, no guarantees, no selling. If a client asks about buying or switching, pass it to Jason.
- Quote figures with their date. Government figures change, usually in January (CPF) and April (MediShield Life).

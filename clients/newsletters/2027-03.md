### Getting through tax filing season calmly

Most of us file our income tax with IRAS around this time of year. Much of your information may already be filled in for you, but it's still worth a careful read before you submit. Filing dates, reliefs and how-to guides are all on the official [IRAS individual income tax page](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax).

**Three practical tips**

1. **Check the filing deadline on the IRAS website and put it in your calendar now.** Filing early leaves time to sort out anything that looks wrong.
2. **Review every pre-filled item instead of just clicking submit.** Compare income and reliefs against your own records, such as payslips and receipts.
3. **Look out for any relief you planned for last year.** For example, if you made a CPF cash top-up before 31 Dec, check that it appears correctly. If something's missing, the [IRAS page on CPF cash top-up relief](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-reliefs-rebates-and-deductions/tax-reliefs/central-provident-fund-(cpf)-cash-top-up-relief) explains how it works.

I can't give tax advice. But if you'd like a review of your wider plans once tax season is over, just reply to this email.

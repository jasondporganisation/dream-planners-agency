### Festive season, steady habits

Chinese New Year is about family, generosity and fresh starts. It is also a season when spending can quietly add up: red packets, new clothes, goodies and gatherings. A little planning lets you enjoy it fully without a January-style surprise when the bills arrive.

**Three practical tips**

1. **Set a red-packet and festive budget before visiting starts.** Decide roughly what you'll give and spend, then track it as you go. A simple note on your phone is enough.
2. **Turn the children's red packets into a savings lesson.** Sit down together, count what they received, and agree how much goes to savings and how much they can spend. It's one of the best money conversations you can have with them.
3. **Use the fresh start to set one money goal for the year.** Keep it specific and small, for example building up your emergency savings or reviewing your monthly spending once a quarter.

### Topping up your CPF before the year ends

If you have been thinking about a cash top-up to your own or a family member's CPF, timing matters. IRAS says you can get tax relief on a cash top-up in the following year if your application to the CPF Board is **received by 31 Dec** ([IRAS: CPF cash top-up relief](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-reliefs-rebates-and-deductions/tax-reliefs/central-provident-fund-(cpf)-cash-top-up-relief)).

A top-up is not right for everyone. Money put into CPF is set aside for retirement, so it is worth weighing it against what you may need in cash over the next few years.

**Three practical tips**

1. **Check your cash needs first.** Keep your emergency savings and any big upcoming expenses (school fees, renovation, a trip) in mind before moving money into CPF.
2. **Don't leave it to the last day.** What counts is when the CPF Board *receives* your application, so give it a few working days' buffer before 31 Dec.
3. **Read the official rules on limits and eligibility.** Relief caps, who counts as a family member, and how relief interacts with other reliefs are all set out on the IRAS page above. If anything there is unclear, I'm happy to walk through it with you.

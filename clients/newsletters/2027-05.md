### Your health, and your hospital cover

**MediShield Life** is basic, lifelong cover for large hospital bills and certain costly outpatient treatments. All Singapore Citizens and PRs have it automatically. It's sized for subsidised treatment in public hospitals (Class B2/C wards), so it pays a smaller share of Class A, B1 or private hospital bills ([MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/medishield-life/medishield-life/)). It is designed to leave some costs to you: a deductible each policy year, then a co-insurance share ([CPF Board](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/annex/understand-medishield-life-payouts)).

MOH has said the next phase of deductible changes starts on **1 Apr 2027**. [JASON: insert the new amounts only if confirmed on moh.gov.sg or cpf.gov.sg, otherwise delete this paragraph.]

**Three practical tips**

1. **Book the health screening you've been putting off.** Catching things early is good for your health and your peace of mind.
2. **Know which ward type you'd expect to use.** Then you'll understand roughly what MediShield Life would and wouldn't pay if you were hospitalised.
3. **Keep your important documents where your family can find them.** Make sure someone you trust knows where your policy documents are and how to reach me.

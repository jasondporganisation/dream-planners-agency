### A mid-year goals check-in

No need for a big overhaul. A short, honest look at where you are is usually enough to keep things on track or adjust gently. It also helps to plan for the long run. SingStat estimates that a Singapore resident who reaches 65 can expect to live about **21.6 more years on average** (2025 figures, [SingStat](https://www.singstat.gov.sg/news/complete-life-tables-for-singapore-resident-population-2024-2025)). So the plans you make today may need to last well into your 80s, or longer.

**Three practical tips**

1. **Revisit the one goal you set at the start of the year.** Are you on track? If not, change the timeline or the amount rather than dropping it.
2. **Check your emergency savings and monthly spending.** Prices and routines change, so a quick look at the last few months of spending can show where your money is really going.
3. **Note any life changes since January:** a new job, a move, a marriage, a new family member. Big changes like these can affect your plans, your nominations and your will. Marriage, for example, cancels an existing will and CPF nomination.

If you'd like a mid-year review, just reply to this email and we'll find a time that suits you.

## Email version

**Subject:** New year, a few CPF changes worth knowing

Hi [Client first name],

Happy New Year! I hope 2027 has started well for you and your loved ones. A few CPF changes took effect on 1 Jan, so here is a short summary.

### What changed on 1 Jan 2027

- **CPF contribution rates for older workers went up** ([CPF Board](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/infohub/news/cpf-related-announcements/new-contribution-rates)):
  - Above 55 to 60: total rate from 34% to **35.5%** of wages (employer 16.5%, employee 19%).
  - Above 60 to 65: total rate from 25% to **26%** (employer 13%, employee 13%).
  - Rates for other age bands are unchanged. The extra contributions go to the Retirement Account up to the Full Retirement Sum, then to the Ordinary Account.
- **The monthly Ordinary Wage ceiling stays at $8,000** ([2027 rate table, CPF Board](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/content/dam/web/employer/employer-obligations/documents/jan2027cpfcontributionrates.pdf)).
- **Retirement sums for members turning 55 in 2027:** Basic $114,100, Full $228,200, Enhanced $456,400 ([CPF Board](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/infohub/educational-resources/what-is-the-cpf-retirement-sum)). These are CPF benchmarks, not a measure of what any one person needs to retire.

**MediShield Life:** no change took effect on 1 Jan. MOH has said the next phase of deductible changes starts on **1 Apr 2027**, but the new amounts have not been published yet ([MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/government-accepts-medishield-life-council-s-recommendations-to-enhance-medishield-life-scheme---government-support-more-than-offsets-premium-increases/)). I'll share an update once they are.

**Three practical tips**

1. **Check your January payslip** if you are above 55 to 65. Your take-home pay may look slightly different because of the new employee rate.
2. **Log in to the CPF website or app** and see how your accounts look at the start of the year, so you have a baseline for 2027.
3. **Note your milestone year.** If you turn 55 this year, the 2027 retirement sums above are the ones that apply to you.

If you'd like a review of how these changes fit your plans, just reply to this email and we'll set up a time.

Educational calculators

Four simple tools to help talk through money questions. They show the maths, not a recommendation.

  • Emergency fund How many months of expenses your savings cover.
  • Retirement income gap An inflation-adjusted illustration using rates you enter.
  • CPF Retirement Sums How far a Retirement Account balance is from the Basic, Full and Enhanced Retirement Sums.
  • Cost of delay What starting to save later can mean, using a rate you enter.

For general information only; it does not consider your specific objectives, financial situation or needs. Jason Ng, Representative, Dream Planners.

This is an educational tool, not financial advice. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere: the page has no server, no cookies, no tracking and no external scripts.

Reference figures are from the Dream Planners fact pack (compliance/sg-fact-pack-2026-11.md, figures checked 3 Nov 2026). Government figures change, often in January; check the official source before relying on them.

Cost of delay

Compares saving the same amount every month starting now with starting a few years later, ending on the same date. You enter the rate.

Illustration only. This is not a projection or a guarantee. It uses the rates you type in, held steady every year, which real life never does. Actual results will be different.

Fill in every box and press Show illustration.

Assumptions

  • The same amount is saved at the end of every month, and both cases stop on the same end date.
  • The yearly return you entered is turned into an equal monthly rate and applied every month, without change.
  • No fees, taxes, withdrawals or missed months. Periods are rounded to whole months.
  • The difference shows the effect of saving for fewer months, plus the growth those months would have had at your assumed rate.

Retirement income gap

A rough illustration of how much money might be needed at retirement to top up other income to the level you want, after allowing for rising prices. You enter every rate.

Illustration only. This is not a projection or a guarantee. It uses the rates you type in, held steady every year, which real life never does. Actual results will be different.

Fill in every box and press Show illustration.

Assumptions

  • The gap today = income you want − other income. If other income covers it, the gap is $0.
  • The gap grows with the inflation rate you entered, every year, until retirement and through retirement.
  • Other income is assumed to keep pace with inflation. CPF LIFE Standard Plan payouts are level and not inflation-adjusted (CPF Board), so if you entered a CPF LIFE estimate the real gap may be larger than shown.
  • Money needed at retirement = the amount that, earning your "during retirement" return, pays the yearly gap at the start of each year for the number of years you entered (rounded up to whole years).
  • Your current savings grow at your "until retirement" return. No new savings, fees, taxes or withdrawals are included.
  • Rates are the same every year. Real returns and inflation go up and down, and returns can be negative.

Sources for reference figures: SingStat Complete Life Tables 2024–2025 · SingStat CPI Dec 2025 · SingStat CPI Sep 2026 · CPF LIFE, cpf.gov.sg

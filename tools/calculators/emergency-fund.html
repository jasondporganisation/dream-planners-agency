Emergency fund

An emergency fund is cash you can reach quickly if income stops or a big bill arrives. This shows how many months of expenses your savings would cover.

Fill in all three boxes and press Calculate.

How it works

  • Target = monthly expenses × months you chose.
  • Months covered = savings ÷ monthly expenses.
  • Assumes your expenses stay the same and ignores any interest on the savings.
  • No default figures are filled in: the fact pack has no official guideline for how big an emergency fund should be.

For general information only; it does not consider your specific objectives, financial situation or needs. Jason Ng, Representative, Dream Planners.

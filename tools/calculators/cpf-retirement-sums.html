CPF Retirement Sums

CPF sets three Retirement Sums each year: Basic (BRS), Full (FRS) and Enhanced (ERS). The figure that applies to you depends on the year you turn 55. Enter a Retirement Account balance to see how far it is from each.

Good to know

  • The Retirement Sums are CPF benchmarks for how much is set aside in the Retirement Account. They are not a measure of how much any one person needs to retire.
  • CPF's own estimates for someone turning 55 in 2026, Standard Plan, payouts from 65, assuming 4% interest: BRS about $950 a month, FRS about $1,780 a month, ERS about $3,440 a month. These are estimates, not guaranteed amounts; actual payouts depend on your plan, cohort and interest. (cpf.gov.sg)
  • This page only compares numbers. It does not check what you can withdraw, property pledges or any other CPF rule.

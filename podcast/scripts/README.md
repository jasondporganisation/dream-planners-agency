# Quiet Compounding — episode scripts (Ep 47 onwards)

Scripts for *Quiet Compounding*, Jason Ng's podcast **for financial advisory professionals in Singapore** about building the practice and the person behind it. Not a consumer show.

- Research source: `content/podcast-research-advisers/` (read `_RULES.md` first). Ep 47–56 use the first 10 topics of that pack.
- Compliance reference: `compliance/sg-fact-pack-2026-11.md` (section 5 covers MAS/FAA rules for representatives).
- Scripts up to Ep 46 live on Jason's Mac and are not in this repo.
- Every script here is `status: draft` until Brand Guardian and Jason's team have checked it. Nothing is voiced from this folder until then.

## Script format
- Front matter: `episode`, `title`, `track`, `status: draft`, `word_count` (spoken words), `sources` (list of URLs).
- `## Script` — the spoken text only, first person as Jason. ~1,400–1,800 spoken words (9–12 minutes).
- Structure: cold open → one clear idea → a short hypothetical example ("picture an adviser who…") → three actions for this week → closing line.
- `## Sources (not read aloud)` — every figure, rule or study mentioned on air, with date, link and status from the research pack.

## Voice and rules
- Calm, practical, plain English, short sentences that read well aloud. Numbers written as words where they are spoken.
- No insurer, bank, fund, platform, product or product-type names. No guarantees. No income, earnings or commission claims. No recruitment pitch.
- Only VERIFIED facts from the research pack. Regulatory items quoted as "MAS has said…" where the pack says so, and with "check with your compliance team".
- Say "checked by my team", never "approved by me".
- Examples are hypothetical only. No real clients, advisers or colleagues.

# Ep 66 — What MediSave Can and Cannot Pay For

**Theme:** Protecting what you build
**Listener's real question:** "I have money in MediSave. Why can't I use all of it when I see a doctor?"

## Key points (in order)
1. MediSave is your own medical savings account inside CPF, but withdrawals are capped by type of care. That stretches it over a lifetime.
2. Hospital stays have daily limits plus surgical limits that depend on the procedure's complexity.
3. Outpatient use is narrower. MediSave500/700 covers chronic conditions, vaccines and screenings. Flexi-MediSave adds $400 a year for seniors.
4. Chronic conditions under the CDMP (23 conditions) can be paid fully with MediSave at your enrolled Healthier SG clinic, within limits, with no 15% cash co-payment.
5. The Basic Healthcare Sum ($79,000 in 2026 for under-65s) is the MediSave cap. Contributions above it flow to other CPF accounts.
6. MediSave can pay MediShield Life premiums in full, and part of private top-up premiums within yearly limits.
7. Everyday GP visits for minor illnesses are generally cash, CHAS or Healthier SG subsidy territory. Do not promise MediSave covers them.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
"Mdm Lee", 68, has diabetes and sees her enrolled Healthier SG clinic. Her illustrative chronic-care bills come to $480 for the year.
- They fall within MediSave500 ($500 a year) for CDMP treatment, so they could be paid from MediSave with no 15% cash co-payment at her enrolled clinic.
- A separate illustrative $300 specialist outpatient bill could draw on Flexi-MediSave ($400 a year for 60+).
*Say on air: "Illustrative. Actual claims depend on the clinic, the condition and MOH rules at the time."*

## Common myths to correct
- "MediSave is just savings, so I can use it for anything medical." Withdrawals have limits and approved uses.
- "When MediSave hits the BHS, the extra is lost." It flows to other CPF accounts.
- "MediSave covers all my GP visits." Mostly not for acute minor illness.
- "MediSave is for old age only." It is used across life, e.g. for hospital stays and maternity.

## "Never say" lines
- "Use your MediSave for [specific plan]."
- "MediSave will cover your bills."
- Any premium amounts.

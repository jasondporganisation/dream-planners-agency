# Ep 58 — The First Year With Baby

**Theme:** Family and life stages
**Listener's real question:** "We're expecting our first child. What government support is there, and what changes are coming?"

## Key points (in order)
1. Support is changing. Births before April 2027 follow current Baby Bonus rules. A new SG Child Support Package starts from April 2027.
2. Current rules (births from 14 Feb 2023): the Baby Bonus Cash Gift is $11,000 for the 1st and 2nd child and $13,000 for the 3rd and later. The CDA First Step Grant is $5,000 ($10,000 for the 3rd and later born from 18 Feb 2025). Government co-matching applies to savings in the CDA.
3. New package (announced at NDR, 23 Aug 2026): up to $62,000 per Singapore Citizen child to age 17, the same for every birth order. That covers a $10,000 Baby Gift, $2,000 a year in Child Credits from age 1 to 16, a $5,000 CDA grant, up to $5,000 CDA co-matching, and a $10,000 PSEA top-up at 17.
4. Children already on the Cash Gift keep it until 31 Mar 2027, then get a top-up to $10,000 by 30 Apr 2027.
5. Tax reliefs for working parents include Qualifying Child Relief and Working Mother's Child Relief (fixed amounts for children born from 1 Jan 2024).
6. Budget the first year on real costs (childcare, medical, time off), not on grants alone.
7. Update paperwork: CPF nomination, will (with a guardian named for the child) and LPA.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
"Daryl and Sze Ying" plan for a baby due in mid-2027 (so the new package applies, if eligible).
- Expected support: the $10,000 Baby Gift in two payments, and the $5,000 CDA grant.
- Illustrative first-year costs they list: medical and delivery (after MediSave) $3,000; essentials $3,500; infant care from month 6 (after subsidies) $3,600.
- They treat government support as a cushion, and plan their own budget for the rest.
*Say on air: "Illustrative costs. Actual support depends on eligibility and the rules at your child's birth."*

## Common myths to correct
- "The Baby Bonus works the same as before." A new package applies from April 2027.
- "Third child gets much more under the new package." It is the same amount for every birth order.
- "Grants cover the cost of a baby." They help, but most costs are the family's.

## "Never say" lines
- "You must buy insurance for your baby."
- Naming CDA banks, hospitals or products.
- Pressure about whether or when to have children.

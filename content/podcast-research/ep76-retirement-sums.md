# Ep 76 — Basic, Full and Enhanced: The Retirement Sums

**Theme:** Retirement and CPF in depth
**Listener's real question:** "What do BRS, FRS and ERS actually mean, and do I have to hit one of them?"

## Key points (in order)
1. The retirement sums are benchmarks for how much you set aside in your Retirement Account (RA) at 55. Monthly payouts from 65 are based on that amount.
2. They depend on the year you turn 55, and are then fixed for life. For people turning 55 in 2026: BRS $110,200, FRS $220,400, ERS $440,800.
3. The ERS was raised to four times the BRS from 2025, so people who want to can set aside more.
4. CPF publishes rough payout estimates for each sum (e.g. about $1,780 a month from 65 at the FRS for the 2026 cohort). These are estimates, not promises.
5. Property owners can use their property to cover part of the FRS, which means setting aside the BRS in cash.
6. Not reaching a sum is not failure. It means smaller payouts, which other savings and family plans can supplement.
7. Choosing a sum is a trade-off between monthly income later and cash access now.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
"Ahmad" turns 55 in 2026 with an illustrative $250,000 across his OA and SA.
- About $220,400 goes into his RA (the FRS for his cohort).
- The remaining ~$29,600 stays in the OA and can be withdrawn.
- CPF's own published estimate for the FRS is around $1,780 a month from 65.
If he owned a flat with a long enough lease, he could choose to set aside only the BRS ($110,200) in cash. That gives more cash now and lower payouts later.
*Say on air: "Illustrative. Actual RA transfers depend on your balances and CPF's rules at the time."*

## Common myths to correct
- "CPF keeps all my money at 55." Savings above the FRS (or $5,000) can be withdrawn.
- "If I don't hit the FRS, I get nothing." You still get payouts, just smaller ones.
- "The sum keeps rising after I turn 55." BRS/FRS are fixed for your cohort once you reach 55.
- "ERS is something everyone must reach." It is an option, not a target.

## "Never say" lines
- "You must hit the ERS to retire comfortably."
- "Your payouts will be $X." Say "CPF estimates around…".
- "Pledging your property is the smart / best move."
- "The BRS is enough for anyone."

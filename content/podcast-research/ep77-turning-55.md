# Ep 77 — Turning 55: What Actually Changes

**Theme:** Retirement and CPF in depth
**Listener's real question:** "My 55th birthday is coming. What happens to my CPF, and can I take money out?"

## Key points (in order)
1. At 55, a Retirement Account (RA) is created, and savings move into it up to your cohort's Full Retirement Sum.
2. Since January 2025, the Special Account closes at 55. SA savings go to the RA up to the FRS, and anything left over moves to the OA.
3. You can withdraw $5,000, or everything above your FRS if you have more than that.
4. Retirement and payouts are not the same event. Payouts start from 65 at the earliest. The retirement age is 64.
5. 55 is a good moment to check your nominations, your LPA, and what your monthly needs might look like.
6. Withdrawn money loses CPF interest. Many people withdraw only what has a clear use.
7. Scammers target people around big CPF milestones. CPF will not ask you to transfer money to "unlock" savings.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
"Lina" turns 55 with an illustrative $180,000 in OA + SA combined. That is below her cohort's FRS.
- All of it is moved into her RA, and her SA closes.
- She can still withdraw up to $5,000.
- She plans a small home repair (illustrative $3,000) and leaves the rest in her RA to support payouts later.
*Say on air: "Illustrative. Your own CPF statement and the CPF website show your actual position."*

## Common myths to correct
- "At 55 I can take everything out." Only the amount above the FRS, or $5,000.
- "My SA still exists after 55." It is closed for members 55 and above (since Jan 2025).
- "Payouts start at 55." Payouts start from 65 at the earliest.
- "CPF will call me to verify my withdrawal." Be wary: that is a common scam script.

## "Never say" lines
- "Withdraw the maximum and invest it."
- "Leaving it in CPF is always better."
- "Your 55 is your retirement."

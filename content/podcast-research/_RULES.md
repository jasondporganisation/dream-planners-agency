# Shared rules for the Ep 47–82 research pack

## What this pack is
- Research notes for an AI script tool and the Brand Guardian compliance review. **Not scripts.**
- Show: *Quiet Compounding*, a calm, solo, 8–12 minute Singapore personal-finance podcast. General education only.
- Research cut-off: 3 Nov 2026. Figures change, often each January. **Check every figure on its source page again in the week of recording.**

## Fact handling
- Every fact gives a figure, the date it took effect or the period it covers, and an official source link.
- **VERIFIED** = seen on the linked official page during research (Nov 2026).
- **UNVERIFIED** = could not be confirmed on an official page. Do not say it as fact on air. Leave it out, or check it first.
- Worked examples use **illustrative numbers only**, which must be described on air as illustrative. They are not projections and not advice.

## Global "never say" list (applies to every episode)
- Any product name, insurer name, bank name, fund name or platform name.
- Any premium, price of a plan, projected return, interest "you will earn", or yield on an investment product.
- "Guaranteed", "risk-free", "safe bet", "can't lose", "best", "top", "number one", "must-have".
- "You should buy…", "sign up", "contact me", "DM me", "book a review", or any call to action to buy.
- Personal recommendations ("in your case you need X"). Use "many people find…", "one way to think about it…".
- Predictions about markets, interest rates or future policy.
- Treating a national scheme as a substitute for the listener getting advice on their own situation.
- Fear-based lines ("you'll be ruined", "your family will suffer").

## Standard on-air disclaimer (to be placed by the script tool)
"This episode is general information only. It does not consider your specific situation or needs. Figures are correct at the time of recording; please check the official sources linked in the show notes."

## Episode file template
1. Working title
2. Listener's real question (one line)
3. Key points (5–7, in order)
4. Verified Singapore facts (3–6): figure | effective date | source link | status
5. Worked example (illustrative numbers)
6. Common myths to correct
7. "Never say" lines (episode-specific, on top of the global list)

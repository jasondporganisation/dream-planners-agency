# Ep 63 — The Hidden Costs of Caregiving

**Theme:** Family and life stages
**Listener's real question:** "My mother needs more help at home. Beyond the obvious bills, what will caregiving cost us, and what support exists?"

## Key points (in order)
1. Caregiving costs are both visible (equipment, help at home, transport) and hidden (reduced work hours, lost income, your own health).
2. The Home Caregiving Grant pays up to $600 a month (from April 2026) for eligible care recipients who need help with 3 or more of 6 daily activities.
3. The Caregivers Training Grant gives $400, topped up by $200 a year, to help caregivers learn skills.
4. Hiring a domestic helper to care for an elderly family member can qualify for a concessionary levy of $60 a month instead of $300.
5. Assistive devices can be heavily subsidised through the Seniors' Mobility and Enabling Fund.
6. MediSave Care allows up to $200 a month for severely disabled people aged 30+, depending on MediSave balance.
7. Think about your own job early. Talk to your employer about flexibility before you are at breaking point.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
"Kumar" cares for his mother, who needs help with 3 daily activities. Their household income per person is an illustrative $2,000.
- Home Caregiving Grant: $400 a month (middle tier).
- If they hire a helper for her care, the levy may be $60 instead of $300 a month: about $2,880 a year less.
- Hidden cost: Kumar moves to four days a week, an illustrative $1,000 less pay a month. That is the biggest number in his plan, and it is easy to overlook.
*Say on air: "Illustrative. Eligibility is assessed by the agencies."*

## Common myths to correct
- "Caregiving is free if family does it." Lost income and health are real costs.
- "Grants are only for the poorest." HCG tiers go up to $4,800 household income per person.
- "I'll cope on my own." Training and respite support exist for a reason.

## "Never say" lines
- Naming care agencies, nursing homes or maid agencies.
- Comparing family members' caregiving.
- Fee figures for care services (not verified).

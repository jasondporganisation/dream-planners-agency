# Ep 65 — MediShield Life, Explained Simply

**Theme:** Protecting what you build
**Listener's real question:** "If I end up in hospital, what does MediShield Life actually pay, and what's left for me?"

## Key points (in order)
1. MediShield Life is the national basic health insurance. It covers all Singapore Citizens and PRs for life, including pre-existing conditions.
2. It is sized for subsidised bills in B2/C wards of public hospitals. In higher wards or private hospitals it covers a smaller share.
3. Three words to know: claim limits (the most it pays per item), the deductible (the first part you pay), and co-insurance (the share you pay after that).
4. From 1 Jun 2026: the policy-year claim limit is $200,000, there is no lifetime limit, and there is a new $500 outpatient deductible.
5. Deductibles depend on ward class and age (e.g. $2,000 in Class C for age next birthday 80 and below).
6. Co-insurance is tiered: 10% of the first $5,000, 5% of the next $5,000, and 3% above $10,000.
7. MOH data shows the median cash payable for common conditions in B2/C wards was $0 after subsidies, MediShield Life and MediSave (2023 bills). Ward choice drives out-of-pocket cost.

## Worked example (illustrative, simplified)
"Mr Tan", 50, has an illustrative $10,000 claimable bill in a Class C ward.
- Deductible: the first $2,000 is his to pay. It is commonly paid from MediSave within limits, but say "may be".
- Co-insurance: a tiered percentage share of the bill. On these round numbers it comes to hundreds of dollars, not thousands.
- MediShield Life pays the rest of the claimable amount.
The lesson is the structure (deductible, then a small percentage, then insurance pays), not the exact sum.
*Say on air: "Simplified. The exact split depends on the claim limits and how the tiers apply. CPF's booklet has worked examples."*

## Common myths to correct
- "MediShield Life pays everything." It is designed for B2/C-class bills, and you share costs through the deductible and co-insurance.
- "I'm not covered because I have a pre-existing condition." MediShield Life covers pre-existing conditions.
- "There's a lifetime cap that I'll run out of." There is no lifetime limit.
- "Private ward = same coverage." The share covered falls in higher wards.

## "Never say" lines
- Any premium figure, or "it's cheap / expensive".
- "You need a top-up plan" or any reference to specific private plans or insurers.
- "Hospital stays will cost you nothing."
- Post-April-2027 deductible numbers.

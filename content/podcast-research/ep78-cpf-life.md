# Ep 78 — CPF LIFE: Payouts for As Long As You Live

**Theme:** Retirement and CPF in depth
**Listener's real question:** "What's the difference between the CPF LIFE plans, and should I start payouts at 65 or wait?"

## Key points (in order)
1. CPF LIFE is the national scheme that pays you a monthly income for life, however long you live. It is built for longevity risk.
2. Citizens and PRs born in 1958 or later are included automatically if they have at least $60,000 in retirement savings when payouts start.
3. There are three plans. Standard gives level payouts. Escalating starts lower and grows 2% a year. Basic starts lower and payouts reduce once balances drop below $60,000.
4. You can start payouts any time from 65 to 70. If you have given no instruction, they start automatically at 70.
5. Each year of deferral raises payouts by up to 7%, so up to 35% if you start at 70.
6. Deferral is a trade-off. Bigger cheques later mean fewer years of cheques, so it fits people who have other income or are still working.
7. Born before 1958? You may be on payouts that stop when savings run out, with an option to join CPF LIFE before age 80.

## Worked example (illustrative, simplified arithmetic)
Use CPF's own example: about $1,780 a month from 65, or about $2,380 a month from 70.
- Start at 65: by 70 you have received about $1,780 × 60 = ~$106,800.
- Start at 70: you receive ~$600 more a month, so it takes about $106,800 ÷ $600 ≈ 178 months (~15 years) to "catch up", around age 85.
The lesson is not "which is better". It is that deferral suits people who expect to live long and do not need the income at 65.
*Say on air: "Very simplified. It ignores interest and changes in plans, and uses CPF's published estimates, which aren't promises."*

## Common myths to correct
- "CPF LIFE stops when my money runs out." Payouts continue for life.
- "If I die early, my family loses everything." CPF says any remaining CPF LIFE premium balance and remaining CPF savings "will be distributed to your loved ones" ([cpf.gov.sg: CPF LIFE](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/retirement-income/monthly-payouts/cpf-life), VERIFIED). How the amount is worked out is not covered here, so do not quote figures.
- "Deferring is always better." It depends on health, other income and needs.
- "I have to decide at 55." Payout start can be chosen between 65 and 70.

## "Never say" lines
- "Everyone should defer to 70."
- "The Escalating Plan is the best plan."
- "You'll get $X a month." Use "CPF estimates around…".
- "CPF LIFE beats buying an annuity / any product."

# Ep 57 — Buying Your First Flat Without Overstretching

**Theme:** Family and life stages
**Listener's real question:** "How much home can we really afford, and how do the loan limits and grants work?"

## Key points (in order)
1. Affordability starts with monthly repayments, not the price tag.
2. Rules set ceilings. The Mortgage Servicing Ratio (MSR) caps property loan repayments at 30% of gross monthly income for HDB flats (and ECs within MOP) financed by banks. The TDSR caps all debt repayments at 55%.
3. Loan-to-value limit: HDB loans are capped at 75% of the price (from the Oct 2024 BTO exercise), and bank loans are also 75%.
4. Grants: first-timer families may receive the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant of up to $120,000, which falls as household income rises.
5. CPF can pay for the home, but it reduces retirement savings until refunded on sale (Ep 80).
6. The ceiling is not a target. Leave room for children, career breaks and parents.
7. Keep your emergency fund separate from the down payment.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
"Ben and Aisha" have an illustrative combined gross income of $9,000 a month.
- 30% of $9,000 = $2,700: the MSR ceiling on monthly property loan repayments, where it applies.
- They choose to keep repayments nearer $2,000. That leaves room for a future child and for Aisha's planned study break.
- Their emergency fund stays separate from their down payment.
*Say on air: "Illustrative. Your eligibility, grants and loan amounts come from HDB and the lender."*

## Common myths to correct
- "If the bank or HDB approves it, we can afford it." Limits are ceilings, not comfort levels.
- "Using CPF is free money for housing." It reduces retirement savings until refunded.
- "Everyone gets $120,000 in grants." The amount depends on income and eligibility.

## "Never say" lines
- "Property always goes up" or "this is a good time to buy".
- Naming banks, loan packages or interest rates on bank loans.
- "Max out your loan: your salary will rise."

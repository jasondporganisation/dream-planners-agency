# Singapore facts: family, life stages, money habits, tax and housing

Checked 2026-11-03. Only official sites were used (*.gov.sg, plus aic.sg, the Agency for Integrated Care, which is a government agency). A fact is VERIFIED only if the figure appeared in page or PDF text fetched in this session. Quotes are copied word for word from the fetched text; "[...]" marks words left out.

**Read this first: three major 2026 changes**
- **Baby Bonus is being replaced.** The SG Child Support Package, announced at the National Day Rally (NDR) on 23 Aug 2026, will replace the Baby Bonus Scheme and the Large Families Scheme. Payouts start in April 2027. Rows 1a-1f are still the current rules until then. Rows 1g-1l are the new package.
- **Household income measure changed.** Key Household Income Trends 2025 (released Feb 2026) no longer leads with "income from work among employed households". Its headline is now "market income" across all resident households, so the 2025 figure cannot be compared directly with older "income from work" figures (row 7a).
- **Paid child-related leave.** At NDR 2026 the Government also said it will cover the cost of all child-related leave and merge Childcare Leave with Extended Childcare Leave. The new number of days is shown only in an image on the page, so it is UNVERIFIED (row 2e).

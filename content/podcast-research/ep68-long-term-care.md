# Ep 68 — Long-Term Care: The Costs Nobody Plans For

**Theme:** Protecting what you build
**Listener's real question:** "If my parent or I need long-term care one day, what help is there and what will it cost?"

## Key points (in order)
1. Long-term care means help with daily living over months or years, at home, in day care or in a nursing home. It is mostly not a hospital bill.
2. The national layer includes CareShield Life (or ElderShield for older cohorts), MediSave Care, and means-tested subsidies.
3. Caregivers at home: the Home Caregiving Grant pays up to $600 a month from April 2026, depending on household income.
4. Equipment: the Seniors' Mobility and Enabling Fund subsidises up to 90% of assistive devices for eligible seniors.
5. Long-term care service subsidies go up to 80% (residential) and 95% (non-residential) for eligible citizens born 1969 or earlier, from July 2026.
6. Family plans matter as much as money: who cares, where, and how the costs are shared.
7. Start the conversation before it is needed. Crisis decisions cost more, emotionally and financially.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
A family cares for "Ah Ma" at home. She needs help with 3 of 6 ADLs, and her household income per person is an illustrative $1,200.
- Home Caregiving Grant: $600 a month (2026 tier).
- If she had CareShield Life or ElderShield cover, a monthly payout would come on top.
- Against an illustrative $2,000 a month of care-related costs, the family plans to share the remaining ~$1,400 among three siblings.
*Say on air: "Illustrative. Eligibility is assessed case by case."*

## Common myths to correct
- "MediShield Life covers nursing homes." It covers hospital bills, not long-term care.
- "Grants are only for the very poor." The HCG goes up to $4,800 per-person household income.
- "It won't happen to our family." MOH estimates 1 in 2 residents could develop severe disability.
- "Care at home is free." There are real cash and time costs.

## "Never say" lines
- Specific nursing home fees or names.
- "Long-term care insurance is a must."
- Guilt-based framing ("good children always care at home").

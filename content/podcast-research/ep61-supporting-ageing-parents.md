# Ep 61 — Supporting Ageing Parents

**Theme:** Family and life stages
**Listener's real question:** "How much should I give my parents, and how do I help them without putting my own future at risk?"

## Key points (in order)
1. Supporting parents is part of many Singaporean families' values. Doing it sustainably helps everyone.
2. Start with a conversation. What do they have (CPF payouts, savings, property), what do they need, and what are they worried about?
3. Know the national support they may qualify for: CPF LIFE payouts, Silver Support for lower-income seniors, CHAS, and the Pioneer/Merdeka Generation benefits.
4. Tax reliefs recognise this support: Parent Relief of $9,000 if living with the parent, $5,500 if not (more for a parent with disability).
5. CPF top-ups to parents can raise their payouts, with tax relief or MRSS matching (Ep 79), but the money is locked into their CPF.
6. Encourage them to make an LPA and keep nominations up to date.
7. Agree on siblings' shares openly, and look after your own emergency fund and retirement too.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
"Sam" and his two sisters support their parents, who live with Sam.
- Parents' illustrative CPF payouts: $1,100 a month combined. Their estimated monthly costs: $2,200.
- The gap of $1,100 is shared $500 / $300 / $300, agreed openly.
- Sam may be eligible for Parent Relief at the "living with" rate, subject to IRAS conditions. Siblings must agree who claims, since relief for the same parent is shared.
*Say on air: "Illustrative. Relief claims depend on IRAS conditions."*

## Common myths to correct
- "Good children give until it hurts." Sustainable support lasts longer.
- "My parents don't qualify for anything." Check the schemes. Many are means-tested, not only for the very poor.
- "Topping up their CPF is like giving cash." It is locked into their retirement payouts.

## "Never say" lines
- A "right" amount or percentage to give parents.
- Judgement on families who can't give much.
- Product names (insurance for parents, etc.).

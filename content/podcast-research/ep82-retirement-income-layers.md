# Ep 82 — Putting the Retirement Pieces Together

**Theme:** Retirement and CPF in depth
**Listener's real question:** "CPF, SRS, my own savings, government support: how do these fit into one retirement picture?"

## Key points (in order)
1. Think in layers. There is a base that lasts for life (CPF LIFE), a flexible layer (cash and savings), and safety nets for those who need them.
2. CPF LIFE gives monthly income for life. Its size depends on your RA savings and plan.
3. The Supplementary Retirement Scheme (SRS) is a voluntary scheme with tax relief on contributions and tax rules on withdrawal (see facts).
4. Safety nets, such as Silver Support for lower-income seniors, top up the base. They are not designed to be a plan.
5. Healthcare needs a separate line in the picture (MediSave, Basic Healthcare Sum, MediShield Life).
6. Start from spending. Estimate monthly needs, then see which layer covers which part.
7. Review every few years. Plans, rules and needs all change.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
"Kok Wai" estimates illustrative spending of $2,800 a month at 65.
- Layer 1, CPF LIFE (illustrative): about $1,800 a month.
- Gap: about $1,000 a month, which he plans to meet from savings and part-time work for the first few years.
- Healthcare: kept as a separate line, with MediSave and national insurance schemes.
The point is the method: start with needs, then layer the sources.
*Say on air: "All numbers are made up to show the method. They're not a recommendation or a projection."*

## Common myths to correct
- "CPF alone will cover everything." It is a base, not necessarily the whole picture.
- "Government support means I don't need to plan." Safety nets have strict criteria and modest amounts.
- "SRS is tax-free." Contributions get relief, and withdrawals are partly taxable.
- "Retirement planning starts at 55." Earlier habits compound more quietly.

## "Never say" lines
- "You need $1 million to retire."
- "Put your gap into [any product]."
- "This is the best way to plan retirement."
- "Silver Support will cover your gap."

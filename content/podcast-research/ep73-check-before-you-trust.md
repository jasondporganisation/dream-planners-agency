# Ep 73 — Check Before You Trust

**Theme:** Protecting what you build
**Listener's real question:** "How do I check whether a financial company or person is legitimate before I hand over money?"

## Key points (in order)
1. Before any financial dealing, check that the firm is regulated. The MAS Financial Institutions Directory lists regulated entities.
2. Check the individual too. The Financial Institution Representatives Register shows whether a person can give financial advice, and for which activities.
3. The MAS Investor Alert List names unregulated persons who may have been wrongly perceived as MAS-regulated. Not being on it does not mean an entity is safe.
4. Deposits with banks and finance companies that are Scheme members are insured up to S$100,000 per depositor per member (from 1 Apr 2024). Foreign-currency and structured deposits are not covered.
5. For new life insurance policies there is a free-look period of 14 days after receiving the documents, during which you can cancel.
6. MAS Fair Dealing Guidelines set out five outcomes consumers should expect, including suitable advice and clear information.
7. The habit: verify first, take your time, and keep documents.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers only)
"Hui Min" has an illustrative S$140,000 in Singapore-dollar deposits at one bank that is a Scheme member.
- Deposit insurance covers up to S$100,000 per depositor per Scheme member, so ~S$40,000 is above the insured limit at that bank.
- Separately, a stranger online offers her "exclusive" investment returns. She checks the Directory and the Representatives Register and finds neither the firm nor the person. She walks away.
*Say on air: "Illustrative. Coverage details depend on the deposit type. Check the SDIC and MAS pages."*

## Common myths to correct
- "If it has a slick website and an office, it's licensed." Check the official directory.
- "Not on the Investor Alert List means it's safe." The list is not exhaustive.
- "All my bank money is insured." There is a per-depositor per-member limit, and certain deposits are excluded.
- "Once I sign, I can't change my mind." Free-look periods apply to many policies.

## "Never say" lines
- "Keep money with [bank] because it's safer."
- "Licensed means no risk."
- Any claim about specific firms' standing.

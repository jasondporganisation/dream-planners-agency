# Quiet Compounding: research pack (Ep 47–82)

This is the fact base for the AI script tool and for the Brand Guardian compliance review. It is general education only. It contains no scripts, no product or insurer names, no premiums or returns, and no guarantees.

- `INDEX.md`: the 36 episodes, grouped into 4 themes of 9.
- `_RULES.md`: the global "never say" list, the fact-handling rules and the episode template.
- `ep47-…md` to `ep82-…md`: one research file per episode.
- `_fact-register/`: the audit trail. It lists each checked fact with the exact official URL fetched and a short verbatim quote, collected on 3 Nov 2026.

**Status labels.** VERIFIED means the figure was seen on the linked official page on 3 Nov 2026. UNVERIFIED means it could not be confirmed on an official page, so do not say it on air as fact.

**Before recording, check the figures again.** CPF interest rates change every quarter. Many other figures change each January, and family support changes in April 2027.

**Sources outside the core list.** A few facts come from other official Singapore sites: mom.gov.sg, msf.gov.sg, police.gov.sg, mha.gov.sg, sso.agc.gov.sg, madeforfamilies.gov.sg, life.gov.sg and togetherforbetter.gov.sg. Each table row says where a fact comes from.

**publicguardian.gov.sg.** This site could not be reached from the research environment. LPA facts come from the Office of the Public Guardian's pages on msf.gov.sg instead.

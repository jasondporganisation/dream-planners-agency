# Ep 67 — Social media without regret

**Track:** The Agency Blueprint (AB)
**Theme:** 3 — Compliance, ethics and hard conversations

**The adviser's real question:** "I want to post useful content online. How do I do it without getting myself or my FI into trouble?"

## Key points (in order)
1. From 25 March 2026, MAS's Guidelines on Standards of Conduct for Digital Advertising Activities apply. They cover FIs *and* their "digital marketers", and that includes representatives.
2. The FI is responsible for oversight. Expect your FI to have rules on which platforms you may use, approval steps, account registration and monitoring. Learn them before you post.
3. Each post must stand on its own. A post can't rely on a link, a comment or another slide to fix something misleading.
4. Re-posting or sharing counts as advertising. That includes testimonials and other people's content.
5. Format limits are no excuse. Character limits, carousels and short videos still need clear, visible disclosures.
6. MAS has seen deceptive tactics such as catfishing used to get leads. Under these guidelines, how you behave online is part of conduct, and it can bear on whether you are fit and proper.
7. Keep a simple log of what you posted, where, and when it was approved. If a complaint ever comes, your FI needs to find the post.

## Hypothetical example
Picture an adviser who loves making short explainer videos. She used to post first and ask later. Now she keeps a one-page log: date, platform, topic and approval reference from her FI. One evening, she is about to share a friend's glowing post about her service. She pauses, because sharing counts as advertising, and sends it to compliance first. It takes a day longer. She sleeps better.

## Your action this week
Find your FI's current digital advertising / social media policy (it may have been updated for March 2026). Read it once in full and list the platforms you're allowed to use. Check that every account you post from is registered the way your FI requires.

## "Never say" lines (episode-specific)
- "It's my personal page, so the rules don't apply."
- "Just put the disclaimer in the comments / in the link in bio."
- "Sharing isn't the same as posting."
- "Everyone's doing it on [platform]." Name no platforms.
- Don't call the guideline "FSG-G03". The MAS document says FSG-03.
- No examples of real posts, accounts or creators.

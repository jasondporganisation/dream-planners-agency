# Ep 47 — A prospecting system that doesn't lean on friends and family

**Track:** The Agency Blueprint (AB)
**Theme:** 1 — A pipeline without pressure
**The adviser's real question:** "My warm list is running out. How do I build a steady flow of new conversations without leaning on people I love?"

## Key points (in order)
1. A warm list is a starting point, not a system. If it is the only source, it runs out, and every approach to it carries personal weight.
2. A system is a few channels you can repeat each week: people who already know your work, community and interest groups you genuinely belong to, talks or workshops, and content that helps people. Pick two or three and do them steadily.
3. Know the rules for each channel before you use it. MAS is keeping the ban on street canvassing and door-to-door prospecting. At public places, MAS will require you to tell people upfront that you want to market to them, and to get their consent before you start.
4. Phone and text outreach to Singapore numbers comes with Do Not Call duties under the PDPA: check the register unless you have clear consent, say who you are, and don't hide your number.
5. Lead with usefulness. The aim of a first touch is a conversation the other person is glad to have, not a sale.
6. Track the system, not just the outcomes (see Ep 51). Count conversations started, not only meetings closed.
7. Your agency's own rules sit on top of MAS and PDPC rules. Check with your compliance team before trying a new channel.

## Hypothetical example
Picture an adviser who has spoken to almost everyone in her phone in her first year. She feels the pull to go round again. Instead she picks two channels she can sustain. One is a monthly small-group talk on a money-and-life topic for a hobby community she already belongs to. People sign up knowing an adviser is speaking. The other is a short weekly note to people who have said yes to hearing from her. She blocks two hours a week for each and counts conversations started, not sales. After three months the pipeline is slower than she hoped, but steadier, and nobody in her family has had a second awkward call.

## Your action this week
List every place your new conversations came from in the last three months. Circle the two sources you could repeat every week without discomfort, and put one recurring block for each in your calendar. Before you use any new channel, ask your compliance team what applies.

## "Never say" lines (episode-specific)
- "Just approach people at the MRT / void deck / their door." (Street canvassing and door-to-door prospecting are prohibited.)
- "You don't need to say you're an adviser until they're interested."
- "Buy a list of numbers and start calling."
- "Everyone you know is a prospect."
- "This system will fill your diary" or any promise of results.

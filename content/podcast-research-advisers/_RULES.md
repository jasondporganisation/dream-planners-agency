# Shared rules for the adviser-facing research pack (Ep 47–82)

## What this pack is
- Research notes for the AI script tool and the Brand Guardian compliance review. **Not scripts. Nothing here is published.**
- Show: *Quiet Compounding*, a podcast **for financial advisory professionals in Singapore** about running the practice and the person behind it.
- Two tracks, alternating: odd episodes = **The Agency Blueprint** (systems, numbers, structure); even episodes = **The Resilient Advisor** (the person behind the practice).
- This pack replaces nothing. The earlier consumer pack in `content/podcast-research/` is a separate pack and is left as it is.
- Research cut-off: 5 Nov 2026. Regulatory references change. **Check every source again in the week of recording.**

## Fact handling
- Every fact gives: the claim, the date or period, a source link, and a status.
- **VERIFIED** = seen on the linked page (official body, peer-reviewed journal record, or reputable research publisher) during research, Nov 2026.
- **UNVERIFIED** = could not be confirmed on the source page. Do not say it as fact on air. Leave it out, or check it first.
- Research findings are described as what a study found, in the setting it was done in. Do not stretch a study into "advisers always…".
- No "many/most advisers…" claim unless a source in the file says so.

## Global "never say" list (applies to every episode)
- Any insurer, bank, fund, platform or product name. Any product type.
- Any income, earnings, commission, or "you can make $X" claim, including about recruits or "top producers".
- "Guaranteed", "risk-free", "can't fail", "always works", "best", "number one".
- "Many/most advisers…" without a source.
- Anything that sounds like legal or compliance sign-off. Say "check with your compliance team" instead.
- Scripts or tactics to pressure friends, family or prospects.
- Named real advisers, clients or colleagues. Examples are hypothetical only.

## Hypothetical examples
- Always framed as "picture an adviser who…". Made-up people, no numbers about income.

## Episode file template
1. Working title, track, theme
2. The adviser's real question (one line)
3. Key points (5–7, in order)
4. Sourced facts (3–6): claim | date | source link | status
5. Hypothetical example ("picture an adviser who…")
6. Your action this week
7. "Never say" lines (episode-specific, on top of the global list)

# Ep 61 — Money habits of commission earners

**Track:** The Agency Blueprint (AB)
**Theme:** 2 — The working week and your energy

**The adviser's real question:** "Some months are great and some are thin. How do I stop my own money swinging with my commissions?"

> **General information only.** This episode is about personal habits, not advice on any financial product. It is not tax advice.

## Key points (in order)
1. Irregular income is normal for the self-employed. It makes ordinary bills feel stressful even in a good year.
2. Budget on the income you can count on. MAS's MoneySense suggests not counting money you're not sure you'll earn, such as bonuses and commissions.
3. Build a cash buffer first. The usual rule of thumb is 3 to 6 months of expenses. MAS's FAQ notes that someone with unpredictable income may want a bigger buffer for lull periods.
4. Pay yourself a steady amount. Let commissions land in one place, then move a fixed "salary" to your spending account each month. Good months top up the buffer; thin months draw on it.
5. Set money aside for tax as it comes in. Tax on self-employment income is worked out after you file your return, so the bill arrives months after the income. IRAS lets you pay by GIRO in monthly instalments (up to 12 months).
6. Remember MediSave contributions too if your net trade income is above the threshold (see Ep 63).
7. Review it monthly, in your weekly review or a monthly "money hour". Small, boring habits beat big resolutions.

## Hypothetical example
Picture an adviser whose income comes in waves. In a strong month he used to upgrade things; in a quiet month he worried about rent. Now all commission goes into one "income" account. On the 1st of every month he moves the same fixed amount to his spending account, puts a set share aside for tax and MediSave, and leaves the rest as a buffer. Quiet months are still quiet, but they no longer feel like a crisis.

## Your action this week
List your fixed monthly expenses and add them up. That number is your "steady pay" target. Then set one standing instruction: on the same date each month, move that amount from where your commissions land to where you spend.

## "Never say" lines
- Any financial product or product type, and any "put your buffer into…" suggestion.
- "You'll never have a bad month if you do this."
- "Advisers earn $X" / "a good month is $X" or any income figure.
- "Set aside exactly X% for tax." (Depends on the person; say "check your own situation or speak to a tax professional".)
- "Most advisers are bad with money." (No source.)
- "12 months of expenses is the official rule for commission earners." (Not verified.)

# Ep 60 — Sleep, boundaries and after-hours messages

**Track:** The Resilient Advisor (RA)
**Theme:** 2 — The working week and your energy

**The adviser's real question:** "Clients message me at 11pm and I feel I have to reply. How do I protect my sleep without letting people down?"

> **Not medical advice.** If sleep problems or stress go on for weeks, see a doctor. For emotional support, call **National Mindline 1771** (24/7) or **SOS 1767** (24/7).

## Key points (in order)
1. Sleep matters for judgement, not just energy. In one set of studies, less sleep was linked to lower self-control and more unethical behaviour at work.
2. For a profession built on trust and careful advice, that is worth taking seriously.
3. The urge to reply right away has a name: "workplace telepressure". In research, higher telepressure went with more burnout, poorer sleep quality and more absence.
4. Singapore's Health Promotion Board suggests adults aim for about 7 hours of sleep and avoid phones and screens for at least an hour before bed.
5. IRAS treats commission agents as self-employed. If that's you, employee rules or company policies on after-hours contact may not apply, so boundaries are mostly yours to set. Check your own contract and your firm's guidance.
6. Set expectations up front: tell clients your reply hours and what to do in a real emergency. Then keep to it.
7. Use simple tools: an auto-reply after hours, scheduled sending, "do not disturb", the phone outside the bedroom.

## Hypothetical example
Picture an adviser who keeps her phone on the pillow so she never misses a client. She often replies after midnight and wakes up foggy before morning meetings. She adds a line to her welcome message: "I reply to messages between 9am and 8pm. For anything urgent, here is who to contact." She sets an auto-reply for late evenings and charges her phone in the living room. A few clients test it. Most simply wait until morning.

## Your action this week
Choose your "last reply" time. Set an after-hours auto-reply that says when you'll respond and where to go for urgent help. Check the wording with your compliance team before you switch it on.

## "Never say" lines
- "Lack of sleep makes advisers dishonest" or "tired advisers mis-sell." (Overstates the study.)
- "You need exactly 8 hours." (HPB says about 7 for adults.)
- "The law gives you the right to disconnect." (Not verified; and as self-employed persons, employee rules may not apply.)
- "Never answer clients after hours." (Say: set clear hours and an urgent route.)
- Any sleep-aid, supplement or medication suggestion.

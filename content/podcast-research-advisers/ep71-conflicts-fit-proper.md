# Ep 71 — Conflicts of interest and being fit and proper

**Track:** The Agency Blueprint (AB)
**Theme:** 3 — Compliance, ethics and hard conversations

**The adviser's real question:** "What does 'fit and proper' really mean for me day to day, and how do I handle conflicts I can't avoid?"

## Key points (in order)
1. "Fit and proper" is not a one-time test at appointment. MAS expects you to stay competent, honest, of good integrity and financially sound.
2. MAS's guidelines group the criteria into three areas: honesty, integrity and reputation; competence and capability; and financial soundness.
3. The onus is on the person to show they are fit and proper, not on MAS to prove otherwise. Openness with your FI and MAS is part of it. Being untruthful or uncooperative with MAS is itself a factor.
4. Conflicts of interest are normal in any job with incentives. The test is whether you spot them, disclose them as your FI requires, and keep them from skewing advice.
5. MAS Notice FAA-N03 sets out disclosure to clients, including how the adviser is paid and conflicts of interest. Your FI's disclosure forms are built on this.
6. The public can look you up on MAS's Financial Institution Representatives Register. Know what yours shows, and tell clients how to check it.
7. Coming up: from 1 January 2027, a new MAS notice on reporting representatives' misconduct (FAA-N27) takes effect. Ask your FI what changes for you.

## Hypothetical example
Picture an agency leader whose team member mentions, almost in passing, that he's started a side business with a client's relative. Nothing has gone wrong. The leader doesn't panic or lecture. He asks, "Have you told compliance?" Then he walks him through the FI's declaration process that week. The conflict is now on record and managed. Six months later, when a question comes up, nobody has to dig.

## Your action this week
Search your own name on the MAS Representatives Register and check that the details are what you expect. Then look at your FI's conflicts and outside-interest declaration. Is anything in your life that should be on it not on it yet?

## "Never say" lines (episode-specific)
- "Fit and proper is just an exam you pass once."
- "What MAS doesn't know won't hurt."
- "Conflicts only matter if the client finds out."
- "Being on the register means MAS has approved you." It shows the FI certified the person. Listed people are not MAS employees.
- Don't quote FAA-N27 deadlines or penalties (not verified).
- Don't use any real enforcement case or named person.

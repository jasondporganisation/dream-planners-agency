# R28 — Three words that protect your money: Add, Check, Tell

**Life stage:** All ages · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Over nine hundred million dollars, lost to scams."

## Voiceover
Over nine hundred million dollars, lost to scams.

According to the Singapore Police Force, scam losses were about nine hundred and thirteen million dollars in 2025, across almost forty-two thousand cases. That's lower than 2024, but still huge. So let's keep it simple with three words: Add, Check, Tell.

Add. Add the ScamShield app, and turn on your bank's security features, like transaction limits and two-factor authentication.

Check. Before you act on a call, message or offer, check with official sources. Call the ScamShield Helpline at one seven nine nine, any time, day or night.

Tell. Tell the authorities, your family and your friends. If you think money has gone out wrongly, call your bank immediately.

Scammers count on you rushing. Slowing down is your superpower.

Share this with someone you care about, especially your parents.

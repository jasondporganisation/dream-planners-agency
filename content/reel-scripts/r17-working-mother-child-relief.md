# R17 — Working mums: this tax relief changed in 2024

**Life stage:** First child · **Target length:** ~50 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Working mums, this tax relief now works differently."

## Voiceover
Working mums, this tax relief now works differently.

It's called the Working Mother's Child Relief. It used to be a percentage of your earned income. But for Singapore Citizen children born or adopted on or after first January 2024, it's now a fixed dollar amount.

Here's how it works, from Year of Assessment 2025. For your first child, eight thousand dollars. Second child, ten thousand. Third child and beyond, twelve thousand dollars each.

Remember, a relief reduces your chargeable income. It's not cash back. And all your personal reliefs together are capped at eighty thousand dollars a year.

If your children were born before 2024, different rules apply, so check the IRAS page for your situation.

New mums, it's worth spending ten minutes on this when you file your taxes next April.

Know a new mum? Share this with her.

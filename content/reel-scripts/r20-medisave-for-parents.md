# R20 — Can your MediSave pay for your parents' hospital bill?

**Life stage:** Caring for parents · **Target length:** ~50 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Your parent is in hospital. Can your MediSave help?"

## Voiceover
Your parent is in hospital. Can your MediSave help?

Many people don't know this, but yes, it can. According to the CPF Board, hospitalisation bills can be paid with your own MediSave, or with the MediSave of immediate family members.

Approved family members include your spouse, parents, grandparents, siblings and children. For grandparents and siblings, they must be Singapore Citizens or PRs.

A few things to keep in mind. Withdrawal limits still apply, so MediSave may not cover the whole bill. And every dollar you use for a family member is a dollar less for your own healthcare later. So it's worth talking as a family about how you'll share costs.

Good to know before you're standing at the hospital counter.

Save this, and share it with your siblings.

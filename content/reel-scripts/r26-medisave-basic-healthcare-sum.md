# R26 — Your MediSave has a ceiling. Here's what happens at the top

**Life stage:** Medical costs · **Target length:** ~50 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Your MediSave can't grow forever."

## Voiceover
Your MediSave can't grow forever.

There's a cap called the Basic Healthcare Sum. It's the amount the CPF Board estimates you'll need in MediSave for basic healthcare in old age. For members below sixty-five, it's seventy-nine thousand dollars in 2026, up from seventy-five thousand five hundred in 2025.

So what happens when your MediSave reaches the cap? Any MediSave contributions above it are automatically transferred to your other CPF accounts. Nothing is lost. It's just redirected.

Two takeaways. First, if you're far from the cap, MediSave is your built-in healthcare buffer, so think carefully before using it on non-urgent costs. Second, the sum usually goes up over time, partly because healthcare costs rise.

Do you know how much is in your MediSave right now? You can check it in a minute on the CPF website or app.

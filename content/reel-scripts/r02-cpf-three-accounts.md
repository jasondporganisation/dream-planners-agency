# R02 — CPF's three accounts, in under a minute

**Life stage:** First job · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Your CPF isn't one pot. It's three."

## Voiceover
Your CPF isn't one pot. It's three.

First, the Ordinary Account, or OA. This is the one many people use for housing. It can also go towards things like education. It earns two point five per cent interest a year.

Second, the Special Account, or SA. This one is for retirement. Third, the MediSave Account, or MA, for healthcare costs like hospital bills.

The SA and MA currently earn four per cent a year. The Government has extended this four per cent floor until thirty-first December 2027.

There's also extra interest. You earn an extra one per cent on the first sixty thousand dollars of your combined balances, with up to twenty thousand of that counted from your OA.

One more change to know. Since 2025, when you turn fifty-five, your SA is closed and its savings move to your Retirement Account, up to your Full Retirement Sum.

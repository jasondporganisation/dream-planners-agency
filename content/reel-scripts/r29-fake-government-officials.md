# R29 — "This is the government calling." Is it?

**Life stage:** All ages · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Real government officers will never ask you this."

## Voiceover
Real government officers will never ask you this.

Government official impersonation was one of the top scam types by money lost in Singapore in 2025, according to the police. The caller sounds official. They may say you're under investigation. Then comes the pressure.

So remember what the Singapore Police Force says. Government officials will never ask you to transfer or hand over money, gold, luxury watches or cryptocurrency. They will never ask for your banking login details. They will never ask you to install apps from unofficial app stores. And they will never transfer your call to the police.

For CPF, the Board says its calls come only from six two two seven, one one eight eight, and its SMS messages always come from "gov dot S G".

If a call scares you, hang up. Then check through official channels, or call the ScamShield Helpline at one seven nine nine.

Please share this with your parents.

# R19 — Topping up your parents' CPF: what to know

**Life stage:** Caring for parents · **Target length:** ~60 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)

## Voiceover
Want to boost your parents' retirement savings? Here's how it works.

You can make a cash top-up to your loved ones' CPF retirement savings. And you may get tax relief for it. For yourself, top-ups can get you up to eight thousand dollars of relief a year. For loved ones, like your parents, parents-in-law, grandparents, spouse or siblings, there's up to another eight thousand dollars a year.

There's also a government scheme called the Matched Retirement Savings Scheme. For eligible Singapore Citizens aged fifty-five and above with lower retirement savings, the Government matches cash top-ups dollar for dollar, up to two thousand dollars a year, with a lifetime limit of twenty thousand dollars. There are conditions on income, property and savings.

One catch: top-ups that receive this matching grant don't get tax relief.

And remember, money put into CPF for retirement can't simply be taken out again whenever you like.

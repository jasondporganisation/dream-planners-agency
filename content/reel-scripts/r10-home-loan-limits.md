# R10 — How much home loan can you actually take?

**Life stage:** BTO / home loan · **Target length:** ~60 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Your dream flat versus the loan you can get."

## Voiceover
Your dream flat versus the loan you can get.

In Singapore, there are rules that cap how much you can borrow. Here are three to know.

One: loan-to-value. For HDB flats, an HDB housing loan can cover up to seventy-five per cent of the price or valuation, whichever is lower. Bank loans are also generally capped at seventy-five per cent if you have no other housing loan. The rest comes from your CPF and cash.

Two: the Mortgage Servicing Ratio, for HDB flats and some executive condominiums. Your monthly home loan instalment can't be more than thirty per cent of your gross monthly income.

Three: the Total Debt Servicing Ratio, for loans from banks and other financial institutions. All your monthly debt repayments together, including car loans and credit cards, must be fifty-five per cent of income or less.

For context, the HDB loan interest rate is two point six per cent a year as of late 2026, and it's reviewed every quarter.

Plan with these limits first, then go flat hunting.

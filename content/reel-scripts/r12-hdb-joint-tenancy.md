# R12 — Joint tenancy or tenancy-in-common? It matters later

## Voiceover
Two names on the flat. But how they're written matters.

When you buy an HDB flat with someone, you choose how you hold it. There are two ways.

Joint tenancy. You both own the whole flat together. If one owner passes away, their share goes automatically to the surviving owner. The surviving owner then lodges a Notice of Death with the Singapore Land Authority.

Tenancy-in-common. Each of you owns a specific share, like fifty-fifty, or seventy-thirty. If one owner passes away, their share is distributed according to their will. If there's no will, it follows the Intestate Succession Act. So the share might not go to the person you expect.

Neither option is right for everyone. It depends on your relationship, your family and your plans. But it's a decision many couples rush through on signing day.

Do you know how your flat is held? Check your documents this week.

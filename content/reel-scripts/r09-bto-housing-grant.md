# R09 — First home? Check this grant before you count your savings

**Life stage:** BTO / home loan · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Buying your first flat? Don't miss this grant."

## Voiceover
Buying your first flat? Don't miss this grant.

It's called the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant, for families. It's for eligible first-timer households buying a new or resale HDB flat.

How much you get depends on your average monthly household income. Households earning fifteen hundred dollars or less can get up to one hundred and twenty thousand dollars. The grant tapers as income goes up, down to five thousand dollars for households earning between eight thousand five hundred and one dollars and nine thousand dollars. Above nine thousand, this grant doesn't apply.

If one of you is a first-timer and the other has bought before, the maximum is different, at up to sixty thousand dollars.

The grant goes into your CPF and is used for your flat purchase. When you sell the flat later, it's refunded to your CPF with accrued interest.

Check your eligibility on the HDB Flat Portal before you plan your budget.

## Voiceover
Heard of SRS? Many people haven't.

SRS stands for the Supplementary Retirement Scheme. It's a voluntary government scheme to help you save for retirement, on top of CPF.

Here's how it works. Every dollar you put in can get you tax relief, which is given in the following Year of Assessment. The annual cap is fifteen thousand three hundred dollars for Singapore Citizens and PRs, and thirty-five thousand seven hundred dollars for foreigners. This relief counts towards the eighty-thousand-dollar overall cap on personal reliefs.

Now the trade-off. From the statutory retirement age, which is sixty-four from first July 2026, only fifty per cent of what you withdraw is taxable. But withdraw earlier, and it's fully taxable, plus a five per cent penalty, except in some specific cases.

So SRS works best for money you won't need until retirement. Whether it suits you depends on your tax bracket and plans.

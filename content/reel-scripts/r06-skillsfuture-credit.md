# R06 — Unused SkillsFuture Credit? Check yours today

**Life stage:** Career growth · **Target length:** ~50 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"You might have money for courses you've never touched."

## Voiceover
You might have money for courses you've never touched.

It's called SkillsFuture Credit. Every Singapore Citizen aged twenty-five and above gets an opening credit of five hundred dollars, which you can use on eligible courses.

And if you're forty and above, there's more. There's a separate four-thousand-dollar SkillsFuture Credit, called the Mid-Career credit, for selected courses that support a career switch or a step up in your field.

Why does this matter for your money? Skills are part of your earning power. And your earning power is the engine behind every other goal: your home, your family, your retirement.

So here's a five-minute task. Log in to the MySkillsFuture portal, check your balance, and browse one course in an area you're curious about. No need to sign up today. Just look.

What skill would you learn if money weren't the issue? Tell me in the comments.

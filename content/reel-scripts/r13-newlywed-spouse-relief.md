# R13 — Just married? Two money admin tasks people forget

**Life stage:** Marriage · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Newly married? Your tax form may have changed too."

## Voiceover
Newly married? Your tax form may have changed too.

Here's one many couples don't know about: Spouse Relief. If your spouse earned eight thousand dollars or less in the previous year, you may be able to claim a relief of two thousand dollars on your income tax. The income limit was raised from four thousand to eight thousand dollars from Year of Assessment 2025. Other conditions apply, so check with IRAS.

Who does this help? Maybe a couple where one partner is studying, caring for family, or between jobs.

Second task: talk about how you'll share household costs. MoneySense suggests one approach is to contribute in proportion to your incomes, so it feels fair to both of you. There's no single right way. What matters is that you agree.

Wedding admin is a lot. But these two conversations can save stress later.

Couples, how do you split bills? Share in the comments.

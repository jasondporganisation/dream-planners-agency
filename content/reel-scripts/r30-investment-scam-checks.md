# R30 — "Can't lose" investment tips: three checks first

**Life stage:** All ages · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"The biggest scam losses in Singapore come from this."

## Voiceover
The biggest scam losses in Singapore come from this.

In 2025, investment scams caused the most money lost of any scam type, according to the Singapore Police Force. They often start in a chat group, or with a friendly stranger online. Here are three checks before you put in a single dollar.

Check one: is it regulated? Search the MAS Financial Institutions Directory to see if the company is licensed. Then look at the MAS Investor Alert List. It names entities that may be wrongly seen as licensed by MAS.

Check two: does it promise big money with no risk? MoneySense warns to be wary of offers that claim to protect your capital while promising high gains. Bigger promised gains usually mean bigger risk.

Check three: is someone rushing you? "Limited time only." "Bring in friends for a bonus." Those are red flags.

If anything feels off, walk away and call the ScamShield Helpline at one seven nine nine.

Save this before you need it.

# R08 — Going freelance or doing gig work? CPF still matters

**Life stage:** Career switch · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"No boss means no employer CPF. Then what?"

## Voiceover
No boss means no employer CPF. Then what?

If you're switching to freelancing, here's what changes. As a self-employed person, there's no employer putting in that seventeen per cent anymore. But CPF doesn't disappear completely.

If your annual net trade income is more than six thousand dollars, you're required to contribute to your MediSave Account. That's your healthcare savings.

If you do platform work, like ride-hailing or delivery, there's another change. Under the Platform Workers Act, from first January 2025, platform operators deduct and pay CPF contributions for platform workers. It's mandatory if you were born on or after first January 1995. If you're older, you can choose to opt in.

The bigger point? Your OA and retirement savings may grow slower when you're self-employed. So it's worth planning for your own housing and retirement savings on purpose.

# R01 — Your first payslip: where did 20% go?

**Life stage:** First job · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Your salary is five thousand. So why did you get four thousand?"

## Voiceover
Your salary is five thousand. So why did you get four thousand?

Welcome to your first payslip, and to CPF. If you're a Singapore Citizen employee, or a PR from your third year, aged fifty-five and below, earning above seven hundred and fifty dollars a month, twenty per cent of your wages goes into your CPF. Your employer adds another seventeen per cent on top. That's thirty-seven per cent in total.

So in this illustration, on a five-thousand-dollar monthly wage, a thousand comes from you, and eight hundred and fifty comes from your boss.

There are limits. From first January 2026, CPF contributions are calculated on ordinary wages of up to eight thousand dollars a month, and there's an annual wage ceiling of one hundred and two thousand dollars.

That money isn't gone. It's still yours, set aside for housing, healthcare and retirement.

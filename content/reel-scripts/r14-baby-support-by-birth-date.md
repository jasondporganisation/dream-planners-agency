# R14 — Baby on the way? Government support depends on the birth date

**Life stage:** First child · **Target length:** ~65 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Expecting a baby? The support changes on one date."

## Voiceover
Expecting a baby? The support changes on one date.

For Singapore Citizen babies born on or after fourteenth February 2023, the Baby Bonus gives a Cash Gift of eleven thousand dollars for the first and second child. There's also a Child Development Account, or CDA, with a First Step Grant of five thousand dollars, and the Government matches what you save in it, up to a cap.

But a new SG Child Support Package was announced at the National Day Rally 2026. For children born on or after first April 2027, there's a ten-thousand-dollar Baby Gift, a five-thousand-dollar First Step Grant in the CDA, and up to five thousand dollars of co-matching. There are also Child Credits of two thousand dollars a year from age one to sixteen.

Separately, MOH gives a MediSave Grant for Newborns of five thousand dollars for babies born after first April 2025.

Transition rules apply, so check LifeSG for your baby's due date.

Expecting? Tell me your due month in the comments.

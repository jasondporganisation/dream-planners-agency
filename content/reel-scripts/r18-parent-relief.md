# R18 — Supporting your parents? IRAS may recognise that

**Life stage:** Caring for parents · **Target length:** ~50 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"You give your parents an allowance. Did you know about this?"

## Voiceover
You give your parents an allowance. Did you know about this?

If you support your parents, you may be able to claim Parent Relief on your income tax.

For Year of Assessment 2026, it's nine thousand dollars per parent if they live with you, and five thousand five hundred dollars per parent if they don't.

The basic conditions: your parent must be fifty-five or older, and their income in the previous year must not be more than eight thousand dollars. That income limit was raised from four thousand dollars from Year of Assessment 2025. So some families who didn't qualify before may qualify now.

There are other conditions, and siblings who support the same parent may need to share the claim. So check the IRAS page before you file.

It's a small way the system recognises what so many of us already do.

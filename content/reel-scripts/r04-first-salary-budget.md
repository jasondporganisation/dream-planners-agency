# R04 — Your first salary: give every dollar a job

**Life stage:** First job · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"First pay day. Where does it all go?"

## Voiceover
First pay day. Where does it all go?

Getting your first salary feels amazing. Then, somehow, the month ends before the money does. Here's a simple way to start.

One: track before you cut. For one month, write down everything you spend. Use an app, or just your phone's notes. You can't fix what you can't see.

Two: save first, spend later. MoneySense, the national financial education programme, suggests aiming to save at least twenty per cent of your monthly take-home pay. If that feels like a stretch on your first pay, start smaller and build up.

Three: set it and forget it. On pay day, move your savings to a separate account before you start spending.

Four: check your fixed costs. Phone plan, subscriptions, transport. Small monthly items add up quietly.

No perfect system needed. Just start this month.

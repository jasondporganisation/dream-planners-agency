# Reel Script Bank — @jason_thedreamcaptain

30 general-education Instagram reel scripts about everyday Singapore money moments for people aged 25–45. Each one runs about 45–75 seconds, with a 120–180 word voiceover and numbers spoken in words.

**Figures checked against official sources on:** 3 November 2026.

## Format of every script file

Every script follows the same layout:
1. **Hook:** the first-2-seconds line, repeated as the opening line of the voiceover.
2. **Voiceover:** written for TTS, with numbers spelled out.
3. **On-screen text:** 4–7 scenes in a table, with numbers in figures.
4. **Caption:** 2–3 short lines, a soft follow/comment ask, and 3–5 hashtags.
5. **Disclaimer:** the exact required wording.
6. **Sources:** links to official pages.

The rules hold across all 30:
- No product, insurer or plan names.
- No premiums or returns, and no selling.
- No guarantees, no "best", and no client stories.

## Deliberately left out (could not be confirmed on an official page)

- **HDB household income ceiling (\$14,000 vs \$16,000):** official pages disagreed after the NDR 2026 change, so r09 uses only the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant figures.
- **Exact credit card interest rate:** r05 uses MoneySense's "about 25% to 29% a year" range rather than a single rate.
- **"Money Lock" mechanics and a "24/7 kill switch":** no official explainer was found, so r28 does not mention them.

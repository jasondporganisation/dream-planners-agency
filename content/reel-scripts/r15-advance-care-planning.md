# R15 — The conversation most families avoid: Advance Care Planning

**Life stage:** Will, LPA & estate · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"If you couldn't speak for yourself, who would know what you want?"

## Voiceover
If you couldn't speak for yourself, who would know what you want?

That's what Advance Care Planning is about. According to the Agency for Integrated Care, it's a voluntary, ongoing process where you talk with your loved ones and your healthcare team about your values and preferences for future care, in case you lose the ability to decide for yourself. Anyone aged twenty-one and above who can make their own decisions can do it.

There's a related but different document: the Advance Medical Directive, or AMD. It's a legal document where you state in advance that you don't want extraordinary life-sustaining treatment if you become terminally ill and unconscious. It's witnessed by a doctor and one other person, and it must be registered with the Ministry of Health.

Neither is about money. But both spare your family from guessing at the hardest moment.

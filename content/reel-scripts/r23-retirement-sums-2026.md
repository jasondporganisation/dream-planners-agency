# R23 — BRS, FRS, ERS: CPF's retirement sums, explained simply

**Life stage:** Retirement myths · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"BRS, FRS, ERS. What do these letters actually mean?"

## Voiceover
BRS, FRS, ERS. What do these letters actually mean?

They're the three CPF retirement sums. They're the amounts you can set aside in your Retirement Account at fifty-five, for monthly payouts later. Here are the numbers for members turning fifty-five in 2026.

Basic Retirement Sum: one hundred and ten thousand two hundred dollars.

Full Retirement Sum: two hundred and twenty thousand four hundred dollars. That's two times the basic.

Enhanced Retirement Sum: four hundred and forty thousand eight hundred dollars. That's four times the basic. It's the most you can set aside if you choose to top up.

Broadly speaking, the more you set aside, the higher your monthly payouts later. These sums are adjusted for each new group turning fifty-five, so if you're thirty or forty today, yours will likely be different.

# R07 — Retrenched or between jobs? Know this support exists

**Life stage:** Career switch · **Target length:** ~60 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Lost your job? You may not be on your own."

## Voiceover
Lost your job? You may not be on your own.

Since April 2025, there's a scheme called SkillsFuture Jobseeker Support. It gives eligible jobseekers up to six thousand dollars over six months while they look for work or retrain. Payouts start at fifteen hundred dollars in the first month, then taper down, and they're capped by your previous average monthly income.

Who's it for? Based on the announcement by the Ministry of Manpower, you must have lost your job involuntarily, earned an average of five thousand dollars a month or less before, and worked at least six of the past twelve months. Other conditions apply, so check the official page.

Three other things to do early. Update your monthly budget for a lower income. Check what employer benefits, like group health coverage, end with your job. And look at training options to help you move into a new field.

Know someone going through this? Send them this video.

# R21 — Caring for a parent at home? Check this grant

**Life stage:** Caring for parents · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Caring for Mum or Dad at home? There's a grant for that."

## Voiceover
Caring for Mum or Dad at home? There's a grant for that.

It's called the Home Caregiving Grant, run through the Agency for Integrated Care. It gives a monthly cash payout to help with the costs of caring for a loved one at home.

From April 2026, the amounts went up. Depending on your household's monthly income per person, it's six hundred, four hundred or two hundred dollars a month. The top amount of six hundred dollars is for households earning fifteen hundred dollars or less per person.

Who qualifies? The person being cared for must be a Singapore Citizen, or a PR with a Singapore Citizen parent, child or spouse. They must permanently need help with at least three of six activities of daily living, like bathing, dressing or eating. And they must not be living in a residential long-term care home.

Caregiving is hard enough. Don't leave support on the table.

Share this with someone who's caring for a loved one.

# R03 — Your first tax bill: less scary than you think

**Life stage:** First job · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Got a letter from IRAS? Don't panic."

## Voiceover
Got a letter from IRAS? Don't panic.

Starting work means you may need to file income tax for the first time. Here are four things to know.

One: the deadline. Individual income tax filing is due by eighteenth April each year, for the income you earned in the year before.

Two: the first twenty thousand dollars of chargeable income is taxed at zero per cent. The next ten thousand is taxed at two per cent. Chargeable income is what's left after reliefs and deductions, not your gross salary.

Three: reliefs lower your chargeable income. For example, if you're below fifty-five, you get an Earned Income Relief of one thousand dollars.

Four: there's a ceiling. Total personal income tax reliefs are capped at eighty thousand dollars a year.

Check your tax account on the IRAS website each year, and file on time.

# R16 — New parents: how much leave do you actually get?

**Life stage:** First child · **Target length:** ~55 seconds

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
"Dads, you may get more leave than you think."

## Voiceover
Dads, you may get more leave than you think.

Parental leave in Singapore has changed a lot recently. Here's a quick guide for working parents of Singapore Citizen children, based on the Ministry of Manpower.

Mums: Government-Paid Maternity Leave is sixteen weeks, if you meet the conditions, like having worked for your employer for at least three months.

Dads: Government-Paid Paternity Leave is four weeks, for children born, or due, on or after first April 2025. Before that, it was two weeks, with two more only if your employer agreed.

And there's Shared Parental Leave, which the couple can split between them. For children born on or after first April 2026, it's ten weeks. For children born from April 2025 to March 2026, it was six weeks.

Why does this matter for money? Leave time is income protected while you care for your baby. So plan your budget around it early.

## Format of every post

- **Theme** and **Flag** line.
- **Hook:** the first line of the caption.
- **Post:** the caption body. Hook plus body is 80–180 words. The same text works as an Instagram caption and as LinkedIn text; on LinkedIn, drop the line breaks between short lines if preferred.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5.
- **Disclaimer:** the exact required wording, inside the post itself (FSG-03 para 5.4: each post must stand on its own; a bio disclaimer does not count).
- **Sources:** official links for every figure. Figures come only from the fact pack; posts with no figures say so.

## Rules followed

- Figures only from the fact pack, marked VERIFIED, with dates ("for 2026", "from 1 Jan 2027"). Nothing marked UNVERIFIED is used.
- No product, plan or insurer names. CPF LIFE, MediShield Life and Integrated Shield Plans are government schemes or the generic scheme category, not products.
- No guarantees, no "best", no selling, no premiums for any private plan, no returns or illustrations, no testimonials, no client details.
- Jason is described only as a representative. Never "Financial Adviser" or "independent".
- Career posts make no income, promotion or success claims.

## ⚑ Posts that depend on 1 Jan 2027 rule changes

These use figures that take effect on 1 Jan 2027. Re-check each against cpf.gov.sg on or after 1 Jan 2027 before posting. The fact pack expires after 31 Jan 2027 unless re-verified, so **every post in this bank needs its figures re-checked against a refreshed fact pack before it goes out**.

The full list is filled in as each month is written; see the Flag line on each post.

## Date-sensitive (not 1 Jan 2027)

- MediShield Life deductibles: a second phase of increases takes effect on **1 Apr 2027**; the new amounts were not published when this bank was written. Posts quoting 2026 deductibles say so, and must be updated or held after 1 Apr 2027.
- MediShield Life claim limits apply from 1 Jun 2026; MOH updates them from time to time.
- SingStat statistics (population, life expectancy, income) are replaced each year.

# Objection Cheat Sheet (one page, print A4)

**Full version:** [objection-handling-playbook.md](objection-handling-playbook.md) · DRAFT, pending compliance sign-off · Figures VERIFIED 3 Nov 2026. Check the live page before quoting.

**Every time, use LACE:** **L**isten (let them finish, pause) · **A**cknowledge ("That makes sense") · **C**larify ("What's behind that?") · **E**xplain with a source, or **E**xit warmly.

| # | They say | Ask / say (no pressure) | Never say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I have insurance through work" | "What does it cover? What happens to it if you change jobs?" | "Company cover is useless" |
| 2 | "I'll think about it" | "Of course. Is any part unclear? I'll leave the documents." | "Price is only valid today" |
| 3 | "Too expensive" | "What amount could you keep up even in a tight year?" | "Only a cup of coffee a day" |
| 4 | "My friend is an agent" | "That's good. Happy to be a sounding board or step back." | "Your friend isn't experienced" |
| 5 | "Insurance is a scam" | "Did something happen? I'll show you the exclusions too." | "We never reject claims" |
| 6 | "Buy term, invest the rest" | "Works well if you invest consistently. What are your needs?" | "Term is a waste of money" |
| 7 | "I'm young and healthy" | "Who relies on you? 'Nothing now' is a fine answer." | "Lock in before it's too late" |
| 8 | "Let me ask my spouse" | "Very sensible. Shall we all meet together?" | "Your spouse needn't know" |
| 9 | "CPF / MediShield Life is enough" | "MSHL is for life, sized for B2/C wards, with co-payments. Let's check the official page." | "MediShield Life is useless" |
| 10 | "Send me details first" | "Sure. Two quick questions so I send what's relevant?" | (Sending a quote before a fact-find) |
| 11 | "No time" | "20 minutes when it suits you, or I'll check back later?" | "You'll make time in hospital" |
| 12 | "You want commission" | "Yes, reps are paid. So expect my reasons in writing, and check me on the MAS register." | "I earn nothing" / "I'm independent" |
| 13 | "I have enough cover" | "A review against your goals? If it's enough, I'll say so." | "Nobody has enough insurance" |
| 14 | "I'll buy online" | "Valid choice. Here's the MoneySense guide." | "Buying online is dangerous" |
| 15 | "Guarantee the returns?" | "No. Let me show you the guaranteed and non-guaranteed columns." | "You can't lose money" |
| 16 | "Maybe next year" | "What would make it the right time?" | "Next year it'll cost much more" |
| 17 | "My parents bought me a policy" | "Do you know what it covers and who owns it?" | "Cancel those, start fresh" |
| 18 | "Bad experience before" | "I'm sorry. Tell me more? Disputes: insurer, then FIDReC." | "That company always rejects claims" |
| 19 | "Single, no dependants" | "What if you couldn't work for six months?" | "Who'll pay for your funeral?" |
| 20 | "I have a condition" | "MSHL covers pre-existing conditions. Private plans: disclose everything." | "Don't mention it on the form" |
| 21 | "You're new" | "The policy is with the insurer, and I have a team behind me. Let's agree a review date." | "Older agents are out of touch" |
| 22 | "Compare other insurers" | "Please do. I represent one insurer, so I can't compare the market." | "Others are worse" / "I compare all" |
| 23 | "Told to switch" | "Get a written gain/lose comparison: waiting periods, new exclusions, costs." | "Switch to me instead" |
| 24 | "Cancel for cash" | "What's happening? Let's see what you'd lose, and the policy's options." | "Cancel; I'll get you a new one" |
| 25 | "Premium up, drop rider" | "Options: ward class, no rider. One IP only; MediSave limits $300/$600/$900; riders in cash." | "Never downgrade" |
| 26 | "No review needed" | "Any changes? Marriage revokes a CPF nomination." | "Reviews are compulsory" |
| 27 | "ILP is down, I want out" | "What was your goal? Values go up and down; check charges and cover lost." | "It will definitely bounce back" |
| 28 | "I have a will" | "CPF isn't covered by a will; CPF nomination is free. See a lawyer for legal points." | "Nomination = no will needed" |
| 29 | "Don't talk about death" | "Understood. We'll go at your pace, or stop here." | "You must face reality" |
| 30 | "You decide for me" | "I need to understand your situation first, and you should know why." | "Just sign here" |

**Walk away when:** clear "no" (or two) · they can't afford it without hardship · no real need · they don't understand or are vulnerable · someone else is pressuring them · they ask you to hide information · the meeting turns hostile · they ask not to be contacted (update DNC/consent records). Exit: *"Thank you for being honest with me. If anything changes, you know where to find me."*

**Document the same day** in the principal's system: who was there · objections in the client's words · what you explained and the source · the decision and why · documents given · vulnerability signals · next step · for any recommendation, needs, suitability, disadvantages and alternatives · for any switch, the written comparison and your coach's review.

**Red lines:** no guarantees · no product or insurer names · no disparaging others · no "independent" · no twisting or switching pushes · no false urgency · no unverified figures (e.g. 2027 MSHL deductibles).

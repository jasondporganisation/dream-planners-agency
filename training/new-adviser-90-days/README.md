# New Adviser Representative: First 90 Days Training Pack

**For:** Dream Planners new representatives, January 2027 intake
**Training starts:** Monday 4 January 2027 (Day 1). **Day 90:** Saturday 3 April 2027
**Owner:** People Director (Heron), with team leaders as coaches
**Figures checked:** 3 November 2026. Re-check all figures in `sources.md` in the week before Day 1.

Welcome! These 90 days are about building **knowledge, habits and confidence**, in that order. Nobody expects you to know everything in Week 1. We expect you to ask questions, practise a lot, and always put the client first.

> **Important:** This pack is product-neutral and names no insurer or product. It makes **no promises about earnings or income**. All activity numbers are **suggestions** to help you build habits, not quotas. Your principal's compliance manual, forms and approval processes always come first. Where they are stricter, follow them.

## The big rule: no advice before you're on the register

You may give financial advice to clients **only after** you have:

1. Passed the CMFAS exam modules your role needs. For life insurance this is typically **RES5, M9 and M9A**, plus the **Health Insurance (HI)** module if you'll advise on health insurance. Some qualifications give exemptions. Your principal confirms the exact list against MAS Notice FAA-N26. [MAS-4]
2. Been appointed by your principal, and your name **appears on the MAS Financial Institution Representatives Register**. [MAS-5]

Until then, you can learn, practise role-plays with colleagues, and build your warm list. You **cannot** advise or recommend products to anyone, friends and family included.

## Week-by-week plan

Weeks run Monday to Saturday. Public holidays in this period include Chinese New Year (early February 2027), Hari Raya Puasa (around March 2027) and Good Friday (26 March 2027). Check the official 2027 public holiday list and move sessions as needed.

### Phase 1: Foundations (Days 1–30)

#### Week 1 (4–9 Jan 2027, Days 1–6): Welcome, licensing and conduct
- **Learning:** Module A (conduct basics). Module F part 1 (set up your calendar).
- **Licensing:** Confirm exam status and book any outstanding CMFAS modules. Start the appointment paperwork with your principal. [MAS-4] [MAS-5]
- **Set-up:** Firm systems, email, compliance manual, social media policy. Register any business social media account with the firm. [MAS-3]
- **Note:** New CPF senior contribution rates and new retirement sums took effect on **1 January 2027**. See Module E. [CPF-2] [CPF-7]
- **Suggested activity:** Read the compliance manual. Write your first 30 warm-list names. No client contact.
- **Day 7 coach check** (Day 7 is Sun 10 Jan; hold it on Sat 9 Jan or Mon 11 Jan). See `coach-checklist.md`.

#### Week 2 (11–16 Jan, Days 8–13): Fact-find and warm list
- **Learning:** Module B (fact-find). Module D part 1 (warm list).
- **Practice:** Three fact-find role-plays with colleagues.
- **Licensing:** Exam study blocks (suggested: 2 hours a day if you have exams outstanding).
- **Suggested activity:** Warm list grows to about 100 names (suggestion, not quota). Sorted A, B and C, with no sensitive notes.

#### Week 3 (18–23 Jan, Days 15–20): National schemes
- **Learning:** Module E (CPF, MediShield Life, CareShield Life).
- **Practice:** Explain the MediShield Life "bill waterfall" and the CareShield Life trigger to a buddy, using the live official pages. [MOH-3] [CPF-9]
- **Exercise:** Bookmark every CPF and MOH page in `sources.md` on your phone.
- **Suggested activity:** If you're on the register, start honest first approaches to your A list: 3–5 a day after any DNC check. [PDPC-1] If not yet appointed, keep practising.

#### Week 4 (25–30 Jan, Days 22–27): Routine and first review
- **Learning:** Module F (full). A short refresher quiz on Module A.
- **Practice:** Two full mock meetings (fact-find to next step) observed by the team leader.
- **Suggested activity:** 3–5 honest contacts a day. 5–8 conversations this week. 1–2 fact-finds (practice or real, if appointed).
- **Day 30 coach check** (Day 30 is Tue 2 Feb; hold it that week).

### Phase 2: Supervised practice (Days 31–60)

#### Week 5 (1–6 Feb, Days 29–34): Objections
- **Learning:** Module C (objections). Two objection cards at every huddle from now on.
- **Appointments:** First real fact-finds, with the **coach joining or reviewing** each one.
- **Suggested activity:** 5–8 contacts a day. 8–12 conversations this week. 2–4 fact-finds.

#### Week 6 (8–13 Feb, Days 36–41): Referrals (short week around Chinese New Year)
- **Learning:** Module D part 3 (referrals).
- **Practice:** Referral ask role-play. Use the festive season for warm, **non-sales** catch-ups.
- **Suggested activity:** Lighter week. Protect family time. Keep the end-of-day review.

#### Week 7 (15–20 Feb, Days 43–48): First recommendations
- **Learning:** Revisit Module A (disclosures and free-look) and Module B (documenting the reasonable basis, including disadvantages). [MAS-2]
- **Appointments:** First recommendation meetings. The coach reviews the needs summary **before** each meeting.
- **Suggested activity:** 1–3 recommendation meetings, only where a proper fact-find shows a need.

#### Week 8 (22–27 Feb, Days 50–55): Quality focus
- **Learning:** Module F refresh. Weekly quality check deep-dive.
- **Review:** The coach and rep review three completed case files against the quality checklist.
- **Suggested activity:** Keep the same rhythm. Fix any document gaps before new appointments.

#### Week 9 (1–6 Mar, Days 57–62): Social media and reputation
- **Learning:** Module A, social media section, as a workshop. Each rep drafts one educational post and sends it through the **firm's approval process**. [MAS-3]
- **Suggested activity:** 10–15 conversations. 3–5 fact-finds.
- **Day 60 coach check** (around Thu 4 Mar).

### Phase 3: Growing independence (Days 61–90)

#### Week 10 (8–13 Mar, Days 64–69): Schemes refresh
- **Learning:** Module E refresh. Check the live pages for any figures updated in January (for example the 2027 Basic Healthcare Sum and CPF interest for Jan–Mar 2027). [CPF-8] [CPF-5]
- **Appointments:** Own appointments. The coach reviews files afterwards instead of attending.
- **Suggested activity:** 5–10 contacts a day. 2–4 recommendation meetings.

#### Week 11 (15–20 Mar, Days 71–76): Client service
- **Learning:** Policy delivery and the free-look explanation. Setting review dates with clients. [LIA-1] [MS-1]
- **Practice:** Delivery-meeting role-play: explain documents, free-look, and how to contact you.
- **Suggested activity:** Follow-up calls to every client and prospect from Weeks 5–10.

#### Week 12 (22–27 Mar, Days 78–83): Upcoming rule changes (short week, Good Friday 26 Mar)
- **Learning:** The **MediShield Life deductible changes from 1 April 2027**. Read the live MOH table and practise explaining it. [MOH-3] [MOH-4]
- **Suggested activity:** Keep the rhythm. Plan your Month 4 calendar.

#### Week 13 (29 Mar–3 Apr, Days 85–90): Reflect and plan
- **Learning:** Re-take all six module quizzes. Aim to pass each one.
- **Review:** Self-assessment, then the **Day 90 coach check**.
- **Plan:** A written 90-day plan for Months 4–6, covering skills, habits and learning, with **no income targets**.

## How we measure progress

We look at **skills, habits and quality**, never income:
- Quiz scores (suggested pass mark 8/10; 9/10 for Module E)
- Observed role-plays and meetings
- Complete, accurate case files
- Honest activity tracking
- Client feedback and zero compliance issues

That's normal and fine. Tell your team leader or buddy early. We would much rather adjust the plan than have you burn out or cut corners.

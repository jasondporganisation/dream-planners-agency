# Module E: CPF, MediShield Life and CareShield Life Basics

**When:** Week 3 (refresh in Week 10, after the 1 January figures have settled)
**Time:** 20-minute talk, 25-minute role-play, 15-minute quiz and review

> Every figure below was checked against the official page on **3 November 2026** (see `sources.md`). These schemes change often. **Before quoting any number to a client, open the live page.** If you can't check, say "Let me confirm the latest figure from CPF and send it to you." That is always a good answer.

> These are national schemes. Explain them neutrally. Never suggest a client should change what they do in CPF just to fund a product.

## Fact sheet (for the trainer and for revision)

### CPF contributions [CPF-1] [CPF-2] [CPF-3] [CPF-4]

Rates are for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents from the third year, with monthly wages above $750.

| Employee age | Total, from 1 Jan 2026 | Total, from 1 Jan 2027 | Employer / employee from 1 Jan 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 55 and below | 37% | 37% (no change) | 17% / 20% |
| Above 55 to 60 | 34% | **35.5%** | 16.5% / 19% |
| Above 60 to 65 | 25% | **26%** | 13% / 13% |
| Above 65 to 70 | 16.5% | 16.5% (no change) | 9% / 7.5% |
| Above 70 | 12.5% | 12.5% (no change) | 7.5% / 5% |

- The 2027 increase for those above 55 to 65 goes **fully to the Retirement Account (RA)**, up to the Full Retirement Sum. If the RA already holds the FRS, it goes to the Ordinary Account. [CPF-3]
- **Ordinary Wage (OW) ceiling:** **$8,000 a month from 1 January 2026**. This was the last of four steps that started on 1 September 2023. The annual salary ceiling stays at **$102,000**. [CPF-4] The 2027 rate table also shows the OW ceiling at $8,000. [CPF-2]

### CPF interest rates [CPF-5] [CPF-6]

| Account | Rate (1 Oct to 31 Dec 2026) |
|---|---|
| Ordinary Account (OA) | 2.5% a year (the legal minimum) |
| Special, MediSave and Retirement Accounts | 4% a year (the floor; extended to **31 December 2027**) |

- **Extra interest, members below 55:** an extra 1% a year on the first $60,000 of combined balances, of which at most $20,000 can come from the OA. [CPF-6]
- **Extra interest, members 55 and above:** an extra 2% a year on the first $30,000 and an extra 1% on the next $30,000, with the same $20,000 OA cap. [CPF-6]
- OA rates are reviewed **every quarter**. Always quote the current quarter. [CPF-5]

### Retirement sums [CPF-7]

| Turning 55 in | Basic (BRS) | Full (FRS) | Enhanced (ERS) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | $110,200 | $220,400 | $440,800 |
| 2027 | $114,100 | $228,200 | $456,400 |

- FRS = 2 × BRS. Since 2025, ERS = 4 × BRS (that is, 2 × FRS). The BRS and FRS are fixed by the year a member turns 55; the ERS a member can top up to rises each year. [CPF-7]
- CPF LIFE payouts can start from age 65. CPF's own estimates for 2026 (male, Standard Plan) are about **$950 / $1,780 / $3,440 a month** for BRS / FRS / ERS. These are CPF's estimates. Don't round them up, and don't present them as guaranteed for any one person. [CPF-7]

### MediSave: Basic Healthcare Sum [CPF-8]

- **2026 BHS: $79,000** for members aged 65 and below. It is adjusted every year until 65, then fixed for life. Contributions above the BHS flow to the member's other CPF accounts. [CPF-8]
- The 2027 BHS had **not** been published when this pack was written. Check CPF-8 in January 2027.

### MediShield Life [MOH-1] [MOH-2] [MOH-3] [MOH-4]

- **Who:** all Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents, **for life**, including people with **pre-existing conditions**. Premiums can be paid fully from MediSave. [MOH-1] [CPF-11]
- **Design:** sized for subsidised treatment (B2/C wards) in public hospitals. Integrated Shield Plans sit on top of it, but MediShield Life stays as the base. [MOH-1] [MOH-5]
- **Claim limit:** **$200,000 per policy year**, with no lifetime limit. Ward benefit for a normal ward is **$830 a day**, plus an extra **$800 a day for the first two days** of an inpatient stay. These figures apply to admissions or treatments on or after **1 June 2026**. [MOH-2]
- **Deductible:** a fixed amount the patient pays first, once per policy year. It varies by ward class, day surgery and age (81 and above pay more). The 2024 review set higher deductibles in **two phases**: the first from **April 2025**, the second from **1 April 2027**. **Always read the deductible from MOH-3 on the day you explain it**, especially in early 2027. [MOH-3] [MOH-4]
- **Co-insurance (per policy year):** **10%** of the first $5,000 claimable (this $5,000 includes the deductible), **5%** of the next $5,000, and **3%** above $10,000. [MOH-3]
- Deductible and co-insurance can be paid from MediSave (within limits) and/or cash. [MOH-3] [MOH-4]

### CareShield Life [CPF-9] [CPF-10]

- **Who:** people born in **1980 or later** are covered automatically from age 30, or from 1 October 2020, whichever is later. This applies even with pre-existing conditions or disability. People born **1979 or earlier** can choose to join. ElderShield 400 holders born 1970–1979 without severe disability were enrolled from **1 December 2021**. [CPF-9]
- **Trigger:** an MOH-accredited assessor finds the person **unable to perform 3 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living**: washing, dressing, feeding, toileting, walking or moving around, and transferring. [CPF-9]
- **Payout:** a monthly cash payout **for life**, as long as the person stays severely disabled. The 2026 payout is **$689 a month**. Payouts will rise **4% a year until 2030** (to $806 in 2030), then be reviewed. The amount a person can get grows each year until **age 67 or a successful claim**, whichever is first, and then stays fixed. [CPF-9] [CPF-10]
- **Premiums:** paid during working years, until **age 67 or 10 years after joining, whichever is later**. They can be paid from MediSave. Subsidies of up to 30% help lower- and middle-income households. [CPF-9]

## 20-minute talk outline

### 0:00–0:02 Why accuracy matters
- Clients rely on what you say about national schemes. A wrong figure is a **Fair Dealing Outcome 4** problem: information must be clear, relevant, timely and accurate. [MAS-1]
- Habit: **cite, don't recite**. Show the client the official page.

### 0:02–0:07 CPF: contributions and interest
- Walk through the contribution table. Highlight the 1 January 2027 change. [CPF-2] [CPF-3]
- OW ceiling $8,000. Annual ceiling $102,000. [CPF-4]
- Interest: OA 2.5%, others 4% floor until end-2027, plus extra interest. [CPF-5] [CPF-6]

### 0:07–0:10 Retirement sums and CPF LIFE
- BRS, FRS and ERS for 2026 and 2027. The 2× relationships. [CPF-7]
- CPF LIFE from 65. Use CPF's own estimates and calculators, not your own maths. [CPF-7]

### 0:10–0:15 MediShield Life
- Universal, for life, pre-existing conditions covered. Sized for B2/C wards. [MOH-1]
- Draw the "bill waterfall" on the whiteboard: **bill → deductible → co-insurance → MediShield Life pays the rest, up to the limits**. [MOH-3]
- Flag the 1 April 2027 deductible change. [MOH-4]

### 0:15–0:19 CareShield Life
- Who is covered, the 3-of-6 ADL trigger, $689 a month in 2026, 4% a year growth to 2030. [CPF-9] [CPF-10]
- What it is for: a basic monthly cash payout for long-term care. Let the client say whether it feels enough for their family. Don't tell them it isn't.

### 0:19–0:20 Close
- Where to look: the CPF and MOH pages in `sources.md`. Bookmark them on your phone today.

## Role-play script (25 minutes)

### Scene 1: "Is my hospital bill covered?" (8 minutes)

**Client:** "My dad was in hospital in a C ward. The bill is $8,000. MediShield Life covers everything, right?"

**Representative:** "MediShield Life helps a lot with bills like this, but it doesn't usually pay all of it. Here's how it works. First there's a **deductible**, a fixed amount per policy year that depends on ward class and age. I'll show you today's figure on the MOH page. Then there's **co-insurance**, a share of the rest: 10% on the first $5,000 claimable and 5% on the next $5,000. MediShield Life pays the remainder, within its claim limits. Your dad can usually use MediSave for the deductible and co-insurance, within limits. Shall we open the MOH page together so you see the exact numbers?"

**Observer checks:** Did the rep avoid saying "covers everything"? Did they explain the deductible and co-insurance correctly, and point to the live MOH page rather than guess the deductible?

### Scene 2: "What's CareShield Life again?" (8 minutes)

**Client (aged 38):** "I got a CPF letter about CareShield Life. Did I sign up for something?"

**Representative:** "No need to worry. You were born after 1980, so you're covered automatically. It's a national long-term care scheme. If an MOH-accredited assessor finds you can't do 3 of 6 daily activities, such as washing, dressing, feeding, toileting, moving around and transferring, it pays you a monthly cash amount for life while you remain severely disabled. For a claim in 2026 that's $689 a month, and CPF has said payouts rise 4% a year until 2030. Premiums can come from MediSave. Do you want to talk about whether that amount would be enough for your family, or is it fine as it is?"

**Observer checks:** Correct cohort, correct ADL rule, correct figure and source, and an open question with no pressure.

### Scene 3: The "trick" question (9 minutes)

**Client:** "My friend said CPF gives 5% on everything now. True?"

**Representative:** "Not quite. Let me show you. The Ordinary Account earns 2.5% a year. The Special, MediSave and Retirement Accounts earn at least 4% a year, and the government has extended that floor to the end of 2027. On top of that there's **extra interest**: for someone under 55, an extra 1% on the first $60,000 of combined balances, with up to $20,000 of that from the OA. So some balances can earn 5%, but not all. Here's the CPF page that explains it."

**Observer checks:** Did the rep correct the claim politely? Were the figures right? Did they show the source?

## Quiz (10 questions)

1. What is the total CPF contribution rate from 1 January 2027 for an employee aged above 55 to 60?
2. Where does the extra contribution for employees above 55 to 65 go from 2027?
3. What is the CPF Ordinary Wage ceiling in 2026?
4. What is the floor rate on Special, MediSave and Retirement Account savings, and until when has it been extended?
5. What is the Full Retirement Sum for a member turning 55 in 2027?
6. True or false: MediShield Life excludes people with pre-existing conditions.
7. What is the MediShield Life policy-year claim limit for treatment on or after 1 June 2026?
8. State the three MediShield Life co-insurance tiers.
9. What triggers a CareShield Life payout?
10. What is the CareShield Life monthly payout for 2026, and how will it change to 2030?

### Answers

1. **35.5%**: employer 16.5%, employee 19%. [CPF-2] [CPF-3]
2. **Fully to the Retirement Account**, up to the Full Retirement Sum. If the FRS is already met, it goes to the Ordinary Account. [CPF-3]
3. **$8,000 a month** (from 1 January 2026). [CPF-4]
4. **4% a year**, extended until **31 December 2027**. [CPF-5]
5. **$228,200**. [CPF-7]
6. **False.** It covers all Singapore Citizens and PRs for life, including pre-existing conditions. [MOH-1]
7. **$200,000 per policy year**, with no lifetime limit. [MOH-2]
8. **10%** on the first $5,000 claimable (including the deductible), **5%** on the next $5,000, and **3%** above $10,000. [MOH-3]
9. An MOH-accredited assessor finds the person **unable to perform at least 3 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living**. [CPF-9]
10. **$689 a month** in 2026. It rises **4% a year until 2030** (reaching $806), then gets reviewed. [CPF-10]

**Pass mark suggestion:** 9/10. This module is about facts clients rely on, so the bar is higher. Re-test anyone below 9 within the week.

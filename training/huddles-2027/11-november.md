## Week 44: Tue 2 Nov 2027: Complaints and ethics under pressure

**Theme:** Compliance  
**Why this week:** Year-end targets are when shortcuts are most tempting.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Handle complaints quickly, independently and fairly. Acknowledge, escalate through our principal's complaints process, and never try to settle it yourself "off the record".
2. Pressure is not an excuse. Rushed fact-finds, pre-signed forms and incomplete disclosures are conduct breaches, and from 1 Jan 2027 misconduct reporting is covered by MAS Notice FAA-N27 (fact pack §5.8).
3. Speak up early. If something feels wrong, tell Heron, Spectre or compliance before it becomes a problem.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**"What would you do?" drill (pairs).** The facilitator reads 4 pressure scenarios, such as "the client wants to sign today without reading the illustration" or "a teammate offers a 'shortcut' form". Pairs agree on the right action and the words to use.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Chose the compliant action every time
- [ ] Has words ready to say no to a client or teammate politely
- [ ] Knows the escalation route

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *What is one documentation or process standard you will hold to, whatever the targets?*

## Week 45: Tue 9 Nov 2027: Year-end review sprint

**Why this week:** Reviews are clustered in November and December.

- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Use the 30-minute agenda strictly. Cover life changes, the fact-find refresh, nominations, will and LPA prompts, and next steps. Keep it on time.
2. Prepare in 10 minutes: last review notes, open items, and any 1 Jan changes that could affect the client.
3. Write the file note within 2 working days, including decisions not to act.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Speed review (threes, 8 min).** Run a complete compressed review with the persona *"Mrs Tan, 61, re-employed, asks about the CPF rate change for her age."* The adviser uses the fact pack 2027 rate for above 60 to 65 (26%).

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Followed the agenda order
- [ ] Quoted the right 2027 figure with its date
- [ ] Checked nominations
- [ ] Agreed a write-up and next steps

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many year-end reviews will you complete this week?*

## Week 46: Tue 16 Nov 2027: Year-end pressure and staying steady

**Theme:** Mindset  
**Why this week:** This is the peak of year-end comparison and stress.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Control the controllables: activity, preparation and follow-up. Outcomes lag behind effort.
2. Leaderboards can hurt as well as help. Compare yourself with your own Q4 plan, not with your teammates.
3. Celebrate process wins, like a great fact-find or a clean file, as loudly as results.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Process-win round (pairs).** Each person names 3 process wins from the last fortnight. Their partner asks "what made that work?" and the pair writes down one habit to keep.

**Observer checks:**

- [ ] No income or earnings talk
- [ ] One habit identified to keep

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *What is one controllable activity target, and one way you'll switch off this week?*

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

## Week 48: Tue 30 Nov 2027: "I want to cancel or surrender to free up cash"

**Theme:** Objection handling and client care  
**Why this week:** Year-end bills and holiday spending lead to cancellation requests.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Understand the need first. What is the cash for, and how urgent is it? Listen before you explain anything.
2. Lay out the trade-offs neutrally: loss of cover, possible surrender losses, and whether cover could be bought again at the client's current age and health. Do this in general terms, then check the actual policy documents.
3. It is the client's decision. Document the conversation and their choice, and never block or delay a request.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Card drill.** Use playbook card **#24 "I want to cancel / surrender my policy to free up cash."** Do 3 rounds in pairs. Round 3: the client has a genuine emergency, and the adviser supports their decision.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Explored the need first
- [ ] Explained the trade-offs neutrally
- [ ] Respected the client's decision
- [ ] Documented it

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many clients with cashflow stress will you check in with this week?*

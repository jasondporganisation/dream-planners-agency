# Weekly Team Huddles 2027

**For:** Heron (people) and Spectre (sales) directors, running Jason Ng's Dream Planners team huddles.
**Status:** Draft for Jason's review. Not yet checked by Great Eastern compliance.

52 huddles, one every Tuesday from 5 Jan to 28 Dec 2027. There is one file per month. If a Tuesday is a public holiday, hold the huddle on the next working day. Check the MOM public holiday list for 2027 before you print the month.

## The 30-minute format (same every week)

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 min | **Wins and numbers** | Each person gives last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use numbers only. Never name clients, and give no details that could identify them. Share one win. |
| 5–15 min | **Skill topic** | The facilitator covers three teaching points. |
| 15–25 min | **Role-play or drill** | Practise in pairs or threes. The observer uses the checklist in the agenda. |
| 25–30 min | **Commitments** | Each person states one specific, measurable action for the week. The facilitator writes them down and reviews them at the next huddle. |

## Topic rotation

The topics rotate through: prospecting, fact-finding, client reviews, referrals, objection handling, compliance (MAS Fair Dealing, documentation), time management, mindset and product-neutral planning concepts. Calendar moments (Chinese New Year, Budget, tax filing, mid-year, year-end CPF top-ups, holidays) shape the timing.

## Rules for facilitators

1. **Use figures from the fact pack only**, and quote them with their effective date. The fact pack expires after **31 Jan 2027** unless someone re-verifies it. From February, check each figure against the current pack before the huddle. If a figure is not in the pack, or is marked UNVERIFIED, do not quote it. Point people to the official site instead.
2. **Do not name products or insurers.** Planning concepts are discussed generically.
3. **Make no earnings claims.** Do not mention income, commission, "top producer" money or income projections, whether to motivate the team or as a target. Numbers in the check-in are activity counts, not dollars.
4. **Do not use client names** or details that could identify a client (PDPA). Use the role-play personas in the agenda.
5. Unlicensed team members (interns, people in pre-registration) may role-play. They may not give advice to real people. See [Module A](../new-adviser-90-days/module-a-mas-faa-conduct-basics.md).

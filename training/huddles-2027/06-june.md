## Week 22: Tue 1 Jun 2027: CPF LIFE and retirement-age basics

**Theme:** Planning concepts  
**Why this week:** Mid-year is when clients in their 50s start asking about retirement.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no client names. The facilitator records team totals only, never individual numbers by name, and does not rank people.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. There are three CPF LIFE plans: Escalating (starts lower, rises 2% a year), Standard (level payouts) and Basic. Auto-included members can start payouts any time from 65 to 70. Payouts rise by up to **7% for each year deferred**, up to 35% at 70 (fact pack §1.4).
2. CPF estimates that someone turning 55 in 2026 with the FRS could get about **$1,780 a month** from 65 on the Standard Plan. That is an **estimate**, not a guarantee.
3. Since 1 Jul 2026 the minimum retirement age is **64** and the re-employment age is **69** (MOM). No later increase has been confirmed (fact pack §1.5).

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Explain-it-simply drill (pairs).** Client persona: *"Ahmad, 56, asks: should I start CPF LIFE at 65 or wait?"* The adviser explains the trade-off conceptually and points to the CPF planner, without recommending a start age.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Called the estimate an estimate
- [ ] Quoted figures with cohort and plan
- [ ] Gave no personal recommendation in the drill
- [ ] Pointed to the official CPF tools

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many clients aged 50+ will you invite to a retirement-income conversation?*

## Week 25: Tue 22 Jun 2027: Legacy basics: LPA, wills and nominations

**Theme:** Planning concepts  
**Why this week:** Reviews often surface "I keep meaning to do my will".

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no client names. The facilitator records team totals only, never individual numbers by name, and does not rank people.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. For Singapore Citizens, an LPA Form 1 application is **free** (MSF, 5 Mar 2026). A Certificate Issuer must still certify it, for a separate fee. Without an LPA, the family needs a court-appointed Deputy (fact pack §3.1).
2. A will is revoked on marriage. Without one, a non-Muslim's estate follows the Intestate Succession Act: with a spouse and children, ½ goes to the spouse and ½ is shared among the children (fact pack §3.2).
3. CPF nominations and trust nominations sit outside the will. A nomination does not replace a will (fact pack §3.3). We prompt and refer; we don't draft legal documents.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Legacy check-in role-play (pairs).** Client persona: *"Daniel, 38, recently married, assumes his wife gets everything."* The adviser corrects the assumption gently with fact pack wording and refers out for will and LPA preparation.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Corrected "spouse gets everything" accurately
- [ ] Mentioned that marriage revokes a will and CPF nomination
- [ ] Referred out for legal drafting
- [ ] No pressure

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many review clients will you prompt about nominations, wills and LPA this week?*

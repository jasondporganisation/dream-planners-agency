## Week 36: Tue 7 Sep 2027: "I want to compare" and "Another adviser said switch"

**Theme:** Objection handling and compliance  
**Why this week:** Comparison requests rise ahead of year-end.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Welcome the comparison: "That's sensible. Here's what to compare: cover, exclusions, costs and what's guaranteed." Never criticise other providers.
2. Switching can hurt clients through new waiting periods, loss of benefits, higher costs or new exclusions. Any recommendation to replace a policy needs a documented, client-focused reason.
3. If a client is thinking about switching on someone else's advice, help them list the questions to ask. Don't attack the other adviser.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Card drill.** Use playbook cards **#22 "I want to compare with other insurers"** and **#23 "Another adviser told me to switch my policy. Should I?"** Do 3 rounds in pairs.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Did not criticise competitors
- [ ] Named the switching risks neutrally
- [ ] Gave the client a checklist of questions
- [ ] Named no insurer or product

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many clients with pending comparisons or switch questions will you give a neutral checklist?*

## Week 37: Tue 14 Sep 2027: Inflation as a planning concept

**Theme:** Planning concepts  
**Why this week:** Year-end goal reviews need inflation-aware thinking.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Always quote inflation with its period and source. The fact pack has CPI-All Items at **0.9%** for full-year 2025 and **2.2%** year on year for Sep 2026 (SingStat/MAS, §4). By now these may be out of date, so check the latest official figure before quoting.
2. Never project a single inflation rate as fact ("inflation is 3% a year" is a never-say line). Use ranges and scenarios.
3. Standard CPF LIFE payouts are level, and are not adjusted for inflation. The Escalating Plan starts lower and rises 2% a year (fact pack §1.4). This is a concept to discuss, not a recommendation.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Scenario drill (pairs).** Client persona: *"Linda, 45, wants $X a month in today's money at 65."* The adviser explains why the future figure is higher, without quoting an inflation forecast, and suggests using scenarios.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Quoted figures with period and source
- [ ] No single-rate forecast
- [ ] Used scenarios
- [ ] Called CPF payouts estimates

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many retirement-goal fact-finds will you redo with inflation scenarios?*

## Week 38: Tue 21 Sep 2027: Data protection (PDPA) in daily work

**Theme:** Compliance  
**Why this week:** Busy Q4 workflows are when data shortcuts creep in.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Collect only what you need, use it only for the purpose you told the client, and store it only in approved systems.
2. No client data in personal WhatsApp groups, photos on your phone or printouts left in cafés. Treat screenshots as data.
3. Report a suspected data leak immediately to our principal's compliance team. Speed matters more than embarrassment.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Spot-the-breach drill (pairs).** The facilitator reads 6 short scenarios, such as "forwarding a client's NRIC photo to a teammate's personal number". For each one, pairs decide whether it is OK or a breach, and what to do instead.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Identified all the breaches
- [ ] Stated the right alternative
- [ ] Knows the reporting route

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *What is one data clean-up action this week (old photos, chats, printouts)?*

## Week 39: Tue 28 Sep 2027: Q3 review and Q4 plan

**Theme:** Mindset and time management  
**Why this week:** This is the last huddle of Q3. Q4 brings the year-end CPF top-up conversations and the review rush.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Give Q3 totals: appointments, fact-finds, reviews and submissions (counts only). How did your H2 skill focus go?
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Check progress on your H2 skill focus. What evidence do you have that it improved?
2. Plan Q4 now. Pencil in year-end reviews before October fills up.
3. Hold the line on ethics. Year-end targets never justify rushed advice or incomplete documentation.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Q4 calendar build (pairs).** Block review slots for October to December and identify which clients (by count and segment) are due. Their partner checks that admin and rest blocks are protected too.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Review slots blocked
- [ ] Admin and rest protected
- [ ] No earnings targets mentioned

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many year-end reviews will you book, and by when?*

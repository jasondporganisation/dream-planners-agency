# Huddles: December 2027

**Facilitators:** Heron (people) and Spectre (sales). **Length:** 30 minutes. See the [format and rules](README.md).

**December focus:** close out the year cleanly with top-up conversations before the year ends, documentation, rest, and the 2027 review on 28 Dec. Several figures change on 1 Jan 2028, so do not quote 2028 figures until the fact pack is refreshed.

**Standing rules:** figures come from the [fact pack](../../compliance/sg-fact-pack-2026-11.md) only, with their effective dates. Do not name products or insurers. Make no earnings claims. Do not name clients.

---

## Week 49: Tue 7 Dec 2027: Top-ups and nominations before the year ends

**Theme:** Planning concepts  
**Why this week:** There are a few weeks left in 2027 for clients' year-end admin.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Use the top-up conversation in practice: cashflow first, then the concept, then the official source for this year's rules. Quote no figure that isn't in the fact pack.
2. Year-end is a good time for a nomination check. Making a CPF nomination is free and can be done online with Singpass and two witnesses (fact pack §1.6).
3. Don't preview 2028 figures. Several will change on 1 Jan 2028. Wait for the refreshed fact pack.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Combined drill (threes).** Client persona: *"Farid, 52, married 2 years ago, asks about topping up before the year ends."* The adviser handles the top-up question and spots the nomination issue (marriage revokes a nomination).

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Spotted the revoked nomination
- [ ] Put cashflow first
- [ ] Named the official sources
- [ ] Quoted no 2028 figures

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many nomination checks will you prompt before the year ends?*

## Week 50: Tue 14 Dec 2027: Year-end documentation close-out

**Theme:** Time management and compliance  
**Why this week:** Close 2027 files before the holidays.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Run a file sweep: every 2027 recommendation should have a need, options, a reason and the client's decision recorded.
2. Close open loops: pending documents, unanswered client messages, and follow-ups you promised.
3. Archive and delete correctly, following our principal's retention rules, with nothing left on personal devices.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Paired file sweep (10 min).** Each person lists their open loops (by count and type). Their partner helps prioritise the top 5 to close before 24 Dec.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Open loops listed by type
- [ ] Top 5 prioritised with dates
- [ ] No data on personal devices

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *Which open loops (count) will you close before the holidays?*

## Week 51: Tue 21 Dec 2027: Rest, gratitude and holiday boundaries

**Theme:** Mindset  
**Why this week:** It is the last working week before Christmas.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share your favourite win of 2027 (no names) and one thing you're grateful for.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Rest is part of the job. Set an out-of-office message with an emergency contact route for claims.
2. Reflect before you plan. What did 2027 teach you about yourself as an adviser?
3. Thank your team. Peer recognition builds a culture people want to stay in.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Appreciation round (whole team).** Each person names one specific way a teammate helped them this year. Then each person drafts their holiday out-of-office message and its claims-emergency route.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Recognition is specific
- [ ] Out-of-office message includes a claims route
- [ ] No client data in the message

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *What are your holiday rest dates, and how will you handle cover for urgent client needs?*

## Week 52: Tue 28 Dec 2027: 2027 review and 2028 plan

**Theme:** Time management and mindset  
**Why this week:** This is the final huddle of 2027.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Give the 2027 totals: appointments, fact-finds, reviews and submissions (counts only). Name your best ratio improvement of the year.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Look back on the year: which huddle topics changed how you work, and which need repeating in 2028?
2. Refresh the fact pack before quoting anything in January. CPF, MOH and MAS figures change each year, and the 2026-11 pack is out of date.
3. Set one skill focus and one service standard for 2028, and leave earnings targets out of it.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**2028 one-pager (individual 6 min, pairs 4 min).** Each person writes their skill focus, service standard, weekly activity rhythm and one boundary. Their partner checks that each is specific and measurable.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Specific and measurable
- [ ] No earnings targets
- [ ] Fact pack refresh noted

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *Read out your 2028 skill focus and service standard. The facilitators collect them for the first 2028 huddle.*

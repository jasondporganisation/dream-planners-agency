## Week 27: Tue 6 Jul 2027: Risk attitude vs risk capacity

**Theme:** Fact-finding  
**Why this week:** H2 conversations often move from protection to longer-term goals.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no client names. The facilitator records team totals only, never individual numbers by name, and does not rank people.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Risk attitude is how a client *feels* about ups and downs. Risk capacity is how much loss their plans can *absorb*. Record both, separately.
2. Use the client's words. "I'd lose sleep if it dropped 20%" is better evidence than a tick box.
3. If attitude and capacity conflict, discuss it with the client and document the conversation. Never nudge someone towards more risk than they are comfortable with.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Two-question drill (threes).** Client persona: *"Wei Ling, 33, says she wants high growth but has no emergency savings."* The adviser explores attitude and capacity for 8 minutes. The observer notes the conflict and how it was handled.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Asked about feelings and capacity separately
- [ ] Surfaced the conflict out loud
- [ ] Did not push towards more risk
- [ ] Summarised for the file note

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many fact-finds this week will record both attitude and capacity?*

## Week 28: Tue 13 Jul 2027: Talks and events: education first

**Theme:** Prospecting  
**Why this week:** Mid-year is when community and workplace talks are easiest to book.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no client names. The facilitator records team totals only, never individual numbers by name, and does not rank people.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Use approved educational content only. Every slide and handout goes through our principal's approval. No product pitches at the talk.
2. Collect contact details with consent. Use a clear opt-in that says what you'll contact people about, and respect the DNC rules (see client-care referral-ask).
3. Follow up within 48 hours with value, such as a checklist from the talk, and an optional invitation to talk one to one.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Opt-in script drill (pairs).** Practise the 30-second close of a talk: thank the audience, explain the opt-in sheet, and make clear that saying no is fine. Then practise one 48-hour follow-up message.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Opt-in is explicit and specific
- [ ] No pressure
- [ ] Follow-up offers value first
- [ ] No product named

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *Which talk or event (type, not organiser name) will you approach or confirm this month?*

## Week 29: Tue 20 Jul 2027: "I'm young and healthy" and "I'm single"

**Theme:** Objection handling  
**Why this week:** Younger prospects from mid-year events often raise these.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no client names. The facilitator records team totals only, never individual numbers by name, and does not rank people.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Agree first: being young and healthy is an advantage. Then ask what they'd want protected if that changed suddenly (income, savings goals, parents).
2. Single doesn't mean nobody depends on you. Ageing parents, siblings, a future self and debts all count. Let the client draw the conclusion.
3. It's fine if the answer is "not now". Document the conversation and agree on when to revisit it.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Card drill.** Use playbook cards **#7 "I'm young and healthy"** and **#19 "I'm single, I don't have dependants."** Do 3 rounds in pairs. Round 3: the client says "not now" and the adviser closes gracefully.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Agreed before exploring
- [ ] Asked rather than lectured
- [ ] No fear tactics
- [ ] Accepted "not now" gracefully, with a revisit date

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many "not now" contacts will you put on a respectful revisit date in the CRM?*

## Week 30: Tue 27 Jul 2027: Titles, "independent" and the Register

**Theme:** Compliance  
**Why this week:** New name cards, LinkedIn updates and event bios go out mid-year.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no client names. The facilitator records team totals only, never individual numbers by name, and does not rank people.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Representatives may not call themselves "Financial Advisers" on their own; the term may only be used with "representative". "Financial planner" is not restricted (fact pack §5.5).
2. "Independent" is only for firms with no product bias or links to product providers. A tied representative cannot use it (FAA-G05).
3. Every representative has a MAS representative number. Encourage clients to check the public Register. It builds trust.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Bio audit (pairs).** Each person reads out their current LinkedIn headline, Instagram bio and name-card title. Their partner checks them against fact pack §5.5 and fixes anything that breaks the rules on the spot.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] No "Independent"
- [ ] No standalone "Financial Adviser"
- [ ] No "unbiased, I compare every insurer"
- [ ] No earnings or status claims in the bio

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *Which profiles will you update this week, and by when?*

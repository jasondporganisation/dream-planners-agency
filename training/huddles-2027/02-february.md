**Facilitators:** Heron (people) and Spectre (sales). **Length:** 30 minutes. See the [format and rules](README.md).

**February focus:** Chinese New Year falls in early February (CNY Day is Sat 6 Feb 2027; check MOM's list for any holiday in lieu). It is a relationship month, not a selling month. ## Week 5: Tue 2 Feb 2027: "I'll think about it" and "Maybe next year"

**Theme:** Objection handling  
**Why this week:** These are the most common stall lines before the holidays.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Treat a stall as a request for clarity, not a rejection. Ask what part they would like to think through, then listen.
2. Agree on a clear next step: a date, what they will consider and what you will send. A stall without a next step usually goes quiet.
3. Never manufacture urgency ("prices go up next month"). Fair Dealing means the client decides in their own time, with clear information.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Card drill.** Use playbook cards **#2 "I'll think about it"** and **#16 "I'm not ready / maybe next year."** Work in pairs and do 3 rounds each. The adviser must respond with acknowledge, ask and agree a next step within 60 seconds.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Acknowledged the concern before responding
- [ ] Asked a question rather than rebutting
- [ ] Agreed a specific next step
- [ ] Applied no pressure and no false urgency

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *Which open stalls (by count) will you follow up with a clear next-step question this week?*

## Week 6: Tue 9 Feb 2027: CNY week: relationships before results

**Theme:** Mindset  
**Why this week:** This is a short, social week. It is a good time to reset why we do this work.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win, or one reunion-table money conversation you heard. Do not use names.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Being present beats pitching. Do not prospect at reunion dinners. People remember who was pleasant, not who sold to them.
2. Your reputation compounds. Every honest "this isn't right for you" builds trust that lasts for years.
3. Low-activity weeks happen. Plan a lighter target, protect rest, and come back in full on Monday.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**"What do you do?" drill (pairs, 3 rounds).** Practise a 20-second, non-salesy answer for a relative who asks about your job at a CNY visit. It should describe the problem you help with, not a product. Then practise a graceful reply when someone asks "so what should I buy?": book a proper conversation instead of advising on the spot.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Answer is 20 seconds or less
- [ ] Describes the problem solved, not a product
- [ ] No advice given on the spot
- [ ] Makes no earnings or status claims

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *What is one rest or relationship commitment, and one light activity target, for this week?*

## Week 7: Tue 16 Feb 2027: The post-CNY referral ask

**Theme:** Referrals  
**Why this week:** Clients you served well have just been talking with family. It is a natural time to ask.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Earn the referral first, then ask. Only ask clients whose last review went well.
2. Make it specific and easy: "Is there one person who'd appreciate a no-pressure planning chat?" Never ask a client to share someone else's details without that person's consent.
3. Close the loop. Thank the referrer whatever the outcome, and never share the referred person's details or decisions with them.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**Referral script practice (pairs).** Use the scripts in client-care referral-ask. Do 3 rounds: client says yes, client hesitates, client says no. The adviser practises a gracious response to each.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Asked only after confirming the client is satisfied
- [ ] Ask is specific (one person)
- [ ] Respected consent: the client asks the friend first
- [ ] Thanked the client, whatever the answer

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many satisfied clients will you ask for a referral this week?*

## Week 8: Tue 23 Feb 2027: Budget week: verify before you quote

**Theme:** Compliance and planning concepts  
**Why this week:** Budget announcements bring a flood of numbers on social media and in WhatsApp groups.

### 0–5 min · Wins and numbers

- Each person reports last week's appointments, fact-finds, cases submitted and reviews done. Use counts only, with no names.
- Share one win from last week. A learning or a client thank-you counts as a win.
- Read back last week's commitments: done, part done or not done. Do not shame anyone. Ask what got in the way.

### 5–15 min · Skill topic

1. Announcements are not yet in force. Tell clients what was announced, that details come later, and where to read the official page.
2. Figures not in the fact pack are off-limits until it is refreshed. Say "I'll check the official page and come back to you," then do it.
3. Help clients turn news into questions about their own plans, for example "how does this affect my parents?" Do not react with product suggestions.

### 15–25 min · Role-play or drill

**"WhatsApp forward" drill (pairs).** The facilitator reads out 3 made-up "Budget forward" claims. For each one, the adviser practises a client-facing reply that neither confirms nor dismisses the claim, points to the official source, and offers a follow-up.

**Observer checks:**
- [ ] Did not confirm an unverified figure
- [ ] Named the official source (gov.sg agency)
- [ ] Offered a specific follow-up

### 25–30 min · Commitments

Each person states one commitment and the facilitator writes it down. Prompt: *How many clients will you send a neutral "Budget: what we'll look at in your review" note?*

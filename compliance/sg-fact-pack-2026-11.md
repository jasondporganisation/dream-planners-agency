## How Brand Guardian should use this pack

1. **Only quote figures that appear here as VERIFIED**, with the effective date. If a post uses a figure not in this pack, or one marked UNVERIFIED, reject it and send it for human review.
2. **Every figure links to the official page it was read from on 3 Nov 2026.** Allowed sources: cpf.gov.sg, moh.gov.sg, mas.gov.sg, msf.gov.sg, iras.gov.sg, publicguardian.gov.sg, moneysense.gov.sg, singstat.gov.sg, lia.org.sg. Exceptions are labelled where an allowed site did not state the fact (e.g. mom.gov.sg for retirement ages).
3. **Date-stamp figures in posts** ("for 2026", "from 1 Jan 2027"). Government figures change every January; a post without a date goes stale.
4. **Estimates stay estimates.** CPF LIFE payouts, projected returns and illustrations must never be called guaranteed.
5. Each item has a **Safe way to say it** and a **Never say** line. Never-say lines are patterns: reject posts that make the same kind of claim in other words.

## 1. CPF

### 1.1 Contribution rates by age (Singapore Citizens and 3rd-year+ SPRs, private sector, monthly wages above $750)

| Age band | Employer | Employee | Total (from 1 Jan 2026) | Total **from 1 Jan 2027** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 55 and below | 17% | 20% | 37% | 37% (no change) |
| Above 55 to 60 | 16% → **16.5%** | 18% → **19%** | 34% | **35.5%** |
| Above 60 to 65 | 12.5% → **13%** | 12.5% → **13%** | 25% | **26%** |
| Above 65 to 70 | 9% | 7.5% | 16.5% | 16.5% (no change) |
| Above 70 | 7.5% | 5% | 12.5% | 12.5% (no change) |

- A new age band applies from the first day of the month after the employee's 55th, 60th, 65th or 70th birthday.
- From 1 Jan 2027 the extra contributions for ages 55–65 go to the Retirement Account up to the Full Retirement Sum, then to the Ordinary Account.
- **Status:** VERIFIED
- **Sources:** [2026 rates — cpf.gov.sg](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/employer-obligations/how-much-cpf-contributions-to-pay) · [2027 rates announcement — cpf.gov.sg](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/infohub/news/cpf-related-announcements/new-contribution-rates) · [2027 rate table PDF](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/content/dam/web/employer/employer-obligations/documents/jan2027cpfcontributionrates.pdf)
- **Safe way to say it:** "From 1 Jan 2027, total CPF contributions for workers above 55 to 60 rise from 34% to 35.5% of wages (source: CPF Board)."
- **Never say:** "Everyone's CPF contributions go up in 2027." (Only the 55–65 bands change.)

| Annual salary ceiling | $102,000 a year; Additional Wage ceiling = $102,000 − total OW subject to CPF in the year | current | VERIFIED |

### 1.3 Retirement sums

| Member turns 55 in | Basic (BRS) | Full (FRS) | Enhanced (ERS) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | $110,200 | $220,400 | $440,800 |
| **2027** | **$114,100** | **$228,200** | **$456,400** |

- Since 2025 the ERS is 4 times the BRS.
- **Status:** VERIFIED
- **Source:** [What is the CPF retirement sum — cpf.gov.sg](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/infohub/educational-resources/what-is-the-cpf-retirement-sum)
- **Safe way to say it:** "If you turn 55 in 2026, the Full Retirement Sum is $220,400; for those turning 55 in 2027 it is $228,200 (CPF Board)."
- **Never say:** "You need $220,400 to retire." (The FRS is a CPF benchmark, not a personal retirement need.)

### 1.4 CPF LIFE

| Fact | Detail | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Plans | Escalating (starts lower, rises 2% a year), Standard (level payouts, not inflation-adjusted), Basic (starts low; falls once balances drop below $60,000) | VERIFIED |
| Lifelong | All three plans pay monthly for life; any remaining premium balance goes to loved ones at death | VERIFIED |
| Auto-inclusion | SC/PR born 1958 or later with at least $60,000 in retirement savings when payouts start | VERIFIED |
| Payout start | Choose any time from 65 to 70; starts automatically at 70 | VERIFIED |
| Deferral | Payouts rise by up to 7% for each year deferred, up to 35% if deferred to 70 | VERIFIED |
| Estimated payouts from 65 (turning 55 in 2026, Standard Plan, 4% interest) | BRS ≈ $950/month · FRS ≈ $1,780/month · ERS ≈ $3,440/month | VERIFIED (CPF's own estimates) |

- **Sources:** [CPF LIFE — cpf.gov.sg](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/retirement-income/monthly-payouts/cpf-life) · [Reaching 65 — cpf.gov.sg](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/retirement-income/milestones/reaching-age-65) · [Retirement sum page (payout estimates)](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/infohub/educational-resources/what-is-the-cpf-retirement-sum)
- **Safe way to say it:** "CPF estimates that someone turning 55 in 2026 who sets aside the Full Retirement Sum could receive about $1,780 a month from 65 on the Standard Plan."
- **Never say:** "CPF LIFE guarantees you $1,780 a month." (The CPF figure is an estimate tied to plan, cohort and interest assumptions.)
- **Also never say:** "Everyone still has a Special Account." (Since 2025 the Special Account closes at 55 — [Reaching 55, cpf.gov.sg](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/retirement-income/milestones/reaching-age-55).)

### 1.6 CPF nomination

| Fact | Detail | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Who can nominate | Anyone aged 16 or over | VERIFIED |
| Who can be a nominee | Anyone, including people with no CPF account | VERIFIED |
| Covered | Ordinary, Special, MediSave and Retirement Account savings; **CPF LIFE premium balance**; discounted Singtel shares | VERIFIED |
| Not covered | Property bought with CPF; Dependants' Protection Scheme payouts; CPF Investment Scheme investments | VERIFIED |
| Outside the estate | CPF savings are not part of your estate and cannot be left by will | VERIFIED |
| No nomination | Public Trustee's Office distributes to family under intestacy laws or a Muslim Inheritance Certificate; can take up to 6 months; Public Trustee deducts an admin fee | VERIFIED |
| Cost | Making a nomination is free | VERIFIED |
| How | Online with Singpass and two witnesses (not nominees, not organisations), or in person by appointment (CPF staff can witness) | VERIFIED |
| Marriage / divorce | Marriage revokes an existing nomination; divorce does not | VERIFIED |

- **Source:** [Making a CPF nomination — cpf.gov.sg](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/account-services/providing-for-your-loved-ones/making-a-cpf-nomination)
- **Safe way to say it:** "CPF savings don't go through your will. Make a CPF nomination (it's free), and review it if you get married, because marriage cancels it."
- **Never say:** "Just put your CPF in your will." or "Your CPF nomination covers your HDB flat." (Neither is true.)

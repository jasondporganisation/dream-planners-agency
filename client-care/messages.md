# Client-care message templates (M01–M40)

Warm Singapore English for WhatsApp and email. **Templates only.** Jason reviews every outgoing message before it goes out.

## How to use

- `[Square brackets]` are placeholders. Replace every one, and never send a message with a bracket still in it.
- **✍️ PERSONALISE** means the adviser must add a real, specific detail from the relationship before sending. Messages that would feel hollow or hurtful sent generically are marked this way: life events, claims, bereavement, review invitations.
- **○ Light-touch**: fill in the placeholders. Reading it through once is enough.
- Keep the client's own language register. If they write in Singlish, you can relax the tone a little. If they write formally, stay formal.
- Emojis: at most one, and only if the client uses them too. None in bereavement, illness or claims messages.

## Ground rules (every template obeys these; check yours does too)

1. **No selling.** No products, plans, "coverage gaps", promotions or "new launches".
2. **No promises.** Never say "you'll be covered", "the claim will go through", "guaranteed" or "don't worry, sure can".
3. **No premiums, returns, bonuses, payouts or prices.**
4. **No referral asks in these templates.** Referrals are handled only in conversation, per [`referral-ask.md`](referral-ask.md).
5. **Annual-review invitations are about the client's life, not products.**
6. **No sensitive data in chat:** no NRIC, policy numbers, medical details or bank details.
7. Sign off with the adviser's own name. Include the MAS representative number in email signatures if the insurer requires it.

## A. Welcome and settling in (M01–M10)

**M01 — Welcome, day 0–3 (WhatsApp)** ✍️ PERSONALISE
> Hi [Client first name], thank you for trusting me with this. I know it's not a small decision. [Personal line, e.g. "Hope the kids enjoyed the kaya toast after our meeting!"] Your documents will reach you [by post / by email] in the next [X] days. If anything is unclear, just message me here. No question is too small. — [Adviser name]

**M02 — Welcome, email version** ○ Light-touch
> Subject: Thank you, [Client first name]
>
> Dear [Client first name],
>
> Thank you for your time and trust over the past few weeks. Your policy documents will arrive [by post / by email] within [X] days. When they do, it's worth keeping them somewhere your family knows about, and telling one person you trust where they are.
>
> If anything in the documents isn't clear, please reply to this email or WhatsApp me at [number]. I'm happy to go through it with you.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Adviser name]
> [Title as approved] | MAS Rep No. [number]

**M03 — Documents checklist (email follow-up)** ○ Light-touch
> Subject: What to keep handy
>
> Hi [Client first name], a short checklist for when your documents arrive:
> • Keep them together in one place (physical folder or secure digital storage)
> • Tell a family member where they are
> • Save my number under "[Adviser name] – Insurance" so your family can find me
> • Read through the documents. The policy document sets out the terms; if anything is unclear, ask me
>
> That's all. Speak soon, [Adviser name]

**M04 — Thank you after the delivery meeting** ○ Light-touch
> Hi [Client first name], thanks for making time today. Hope it was helpful to go through everything together. As mentioned, just WhatsApp me if anything comes up. Have a good [evening / weekend]!

**M05 — Following up a question from delivery** ✍️ PERSONALISE
> Hi [Client first name], following up on your question about [topic they raised]. [Factual answer, or "I've checked and the details are on page [X] of your documents". Don't paraphrase the terms]. Happy to explain over a call if that's easier.

**M06 — Day 7: did everything arrive?** ○ Light-touch
> Hi [Client first name], just checking: have your documents reached you? If not, let me know and I'll chase it up. 🙂

**M07 — 30-day check** ○ Light-touch
> Hi [Client first name], it's been about a month. Hope things are going well! Now that the paperwork has settled, is there anything you'd like me to explain again? No need to reply if all's fine.

**M08 — 30-day check, personal version** ✍️ PERSONALISE
> Hi [Client first name], a month already! How did [thing they mentioned, e.g. "the move" / "your mum's check-up"] go? Also, a gentle reminder to let [family member] know where your documents are kept, in case they ever need them. Take care!

**M09 — 30-day check, email** ○ Light-touch
> Subject: Any questions so far?
>
> Hi [Client first name], it's been about a month since your policy started. If anything in your documents has raised questions, I'm happy to go through it, whether by call, video or coffee. If everything's clear, no need to reply. Wishing you a good month ahead. [Adviser name]

**M10 — Month 3: keep contact details current** ○ Light-touch
> Hi [Client first name], hope you're well! A small housekeeping reminder: if you change your phone number, email or address, do let me know so nothing important goes missing. Same for your emergency contact. That's all. Have a good week!

## E. Life events (M25–M32) — all ✍️ PERSONALISE

Respond only to events the client shares with you. The first message is about them. Anything about plans comes later, and only if they want it.

**M25 — Marriage**
> Congratulations, [Client first name] and [partner's name]! 🎉 So happy for you both. [Personal line, e.g. "The photos look beautiful."] Wishing you a lifetime of happiness together.

**M26 — New baby**
> Congratulations, [Client first name]! Welcome to the world, [baby's name if shared]! Hope mum and baby are resting well. Enjoy every sleepy moment. No need to reply, just sending love.

**M27 — New home**
> Congrats on the new place, [Client first name]! Moving is tiring, so hope the boxes are behaving. 🏠 Wishing you many happy years there. When you're settled, do send me your new address so I can update my records.

**M28 — New job or promotion**
> Congratulations on the new role, [Client first name]! Well deserved. [Personal line]. All the best for the first few weeks!

**M29 — Gentle review offer, sent 4–8 weeks *after* a life event, never in the same message**
> Hi [Client first name], hope you're settling in well after [the wedding / the baby / the move / the new job]. When things are calmer, and only if it's useful, I'm happy to sit down and check whether anything in your plans, nominations or contact details needs updating to reflect the change. No rush at all. Just let me know.

**M30 — Retirement**
> Congratulations on your retirement, [Client first name]! After [X] years, you've more than earned it. What's the first thing on your list? [Personal line]

**M31 — Illness or hospitalisation (the client told you)**
> Hi [Client first name], I'm so sorry to hear about [what they shared]. Please focus on resting and getting better. If there's anything practical I can help with, even just finding out what paperwork is involved, I'm here. No need to reply.

**M32 — Bereavement (condolence)**
> Dear [Client first name], I'm deeply sorry for the loss of your [relationship, e.g. father]. [Personal memory or line, if you have one.] Please don't worry about replying. When you and your family are ready, and only then, I'm here to help with anything you need.

*(At the wake: attend only if appropriate and welcome. Don't bring up policies. Paperwork comes later, at the family's pace. See [`claims-support.md`](claims-support.md).)*

## F. Claims support (M33–M36) — all ✍️ PERSONALISE

Call first if you can. These follow or replace a call. **Never predict the outcome of a claim.**

**M33 — First response when a client reports an event**
> Hi [Client first name], thank you for letting me know, and I'm sorry you're going through this. I'll call you at [time] to understand what happened and walk you through the next steps. If now isn't a good time, tell me when suits you. First, please take care of yourself and your family.

**M34 — After the first call: next-steps recap**
> Hi [Client first name], thanks for the call. As discussed, the next steps are:
> 1. [Step, e.g. "collect the documents we listed"]
> 2. [Step]
> 3. [Step]
> I'll [what you'll do] by [day]. Please don't send medical or ID documents over WhatsApp. [Use the secure method you discussed]. I'll check in with you on [day].

**M35 — Weekly update while the claim is in progress**
> Hi [Client first name], a quick update: [factual status, e.g. "the documents have been submitted and are being reviewed" / "they've asked for one more document: X"]. I'll update you again by [day]. How are you holding up?

**M36 — After the claim closes (whatever the outcome)**
> Hi [Client first name], now that this is settled, I wanted to say thank you for your patience through a difficult time. [If the outcome was not what the client hoped: "I know this wasn't the outcome you hoped for. If you'd like, I can explain the options available to you, including how to raise a concern."] How are you and the family doing?

## G. Annual review (M37–M40)

About their life, not products. Never mention premiums, returns, cover levels or "gaps".

**M37 — Annual-review invitation (WhatsApp)** ✍️ PERSONALISE
> Hi [Client first name]! Can't believe it's been almost a year. A lot can happen in a year: [personal line, e.g. "Ethan starting P1!"]. Would you like to catch up for about 30 minutes? It's a chance to hear what's changed for you and the family, and to make sure your contact details, nominations and paperwork still match your life today. [Coffee near you / video call], whichever is easier. Any of these work? [Option 1] / [Option 2] / [Option 3]

**M38 — Annual-review invitation (email)** ○ Light-touch
> Subject: A year on: time for a catch-up?
>
> Dear [Client first name],
>
> It's been about a year since we last sat down properly, and I'd love to hear how things are going.
>
> If you're open to it, I'd suggest a 30-minute catch-up to talk about:
> • what has changed in your life and family this year
> • anything on your mind for the year ahead
> • making sure your contact details, nominations and important paperwork are up to date
>
> We can meet in person or by video, whichever suits you. Here are some times: [Option 1], [Option 2], [Option 3]. Or reply with what works for you.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Adviser name]

**M39 — Gentle nudge if no reply after 2 weeks (send once only)** ○ Light-touch
> Hi [Client first name], just floating this up in case it got buried. No pressure at all. If now's a busy season, we can catch up later in the year. Just let me know what suits you. 🙂

**M40 — Thank you after the review** ✍️ PERSONALISE
> Hi [Client first name], thank you for the catch-up today. It was really good to hear about [personal highlight]. As promised, I'll [follow-up action, e.g. "send you the link to update your nomination" / "check X and get back to you by Friday"]. Speak soon!

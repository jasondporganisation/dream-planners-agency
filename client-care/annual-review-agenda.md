# Annual review: 30-minute agenda and checklist

**What it is:** a yearly catch-up about the client's **life**, plus housekeeping so their paperwork still matches it.
**What it is not:** a sales meeting. If a new need comes up, note it, and offer a **separate** advice meeting with the full advisory process (see [Fair dealing and documenting advice](#fair-dealing-and-documenting-advice)).

Invitation wording: M37–M39 in [`messages.md`](messages.md). Thank-you afterwards: M40.

---

## Before the meeting (10 min prep)

- [ ] Re-read the tracker notes: last conversation, life events shared, festivals, contact preference
- [ ] Re-read last year's review record and any open follow-ups (did we do what we promised?)
- [ ] Prepare the client's current contact details and nominations status **from the approved system**. Don't print or carry more personal data than you need
- [ ] Bring nothing promotional: no brochures, illustrations or product sheets
- [ ] Confirm time, place or video link the day before

---

## The 30 minutes

| Time | Section | What to do | Sample questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | **Welcome and purpose** | Thank them. Say what the meeting is for and that it will take 30 minutes. Say clearly: "This isn't about buying anything." Ask if there's anything they specifically want to cover | "Before we start, is there anything on your mind you'd like to make sure we talk about?" |
| 3–12 | **Life update** (the heart of the meeting) | Let them talk. Listen for changes: family, health, work, home, caregiving, plans | "What's changed for you this year?" · "How's everyone at home?" · "Anything big coming up next year, like a move, a new job or retirement?" · "Are you looking after anyone new, like parents or grandchildren?" |
| 12–17 | **Fact-find refresh** (generic) | Update the facts on file. Record only what's needed and what they're willing to share | See [checklist A](#a-fact-find-refresh) |
| 17–24 | **Nominations, will and LPA check-in** | Ask, don't advise on legal matters. Point to official sources and professionals | See [checklist B](#b-nominations-will-and-lpa-check-in) |
| 24–27 | **Contact details and beneficiaries** | Confirm how to reach them, their emergency contact, and that their family knows how to reach you | See [checklist C](#c-contact-details-and-beneficiaries) |
| 27–30 | **Close** | Summarise what you heard. Agree follow-ups (who does what, by when). Ask how they'd like to hear from you this year. Thank them | "Here's what I've noted… Did I get that right?" · "How would you like me to keep in touch: WhatsApp, email, a call?" |

**If a need comes up** ("we're thinking about a second child", "my dad's health is declining"):
> "That's important. Let's not rush it today. Would you like to set up a separate session where we look at it properly?"

Then book it as a separate advice meeting. Don't discuss products, premiums or recommendations in the review.

## Checklists

### A. Fact-find refresh
- [ ] Marital status / partner
- [ ] Dependants (children, parents, others they support), with ages if shared
- [ ] Occupation and employment changes (new job, self-employed, retired)
- [ ] Health changes **they choose to share**. Don't probe, and don't record more than is needed
- [ ] Home situation (moved, bought, sold)
- [ ] Major plans for the next 1–3 years
- [ ] Changes in how they feel about risk or money (in their words)
- [ ] Any existing arrangements they've made elsewhere since last year (for the record only; no comparison or criticism)

### B. Nominations, will and LPA check-in
Ask whether these exist and whether they still reflect the client's wishes. **Don't draft, interpret or give legal advice.** Refer the client to the official source or a lawyer.

- [ ] **Insurance nominations**: have life events (marriage, divorce, birth, death) changed who they'd want to benefit? Remind them that nominations can be made or changed through the insurer's process. The rules are in the Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009, as amended by the 2023 Amendment Regulations (in force 2 Jan 2024) ([MAS, amendment PDF](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas/regulations-and-financial-stability/regulations-guidance-and-licensing/insurance/regulations-guidance-and-licensing/subsidiary-legislation/insurance-nob-regs-published-10-july-2023.pdf))
- [ ] **CPF nomination**: CPF savings are not covered by a will; they are distributed by CPF nomination ([CPF Board, Making a CPF nomination](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/account-services/providing-for-your-loved-ones/making-a-cpf-nomination))
- [ ] **Will**: do they have one, and is it up to date after life changes? Without one, intestacy rules apply ([MoneySense, Intestacy](https://www.moneysense.gov.sg/intestacy-what-if-you-don-t-have-an-estate-plan/) · [MoneySense, Will](https://www.moneysense.gov.sg/legacy-planning/will/)). Suggest a lawyer; don't recommend a specific one for a fee or referral
- [ ] **Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA)**: have they made one? Without one, family may have to apply to court for a deputy ([MSF / Office of the Public Guardian, LPA](https://www.msf.gov.sg/what-we-do/opg/lasting-power-of-attorney/) · [Deputyship](https://www.msf.gov.sg/what-we-do/opg/deputyship))
- [ ] **Where documents are kept**: does a trusted family member know?

See [`../compliance/sg-fact-pack-2026-11.md`](../compliance/sg-fact-pack-2026-11.md) §3 for verified facts and "safe ways to say it".

### C. Contact details and beneficiaries
- [ ] Mobile, email, mailing address: still correct?
- [ ] Preferred channel and best time to reach them
- [ ] Emergency contact: still correct, and has that person agreed to be listed? (They should know.)
- [ ] Does a family member have the adviser's contact, in case something happens to the client?
- [ ] Beneficiaries / nominees (from B): any change they want to start? Give them the insurer's process; don't complete it on their behalf over chat
- [ ] Festivals and greeting preferences (update the `festivals` and `contact_pref` columns)
- [ ] Consent: are they still happy to receive birthday and festive greetings?

## After the meeting (within 2 working days)

- [ ] Send M40 (thank you and agreed follow-ups)
- [ ] Write the review record in the approved system (see below)
- [ ] Update the tracker: `last_review`, `next_review`, `next_action`, `life_events`
- [ ] Do what you promised, by when you promised it
- [ ] If a separate advice meeting was requested, book it and start the full advisory process there

## Fair dealing and documenting advice

### Fair dealing
MAS expects financial institutions and their representatives to deliver five fair dealing outcomes ([MAS Guidelines on Fair Dealing, rev. 30 May 2024, PDF](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/fair-dealing-guidelines-30-may-2024.pdf)):

1. Customers have confidence that they deal with financial institutions where fair dealing is central to the corporate culture.
2. Financial institutions offer products and services that are suitable for their target customer segments.
3. Customers are served by competent representatives.
4. Customers receive clear, relevant and timely information that accurately represents the products and services offered and delivered.
5. Financial institutions handle customer complaints in an independent, effective and prompt manner.

**What this means for the review:**
- **Outcome 1 (culture):** the review is offered because it's good for the client, not to hit a sales target. Say so, and mean it.
- **Outcome 3 (competence):** if a question is outside your competence (legal, tax, medical), say so and refer.
- **Outcome 4 (information):** don't paraphrase policy terms from memory. Point to the documents or confirm with the insurer, then answer in writing.
- **Outcome 5 (complaints):** if the client raises a concern or complaint, record it and escalate it through the insurer's complaints process the same day. Don't try to talk them out of it. Tell them that if they're not satisfied with the outcome, they can take an eligible dispute to FIDReC ([fidrec.com.sg](https://www.fidrec.com.sg/)).

### Documenting advice
- A **care review** (this agenda) is not a recommendation. Record: date, attendees, what was discussed, facts updated, follow-ups agreed, and **"no recommendation made"**.
- If any **recommendation** follows (in a separate meeting), it must go through the full process: know-your-client, needs analysis, a reasonable basis for the recommendation, and written documentation and record-keeping, as set out in MAS Notice FAA-N16, *Recommendations on Investment Products* ([MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/notices/notice-faa-n16), last revised 29 Dec 2025), and in your insurer's own procedures. MoneySense explains what clients should expect from the advisory process ([MoneySense, Financial advisory process](https://www.moneysense.gov.sg/financial-advisory-process/)).
- If the client **declines** to share information, or decides against advice, record that in their words.
- Write the record on the same day where possible. Notes should be factual, neutral and something you'd be comfortable for the client to read.
- Store records only in the approved system, and keep them only as long as needed for legal or business purposes (PDPA retention limitation, [PDPC, PDPA overview](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/the-legislation/personal-data-protection-act)).

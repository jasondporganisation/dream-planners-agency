# 90-Day Onboarding Plan (Recruitment → Licensing → Training Pack)

**For:** recruits joining a 2027 intake, their mentor, and Heron
**How this fits:** this plan wraps the existing training pack [`training/new-adviser-90-days/`](../../training/new-adviser-90-days/README.md). The training pack covers *what to learn*; this plan adds the **recruitment-side steps** (offer, licensing, appointment, retention check-ins) and shows which training module runs each week. Dates below match the **January 2027 intake** (Day 1 = Mon 4 Jan 2027, Day 90 = Sat 3 Apr 2027). For later intakes, keep the week numbers and shift the dates.
**Coach checklist:** [`coach-checklist.md`](../../training/new-adviser-90-days/coach-checklist.md) at Day 7, 30, 60, 90.

> **The licensing line:** no recruit advises, recommends or quotes to anyone until (1) they've passed the CMFAS modules their role needs and (2) they've been appointed by the principal and appear on the MAS register. [S5][S6][S11] Every client-facing activity below is marked **(after appointment)**.

## Licensing path at a glance

| Step | What | Typical timing (estimate) | Ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Confirm eligibility: 21+ at appointment, education minimum | Before offer | [S5] |
| L2 | Book and pass **RES5** (Rules, Ethics and Skills; 150 MCQ, 3 h; Part I 75%, Part II 80%) | Weeks 0–3 | [S12] |
| L3 | Book and pass **M9** (life insurance and investment-linked policies; 100 MCQ, 2 h, 70%) | Weeks 1–4 | [S13] |
| L4 | Book and pass **M9A** (ILP; 50 MCQ, 1 h, 70%) | Weeks 1–4 | [S14] |
| L5 | Book and pass **HI** (health insurance; 50 MCQ, 1 h 15 min, 70%) if advising on health insurance | Weeks 2–5 | [S15] |
| L6 | Principal's fit and proper checks and onboarding; principal lodges notification with MAS | After exams | [S16][S17] |
| L7 | Name appears on the MAS Register of Representatives; supervised client work may start | Around weeks 4–8 | [S6] |
| L8 | Ongoing: at least 30 h structured CPD a year (6 h ethics/rules/CPF) | From appointment | [S11] |

- **Estimate:** 4–8 weeks from offer to appointment for a focused full-time recruit. Part-time recruits take longer. (Research file, section 4.)
- Exams run daily on weekdays at the Singapore College of Insurance; results are given immediately (SCI registration policies; research file, section 4). M8/M8A are needed only if the principal requires unit-trust licensing (research file, section 4).
- **Budget for resits.** RES5 Part II (80%) is the usual stumbling block. Planning estimate S$700–900 per recruit including resits. Fees and reimbursement terms: see `06-honest-career-preview.md` and the principal's written scheme.
- A RES5 pass lapses if the person doesn't start advising within 3 years. [S5]

## Week 0: Before Day 1 (from offer to 3 Jan 2027)

- [ ] Recruit receives and signs as read: honest career preview, principal's **written** terms (exam reimbursement, any allowance, clawback). No verbal promises. [S10]
- [ ] Heron confirms supervision capacity for the cohort. [S8]
- [ ] Recruit books RES5 (and starts study); mentor assigned and named in writing.
- [ ] Recruit completes principal's pre-contract forms and fit and proper declaration. [S16]
- [ ] Tracker stage moves to **Offer accepted / Pre-licensing**.

## Retention and quality measures (record in the pipeline tracker)

- Day 30, 60, 90 check completed (Y/N), and who led it.
- Exams passed and date appointed.
- Quality: % of files passing mentor review first time (after appointment).
- Wellbeing self-rating 1–5 at each check.
- Still active at Day 90 and Month 13 (Y/N). There is no published Singapore benchmark; set your own baseline from the first cohort. [Research file, section 6]

**We never set or review income targets in these checks** (same rule as the coach checklist).

## If a recruit decides to leave
- Hold a calm exit conversation; thank them. No pressure to stay.
- Explain any clawback exactly as written in their signed terms; nothing extra.
- Record the reason category (fit, income, exams, personal, other) for the cohort review.

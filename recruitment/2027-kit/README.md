## Rules every file in this kit follows

1. **No income promises.** No earnings figures, no "up to $X", no "unlimited income", "uncapped earnings", "be your own boss and earn more", "financial freedom", or "replace your salary". We describe **what we provide** (training, mentoring, exam support, structure), not what a recruit will earn. [S1][S2][S3]
2. **No company pay figures.** This kit names no allowance, commission rate or bonus. Those come only from the principal's approved, current terms.
3. **State the hard parts.** Commission-based income is variable, the first year is a learning year, and some people leave. We say so plainly.
4. **Fair hiring.** No age bands, race, religion, gender, marital status or "young and dynamic" style wording. The only age reference is the **legal minimum of 21 to be appointed** under MAS Notice FAA-N26, stated as a regulatory requirement. [S4][S5]
5. **No advice before appointment.** Candidates, interns and recruits do not advise anyone until they appear on the MAS Register of Representatives. [S6][S7]
6. **Size intake to supervision.** Plan cohorts the team can actually supervise (research suggests 2–4 per supervisor, an estimate). [S8]
7. **Interviews include a person without recruitment targets**, per LIA Good Recruitment Practices. [S9]
8. **Every figure has an official link** in `sources.md`. Re-check each figure in the week before use.

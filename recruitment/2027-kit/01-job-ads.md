## Wording rules for all five ads

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Describe what we provide: structured training, named mentor, exam support (once confirmed), a 90-day plan | Any earnings figure, "up to $X", "6-figure", "uncapped / unlimited income", "financial freedom", "retire early", "be your own boss" |
| Say the income is **commission-based and variable** | "Guaranteed", "easy", "passive income", "no experience? earn big" |
| Say the first year is a **learning year** | Imply success is typical or fast |
| State the **regulatory** minimums (age 21 at appointment, education per MAS FAA-N26) [S5] | Any other age wording ("young", "fresh blood", "below 35"), race, religion, gender, marital status, nationality preferences beyond legal requirements [S4][S18] |
| Say no one advises clients before appointment and registration [S6] | Imply candidates can start selling right away |
| Invite to a free, no-obligation career conversation | Charge for a career talk, pressure with "limited slots" |

Exam support, allowances and clawback terms are **not** described in these ads. Add them only after the principal confirms the current scheme in writing, and only as approved wording. [S10]

## Ad 1: LinkedIn (post + job listing)

**Title:** Financial Representative (2027 intake), Dream Planners

**Body:**

Some people want a career where they help families make better money decisions, and are willing to put in a serious first year to learn how. If that's you, we'd like to talk.

**Dream Planners** is a small team of financial representatives with [Principal insurer] in Singapore. We take a few people per intake so each one gets real coaching.

**What the role is**
- Learn needs-based financial planning: protection, health, savings and retirement
- Meet clients, understand their situation, and recommend only what fits, with full disclosure
- Build your own client base over time, mostly through people you know and referrals

**What we provide**
- A structured 90-day onboarding plan, week by week
- A named mentor and weekly one-to-one coaching
- Study plan and practice support for the CMFAS exams (RES5, M9, M9A, HI)
- Role-play practice before you ever meet a client
- Check-ins at Day 7, 30, 60 and 90 that look at skills and wellbeing, not income targets

**What to know before you apply**
- Income is **commission-based and varies** from person to person and month to month. We don't publish earnings examples.
- The first year is mostly learning and building. Many people find it harder than they expected.
- You'll need to pass the CMFAS exams and be appointed before advising anyone.

**Requirements**
- Meet MAS minimum requirements to be appointed: at least 21 years old at appointment, and A Level (3 H2 + 2 H1 passes), IB Diploma, polytechnic diploma, degree or equivalent [S5][S11]
- Meet MAS fit and proper criteria (honesty, integrity, financial soundness) [S16]
- Comfortable starting conversations, and patient with people

**Next step:** Book a free, no-obligation 20-minute career conversation: [Apply link]. We'll also send you our one-page honest career preview before we meet.

## Ad 5: Part-time to full-time

**Headline:** Explore financial planning part-time first, then decide.

> **Check before posting:** confirm with the principal whether part-time representatives are permitted, what minimum commitment applies, and any conflict-of-interest rules for people with another job (e.g. employer approval for outside work). Remove this ad if part-time appointment is not allowed.

**How it works**
1. **Explore (part-time, weeks 1–8 approx.):** evening/weekend study for the CMFAS modules, two evening training sessions a week, and role-play practice. You keep your current job. You do not advise anyone during this stage.
2. **Decide (around week 8):** a structured review with your mentor and someone without recruitment targets. [S9] You decide whether to continue; no pressure and no penalty for stopping (subject to any written exam-sponsorship terms you agreed to).
3. **Commit (full-time):** appointment by the principal, then the full 90-day onboarding plan.

**Be realistic:** studying while working full-time is tiring. Commission-based income is variable, and part-time representatives usually build a client base more slowly. We won't tell you otherwise.

**Requirements:** MAS FAA-N26 minimums [S5]; fit and proper [S16]; your current employer's rules on outside work.

**Next step:** Free 20-minute career conversation: [Apply link]
